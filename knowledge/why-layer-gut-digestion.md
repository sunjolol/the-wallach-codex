# WHY-layer: Gut & Digestion (Hypochlorhydria, Enzymes, Leaky Gut, Candida)

Eighth entry in the WHY-layer build. The user-facing goal is digestive function — efficient absorption, no bloat/burp/belch, intact gut lining, controlled microbiome. Wallach's gut framework is centered on **stomach acid restoration**, not probiotic balance. He treats hypochlorhydria (low stomach acid) as the master upstream variable: when HCl is adequate, B12 absorbs, protein digests cleanly, pathogenic bacteria/yeast die in the acid bath, intrinsic factor functions, mineral chelation works. When HCl drops, every downstream organ system suffers. This note works through his explicit protocols then maps back to products.

**Headline up front (three parts):**

1. **Wallach's gut master variable is stomach acid, not probiotics.** Achlorhydria — the loss of stomach acid — is described as *"perhaps the most significant aging phenomenon"* with 75% of people over 50 requiring supplementation (LPD page 93). The downstream cascade: low HCl → undigested protein → intestinal bacteria + Candida migrate up into the stomach → fermentation of carbs → burp/belch/bloat → B12, Ca, protein deficiency → new food allergies from absorbed peptide fragments. **The framework lever is betaine HCl 75-250 mg before each meal, not Lactobacillus.** Modern functional medicine starts with probiotics; Wallach starts with HCl restoration.

2. **Pancreatic enzymes are the second pillar, taken with or between meals depending on use.** Wallach pairs betaine HCl with pancreatic enzymes (protease + lipase + amylase). With meals: protein digestion support. Between meals: systemic inflammation reduction (the enzymes circulate and break down inflammatory peptides). For acute injury he uses enzymes between meals specifically — the cartilage repair pathway. **The user already takes Ultimate Enzymes 1-2 per meal; this is the right pattern. The between-meals path is the upgrade for systemic inflammation or acute injury context.**

3. **Candida is real but downstream — it lives where the HCl doesn't reach.** Wallach describes Candida as an opportunist that colonizes the upper GI when achlorhydria removes the acid bath that normally kills it. Anti-fungal protocols (caprylic acid, oregano, etc.) treat the symptom; HCl restoration treats the upstream cause. **Framework call: address HCl first; revisit Candida-specific protocols only if symptoms persist after 4-8 weeks of HCl restoration.**

## The WHY (Tier 1 — Wallach corpus)

### Achlorhydria — the master protocol (LPD page 93)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "ACHLORHYDRIA (loss of stomach acid): contrary to belief stress will result in loss of stomach acid production. Achlorhydria is also a natural process of aging (perhaps the most significant aging phenomenon) so much so that 75% of people over the age of 50 years require supplementation. Symptoms include 'burp/belch and bloat' because the absence of acid in the stomach allows intestinal bacteria and yeast (Candida albicans) to enter the stomach and ferment high carbohydrate foods (i.e. juice, fruit, breads, etc.). Failure to deal with achlorhydria will result in B-12, calcium and protein deficiencies and the onset of new food allergies as a result of absorption of partially digested polypeptides..."

> "Treatment includes supplementation of betaine HCl at the rate of 75-250 mg 15-20 minutes before each meal. In the absence of betaine HCl 1-2 oz. of vinegar with English bitters (Gentian) may be taken before meals."

**Key elements:**
- Stress causes HCl loss (not increase, as mainstream often claims)
- 75% of people over 50 need HCl supplementation
- Symptom cluster: burp + belch + bloat
- Downstream cascade: B12 + Ca + protein deficiency + new food allergies
- Dose: **75-250 mg betaine HCl 15-20 min before meals**
- Fallback: 1-2 oz vinegar + Gentian bitters

### Ulcer — protocol (LPD page 185)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "ULCER (stomach): is usually brought on by decompensation from chronic stress (your adrenals give out). Prevention of ulcers should include distress systems (i.e., hobby, projects)..."

Wallach frames stomach ulcer as an **adrenal exhaustion** condition, not an excess-acid condition. Mainstream H. pylori framing is acknowledged separately but not the primary lens. The treatment direction is stress mitigation + adrenal support, NOT acid suppression. **Framework-conflict-direct with PPI/H2 blocker mainstream protocols.**

### B12 absorption pathway — why HCl matters upstream of cognition

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

From the cognition WHY-layer cross-reference: B12 absorption requires intrinsic factor + adequate stomach acid. Low HCl → low B12 absorption → demyelination + cognitive decline. This is the **gut → cognition** bridge.

> "Vitamin B12/cobalt joins with folic acid, choline and the amino acid methionine to transfer single carbon groups (methyl groups)... Growth, myelin formation (converts cholesterol to the insulating material myelin found around nerves in the brain and large nerve trunks) and RBC synthesis are dependant on B12."

The gut→cognition pathway is one of the strongest reasons HCl restoration matters beyond digestion itself.

### Food allergies — the leaky gut pathway

**Wallach-mechanism-extension (Moderate confidence):**

Wallach's framing: undigested polypeptide fragments crossing the gut barrier → immune reaction → new food allergies. This is essentially what modern functional medicine calls "leaky gut" (intestinal permeability) but Wallach roots the cause specifically in **inadequate HCl + protease**, not in zonulin/tight-junction biology that modern science describes.

The corpus solution: HCl + pancreatic enzymes before meals. The Coca pulse test for identifying offending foods. Rotation diet to give the immune system time to reset.

### Candida — opportunist downstream of achlorhydria

**Wallach-mechanism-extension (Moderate confidence):**

Wallach treats Candida as a downstream consequence of low HCl. The acid bath of the stomach normally kills yeast that arrives via food; when HCl is gone, Candida colonizes upward into the stomach and feeds on fermentable carbs. **Restore HCl, starve Candida.** Anti-fungal protocols address the colonization but not the cause.

## Conflicts with mainstream

- **PPIs (omeprazole, esomeprazole) + H2 blockers for chronic use.** Wallach's framework is fundamentally opposed: these suppress acid, which is the opposite of what Wallach prescribes (acid restoration). Long-term PPI use is associated with B12 deficiency, magnesium deficiency, increased fracture risk in mainstream literature — confirming Wallach's framework prediction from the achlorhydria cascade. Acute short-term PPI for documented ulcer is not a framework violation; chronic PPI for reflux symptoms is.
- **Reflux = too much acid.** Wallach inverts this: reflux is often **too little acid** (low HCl → poor LES tone + delayed gastric emptying → contents back up). HCl supplementation often resolves reflux paradoxically.
- **Probiotic-first gut protocols.** Mainstream functional medicine starts with probiotics + prebiotics + L-glutamine. Wallach starts with HCl + enzymes. Order matters: probiotic colonization is less effective when HCl is missing because the acid bath is needed to control the upper-GI environment.
- **Eliminate animal protein for gut healing.** Some functional protocols recommend low-protein. Wallach's framework keeps animal protein but ensures HCl + enzymes are present to digest it cleanly.
