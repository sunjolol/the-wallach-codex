# WHY-layer: Cognition (Memory, Brain Function, Alzheimer's, Korsakoff's)

Fourth entry in the WHY-layer build per `COMPLEMENTARY-DATA-DOCTRINE.md` §6. Goal-based naming continued from prior notes. The user-facing goal is cognition; the corpus delivers explicit protocols for Alzheimer's disease, Korsakoff's syndrome, cerebrovascular disease, and chorea — all of which Wallach frames as cholesterol-and-myelin problems first, drug-and-supplement problems second. This note works through them individually then maps back to the user's actual products.

**Headline up front (three parts):**

1. **Wallach treats cognition as a cholesterol-and-myelin rebuilding problem.** His most-cited cognitive food prescription is "**Eat soft-scrambled (not fried) eggs every day and 72 oz. red meat each month to replace cholesterol needed to rebuild myelin**" (DDDL, immediately following his Alzheimer's protocol). Cholesterol is the raw material the brain converts into the insulating myelin sheath via B12/cobalt. Statins, low-fat dieting, and "anti-cholesterol" advice are direct enemies of the framework's cognitive layer. This is the food anchor — every other supplement claim sits on top of it.

2. **Wallach's actual Alzheimer's, Korsakoff's, and cerebrovascular protocols include prescription nootropic drugs** — Hydergine 6-12 mg/day, Vasopressin/Diapid 12-16 units nasal, Piracetam 1.6 g QID, Centrophenoxine/Lucidril 4.4-8.0 g/day — alongside the supplement stack. **None of these are in any YGY product.** Synaptiv's modern nootropic stack (Bacopa, Huperzine-A, Ginkgo, Lemon Balm, phosphatidylserine) is framework-ADJACENT in the same way Ocutiv's lutein/zeaxanthin/astaxanthin were adjacent for eyes — it fits a category Wallach implicitly addresses (modern memory enhancement) without being named in his books. Treat Synaptiv as a modern-paradigm adjunct, not as a Wallach-protocol substitute.

3. **The Wallach-aligned cognitive cluster is high-dose choline/lecithin + B-complex + minerals + EFAs**, NOT the lutein-style "smart nutrients" most cognitive products are built around. Specifically: lecithin 2,500 mg TID = 7.5 g/day (recurring across Alzheimer's, Korsakoff's, chorea); choline up to **9 g/day** in cerebrovascular protocol; Vit B-1 100-200 mg TID; Vit B12 (for myelin formation); Vit E 1,200-2,000 IU/day; selenium 500-1,000 mcg/day; EFAs 5 g TID; chromium/vanadium 50-200 mcg TID (for the blood-sugar-stabilizes-mood pathway). A user with cognitive concerns following Wallach's framework needs **lecithin granules at 7.5 g/day** (no YGY product carries this dose), eggs and animal fat in the diet, and the mineral baseline. The Synaptiv/Memory FX layer sits on top — it doesn't replace the food/lecithin/mineral spine.

## The WHY (Tier 1 — Wallach corpus)

### Alzheimer's Disease — full protocol (LPD pos 95, replicated DDDL Appendix B)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "ALZHEIMERS DISEASE: is the progressive loss of memory with difficulties with arousal and motor function presenting late in the disease. Avoid aluminum cook ware, aluminum containing deodorants, soft drinks and fruit juices in aluminum cans. Treatment should include germanium IM 5 ml of standard solution every other day for 24 days followed by oral germanium at 150/mg b.i.d.; hydergine 6-12 mg/day; piracetam/choline 1.6 grams q.i.d.; vasopressin (Diapid) at a rate of one whiff in each nostril q.i.d. which delivers a total daily dose of 12-16 units of U.S.P. Posterior Pituitary; centrophenoxine (Lucidril) 6.2-8.0 gram/day. Lecithin at 2,500 mg t.i.d. is very useful at all stages (phosphatydil choline is more efficient). Don't forget the baseline nutritional supplements (including colloidal minerals), betaine HCl and pancreatic enzymes 75-200 mg t.i.d., oral and IV H2O2; 200 mg vitamin B-1 t.i.d., vitamin E at 1200 iu daily and IV chelation will be of value."

DDDL replicates with two notable differences: **Vit E at 2,000 IU daily** (vs LPD's 1,200) and **selenium 500 mcg daily** added explicitly. DDDL also adds the food anchor:

> "**Eat soft-scrambled (not fried) eggs every day and 72 oz. red meat each month to replace cholesterol needed to rebuild myelin.**"

**Mechanism, distilled:** Wallach frames Alzheimer's as (1) aluminum-driven neural damage to be avoided/chelated, (2) myelin breakdown to be rebuilt with cholesterol-rich food + B12 + lecithin/choline, (3) acute brain support via the nootropic drugs (Hydergine/Piracetam/Vasopressin/Centrophenoxine), and (4) the baseline 90-essentials mineral floor. Not a single-nutrient problem.

### Korsakoff's Syndrome — full protocol (LPD pos 153)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "KORSAKOFF'S SYNDROME (recent memory loss, false Alzheimers Disease): is characterized by an inability to record new memory; the patient can perform detailed tasks learned before onset but cannot learn the simplest of new tasks... This type of 'amnesia' can result from a blow to the head or be the result of chronic alcoholism and vitamin B-1 deficiency."

> "Treatment of Korsakoff's syndrome includes chelation, hydrogen peroxide IV, vitamin B-1 at 100 mg t.i.d., **lecithin at 2,500 mg. t.i.d.**, avoid alcohol, avoid sugar, **chromium/vanadium at 50-200 mcg t.i.d.**, betaine HCl and pancreatic enzymes at 75-200 mg t.i.d. before meals; hydergine at 4.5-9 mg/day, vasopressin (Diapid) at 12-16 units per day as a nasal spray, centrophenoxine at 4.4-8.0 gm per day and piracetam (Dinagen) at 1.6-4.8 gm per day."

**Notable in this protocol:** Cr/V at the **same dose Wallach uses for diabetes**. Memory + blood sugar are linked in his framework — alcoholism damages both pancreas and brain, and the same Cr/V protocol shows up in both. Lecithin 7.5 g/day shows up identically to Alzheimer's. "Avoid sugar" is explicit (not a generic wellness instruction — sugar is named as Cr-depleting and brain-damaging).

### Cerebrovascular Disease — full protocol (LPD pos 113 + DDDL part 23)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "Treatment of cerebrovascular disease includes EDTA chelation therapy to help remove plaque, hydrogen peroxide IV to increase oxygen, vitamin C to bowel tolerance, avoid alcohol, vitamin E at 800-1,200 IU/day, essential fatty acids at 5 mg t.i.d., selenium at 1,000 mcg/day, centrophenoxine at 1,000-2,000 mg/day, hydergine at 4.5-9.0 mg/day, vasopressin (DIAPID) at 12-16 units/day, Lucidril at 4.4-8.0 gm/day, piracetam at 1.6 gm t.i.d. and **choline at 9 gm/day** concurrently, and **lecithin at 4 gm b.i.d.** [= 8 g/day]."

**Critical dose escalation:** Cerebrovascular has the HIGHEST choline (9 g/day) and lecithin (8 g/day) doses across Wallach's cognitive protocols — because vascular brain insult demands maximum myelin/membrane repair material. Modern citicoline supplementation at 500-1,000 mg/day is ~1/10 to 1/20 of this target.

### B12 / Cobalt → Myelin — mechanism anchor (Rare Earths pos 328, DDDL)

**Directly supported (High confidence) — Rare Earths Co entry:**

> "Growth, myelin formation (converts cholesterol into the insulating material myelin found around nerves in the brain, spinal cord, and large nerve trunks), and red blood cell synthesis are dependent on B12. Cobalt is also required as a necessary cofactor for the production of thyroid hormone."

> "Pernicious anemia and **demyelination of the spinal cord and large nerve trunks are classic for B12/cobalt deficiency**."

> "The RDA for B12/cobalt is 3 to 4 mcg per day, we prefer expensive urine and like **250 to 400 mcg per day**, especially while preparing for a pregnancy and nursing."

**Mechanism:** B12 is the enzymatic cofactor that converts dietary cholesterol into the myelin insulating sheath. Without B12, the cholesterol you eat doesn't reach myelin → demyelination → progressive neurological decline. **This is why the eggs-and-red-meat food anchor and B12 supplementation are two ends of the same mechanism** — you need both the raw material (cholesterol) AND the cofactor (B12) to rebuild nerves. Wallach prefers 250-400 mcg/day vs RDA 3-4 mcg (~80x higher).

### Chorea — uncontrolled movement protocol (LPD pos 114)

Useful as a "calibration" condition: chorea shows up the choline/lecithin/B-complex cluster at slightly lower doses.

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "We recommend the base line supplement program, essential fatty acids at 5 gm t.i.d., B-complex at 100 mg b.i.d. each, vitamin E at 800-1,200 IU/day, selenium at 500 mg/day [likely 500 mcg — see open question], **lecithin at 2,500 mg t.i.d. and choline at 500 mg/day**. Pulse testing for food allergies and hair analysis to monitor absorption is essential."

The recurring core is visible: **EFAs 5 g TID + B-complex high-dose + Vit E + Se + lecithin 7.5 g/day + choline** = Wallach's neurological-condition cluster, modulated up or down depending on severity and specific diagnosis.

### Aluminum avoidance — environmental anchor (LPD Alzheimer's section)

> "Avoid aluminum cook ware, aluminum containing deodorants, soft drinks and fruit juices in aluminum cans."

Specific to Alzheimer's. Wallach names aluminum as causally implicated and asks for both dietary and topical (deodorants) avoidance. This is part of the framework even when no acute cognitive concern is present — it's a baseline lifestyle constraint.

### Hypothyroid → Cognitive Symptoms — adjacent mechanism (LPD pos 146)

**Directly supported (High confidence):**

> "HYPOTHYROID (low thyroid, goiter) states frequently do not produce goiter (thyroid gland enlargement), but rather produce obesity, fatigue, disinterest, low blood pressure, water retention (edema)..."

Wallach names "**fatigue and disinterest**" as hypothyroid symptoms — both cognitive in presentation. The hypothyroid-iodine link extends the cognition framework: iodine deficiency → low thyroid → cognitive fog. **Iodine fits the cognition WHY-layer as an indirect input** (via thyroid hormone synthesis) even though the direct "iodine → brain" wiring isn't named.

## Takeaways for the agent

1. **Lead with the food anchor.** When a user asks about cognition products, the first sentence should reference eggs/red meat/cholesterol/myelin. Wallach's framework starts with food, not supplements. Skipping this conflicts with the corpus directly.

2. **Surface the prescription-drug gap proactively.** Wallach's actual Alzheimer's/Korsakoff's/cerebrovascular protocols include Hydergine, Diapid, Piracetam, Centrophenoxine. The user needs to know these exist as part of his stated protocol even if they're not accessible via YGY — flag and let them decide. Don't pretend Synaptiv replaces them.

3. **Don't let Synaptiv's pro-line label overstate its Wallach alignment.** Doctrine §3 says pro-line products are "optimal formulation hypothesis (HIGH PRIOR, NOT proof)." For cognition specifically, Synaptiv hypothesizes that Bacopa+Huperzine+Lemon balm+phosphatidylserine is optimal — but that's the modern nootropic optimum, not the Wallach optimum. Both are legitimate frames; conflating them is the failure mode.

4. **Cite the lecithin gap.** No YGY product carries clinical-dose lecithin. This is a real gap that affects cognition planning. If a user is serious about Wallach's cognitive protocol, third-party lecithin granules (~7.5 g/day) is non-negotiable.

5. **Bio-Alert is the choline anchor that no one talks about.** Biometics Bio-Alert at 1,000 mg choline per tablespoon is by far the strongest single-source choline in the catalog. It rarely shows up in cognition-product discussions because it's under the Biometics line, not the pro-line/Ultimate line. Surface it.

6. **B12 at Wallach's 250-400 mcg target is achievable via foundational multis.** BTT 2.5 (500 mcg methylcobalamin) or Ultimate Iodine Spray (24 mcg + bonus from other sources). The mineral spine for cognition is more accessible than the lecithin/choline layer.

7. **Hypothyroid → cognitive symptoms is the silent cognitive issue.** Fatigue and "disinterest" can present as cognitive concerns when the root is iodine/thyroid. Iodine supplementation (Ultimate Iodine Spray 300 mcg) is part of cognitive framework via the thyroid axis. If a user complains of brain fog + low energy, surface the iodine/thyroid check before going deep on nootropics.

8. **The Cr/V cognitive link is real and underused.** Korsakoff's protocol has Cr/V at diabetes doses. Blood sugar destabilization → brain function decline (hypoglycemia presents with confusion, irritability, difficulty concentrating). For a user with mood/cognitive concerns AND sugar in the diet, Sweet Eze or Glucogenix is cognitively relevant — not just a blood-sugar product.

9. **For "which product for cognition" specifically: lead with the product name in the first sentence per the lessons.md rule.** The answer is either: "Synaptiv (modern paradigm) or Memory FX (closer to Wallach base) — both as adjuncts to food + lecithin + minerals" — not a wall of text starting with framework discussion.

10. **Surface the Wallach-style high-stakes carve-out where it applies.** Per brain v2.4, a serious + common condition warrants visual prominence. Aluminum avoidance for active Alzheimer's concern, or B12 deficiency presenting as demyelination — both qualify if the user is in those contexts.
