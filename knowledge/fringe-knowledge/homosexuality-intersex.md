## The core thesis

Wallach places homosexuality, hermaphroditism, and intersex conditions in a single category of
**preventable congenital "defects" caused by mineral/vitamin deficiency of the embryo in early
pregnancy** — preventable, he argues, for "a few pennies per pregnancy" with complete
preconception nutrition.

> "Children without a conscience (sociopaths), violent behavior, dyslexia, retardation, fetal alcohol or drug syndrome, ADD and homosexuality are all preventable for a few pennies per pregnancy but expensive and difficult to deal with after the fact."

> "A series of preventable embryologic events frequently result in physical and/or emotional and behavioral aberrations. These congenital defects can result in individuals who are neither male nor female or who could be both male and female at once, forming five commonly accepted classifications."

His framing premise on sex:

> "There is now a kaleidoscope of sexual behaviors, choices and displays; however, none of the members of this ever growing subculture can refute the fact that there are only two sexes - male and female."

## How the thinking shifted (Wallach's account): learned behavior → choice → prenatal defect

> "The current focus on the 'gay' phenomena has moved from that of a learned behavior or a 'lifestyle choice' to that of a prenatal biological event - in other words a preventable congenital defect caused by a deficiency of minerals and or vitamins in early pregnancy. This 180 degree change in thinking resulted from the finding of a common physical defect in the brains of gay men."

He dates the brain-difference idea to German doctors in the 1970s who believed an area of the
**preoptic area of the hypothalamus** in gay men was abnormal and tried (unsuccessfully) to
"normalize" it surgically.

## The soil-depletion link

> "The gradual increase in the percentage of gays in America and other industrialized nations of the world parallels the gradual decrease in the mineral content in our farm and range soils and therefore our food supply."

## Intersex / hermaphroditism (the taxonomy he lays out)

> "In firstly talking about congenital defects, 'intersex' is the medical term used to describe the term hermaphrodite. A female hermaphrodite might possess one testicle and one ovary, a male pseudo hermaphrodite could have testes and some recognizable female genitalia but lack ovaries, and a female pseudo hermaphrodite may have ovaries and some noticeable features of male genitalia but no testicles."

- **True hermaphrodite** — testicles and ovaries develop separately (or fuse into an "ovo-testes");
  often at least one gonad functions, producing sperm or ova plus the matching hormones.
- **Male pseudohermaphrodite** — testicles + XY chromosomes, yet also a vagina and clitoris;
  develops breasts at puberty; does not cycle or menstruate.
- **Female pseudohermaphrodite** — ovaries + XX chromosomes (sometimes a uterus), plus some
  external male genital features.

**Authorities / cases he cites:**
- **John Money (Johns Hopkins)** — "a specialist in the field of congenital sexual organ defects
  (defects that are found at birth and related to mineral or vitamin deficiencies of the embryo)
  states that 'intersexuals can constitute as many as four percent of all births.'"
- **Christine Jorgensen** — her high-profile sexual conversion "opened the subject of intersexuals
  to the general public."
- **1972 Munich Olympics** — all female athletes given a chromosome (hair) test; "female" athletes
  with the male chromosome pattern were disqualified; the Russian "sisters" Tamara Press
  (shotput) and Irina Press (pentathlon) retired when the test became mandatory.
- **Paul Guinet (Lyons) & Jacques Decourt (Paris), 1969** — "listed 98 different types of true
  hermaphrodites, based on the appearances of external genitalia and associated tubes and ducts."
- **Hugh H. Young (Johns Hopkins urologist), 1937** — "Genital Abnormalities, Hermaphroditism and
  Related Adrenal Diseases"; cites the case of **Emma**, a "female" with a penis-sized clitoris.
- **Christopher J. Dewhurst & Ronald R. Gordon, 1967** — "The Intersexual Disorders."
- **Etymology** — "hermaphrodite" from the Greek Hermes + Aphrodite (Greek myth of a male
  hermaphrodite "born over 2,000 years ago").
- **Plato** — "the only recognised authority to have surmised that there were 'three sexes (male,
  female and hermaphrodite).'" Wallach recounts Plato's myth of humans originally joined in pairs
  (two men, two women, a man/woman pair) split by Zeus, "thus Plato gave the first interpretation
  of the origin of homosexual behavior."
- Historical penalty: in the 1600s a Scottish hermaphrodite living as a woman "was buried alive as
  a witch."

He stresses the medical-correction programs (hormonal/surgical) are "clearly non-judgemental and
humanitarian and are to be applauded," but argues:

> "the social and surgical problems of intersexuals could be totally avoided by proper preconception nutrition of the embryo."

## Animal parallels

- **Marsupials** (kangaroos, wallabies, mouse opossums) "normally have three vaginas"; the
  platypus is a mammal that lays eggs — used to argue unusual sexual anatomy is common in nature.
- **"Freemartin"** — "a term given to female animals born as fraternal twins of males (80% occur in
  cattle, 5% in sheep and goats); they are infertile and frequently have congenital defects of the
  genitalia." Wallach treats the freemartin as the animal model of a nutrition/embryo-competition
  intersex defect.
- "Every known congenital defect of the sexual organs of man has been recorded in many species of
  animals (frequently many different defects in the same litter if nutrients are not available)."

## The "not genetic, but congenital" argument (the twin / brain studies)

Prevalence figures he cites:
- **Alfred Kinsey (40s–50s)** — homosexuals 4–10% of the US population.
- **Richard Pillard (Boston University)** — 4% of US males, 2–3% of females; claims Great Britain
  "has possibly reached 25% in the 90's."

The genetic-vs-congenital logic (his key move):
> "[Pillard] joined forces with Michael J. Bailey... and they found that only 52% of the identical twins of gay men were gay themselves (proving that gay behavior is not genetic, for if it was genetic 100% of the identical gay twins would have to be gay!!); 22% of gay mens fraternal twin brothers and other biological brothers were gay, while less than 10% of adopted brothers were gay."

- Parallel study of 147 gay women with identical twin sisters: 48% concordant identical twins,
  16% fraternal, 6% adopted — "approximately the same results as in the identical twin study in
  gay men."
- **Dean Hamer** (geneticist, National Cancer Institute) quoted: "It is a mistake to think there is
  a gay gene."

Brain-anatomy evidence he marshals:
- **Simon LeVay**, 1990 — compared the medial preoptic hypothalamus (INAH3, "third interstitial
  nucleus of the anterior hypothalamus") of 19 homosexual men (all died of AIDS) vs 16 heterosexual
  men (6 died of AIDS); reports the region is smaller in gay men. Wallach notes the AIDS confound
  but says other cell collections were identical.
- The **anterior commissure** (nerve bundle joining the brain's hemispheres) — "smallest in the
  heterosexual man and of identical size in gay men and women."
- **Roger A. Gorski (UCLA), 1978** — preoptic hypothalamic nuclei in rats; a prenatal **testosterone
  surge** in the male fetus stabilizes these cell populations (in females the lack of the surge lets
  them atrophy); castrating adult males does not shrink the cells — i.e., the difference is set
  before birth.

His synthesis: because twin concordance is high but **not 100%**, the trait is **congenital
(developmental/nutritional)**, not strictly genetic —

> "Combined with the study of identical twins that failed to show a genetic pattern... the LeVay study is a sure indication that gay behavior is a congenital defect rather than a genetic defect or behavioral choice!!"

He reads the high-but-partial twin concordance through embryo-competition: twins (like rats with
14 embryos, or freemartin cattle) compete for limited nutrition, so multiple births carry more
congenital defects.

## The conclusion / prevention claim

> "It is an easy mental jump to perceive that one should be able to prevent the congenital hermaphrodite and the intersexual as well as the gay embryo with complete preconception nutrition with 90 nutrients including minerals (i.e.- zinc, manganese, magnesium, gallium, copper, etc.), vitamins (B-12, folic acid, vitamin A), amino acids and essential fatty acids. This can perhaps best be described as insurance against gay embryo's."
