Do not overcook eggs."** To cook: **Poach** (#1 choice — water never exceeds 212°F), **Scramble** (butter, very low heat), **Soft Boil** ("2-minute eggs"), **Raw** (fine but increase Biotin + take 90 For Life; clean shell). - **PORK / LAMB** — organic non-GMO fed.
