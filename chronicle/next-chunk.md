## ★ PHASE F — Youngevity Product DB (`eden/products/`). THE ACTIVE WORK.
**What it is:** the sealed COMPOSITION record of every Youngevity product — what's actually in each. Powers the Coverage math ("does my regimen hit my Wallach targets?"), cost-per-nutrient recommendations for ALL 91 essentials ("cheapest product for my selenium goal"), and blend/ingredient search. Replaces the transitional `eden/eden-catalog.json`.

**★ AUTHORITATIVE SCHEMA + DESIGN: `eden/products/README.md` — READ IT FIRST.** (It supersedes the blueprint §2.1 "Shape (proposed)" sketch, which was simpler than what we actually locked.) Summary:
- `products.json` — composition, keyed by `product_id`. Product = `{product_id, name, sku, ygy_id, components:[…]}`. Component = `{role?, form, serving_size, servings_per_container, directions?, extract_ratio?, macros{}, nutrients[], blends[], other_ingredients[], source_label}`. Multi-panel products (tablet+softgel) → multiple components. nutrient = `{name, form?, amount, unit, unit_detail?(RAE/NE/DFE), label_iu?, pct_dv, part?, latin?}`. blend = `{name, total{amount,unit}|null, pct_dv?, as_labeled, ingredients:[{pos, name, part?, form?, latin?, standardization?, amount?, sub_ingredients?}]}` — **array ORDER = label order = descending amount (FDA)**.
- `prices.json` — VOLATILE sidecar (retail/wholesale + YGY description), all 432, keyed by ygy_id, auto-generated from the saved .mhtml manifest. NOT sealed.
- `warnings.json` — VOLATILE sidecar, YGY label warnings, source:ygy, quarantined.
- Enforcement: `eden/tools/products_verify.py` (structure + PROSE-CONTAINMENT, negative-tested). §00.A: composition-only, NEVER a target; the ONLY long free-text token anywhere is a blend's bounded `as_labeled`; prices/desc/warnings live in the source:ygy sidecars, never a Wallach claim/target.

**★ DECISIONS LOCKED (do NOT re-litigate — Luneth signed off):** (1) prices/desc separated into volatile prices.json; (2) bounded `as_labeled` fidelity token per blend/ingredient; (3) EVERY ingredient catalogued (essentials→91 + botanicals/actives) into the rebuilt registry; (4) warnings → warnings.json sidecar; (5) commit every few batches (not per batch); (6) bundles/paks skipped (no label of their own); non-supplements (books/devices/patches) excluded; multi-panel single products → components[]. (7) **ONE RECORD PER FORMULA, not per sku** — a pack-size variant sharing an IDENTICAL formula → a `variants[]` entry (sku·ygy_id·name·form·source_label); a sku whose formula DIFFERS gets its OWN record; older/regional label images of the SAME sku → `additional_labels[]` (both fields documented in README). (8) amino profiles captured AS BLENDS (per-AA amounts where labeled). (9) a buy-together 2-product bundle that's the ONLY way to get either item (e.g. 3.0 Rise & Restore) → ONE record with TWO role-named components. (10) for same-name CLUSTERS, get Luneth's authoritative label→product map before finalizing — even careful formula-matching can mislabel WHICH product a label is (BTT/osteo/CAL/Collagen all needed his map).
