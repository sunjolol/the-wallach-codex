# ★ LESSONS THAT WILL COST A SESSION IF LOST

1. **MULTIPLE DEMOS ALWAYS — and mine the dossier's DISCARDS.** The winner this round was an angle
   the dossier described and then declined to build. Its "gaps/flags" section is a concept source,
   not a fence.
2. **A round shape can assemble a face out of parts.** An axial cross-section — two symmetric
   circles, a central ellipse, radiating strokes — rendered as a cartoon cat. Nothing in the figure
   was wrong individually. **If a figure has a symmetric pair of circles above a central mark, look
   at it before anything else.**
3. **A redesign inherits none of the old checks and can be wrong in a new way.** The replacement
   pinched the nerve symmetrically, drawing it pulling AWAY from the bone at the moment it is meant
   to be crushed against it. Re-derive the claim from scratch after any redraw.
4. **`elementFromPoint` takes VIEWPORT coordinates.** With the panel scrolled off-screen every hit
   test returned null and the probe reported 0u — including for the control. **A probe that reads
   zero for its own negative control is broken, not passing.** `scrollIntoView` first.
5. **Print each element's own numbers.** The clip sweep printed the description's dimensions beside
   a verdict about the rec element, turning one design fact into 20 fake defects.
6. **The tracker's display face is a TRIAL FONT that watermarks digits.** `vitamin b6` rendered with
   a "TRIAL FONT / 177studio.com" badge over the numeral. That is why existing copy spells out
   "vitamin B six". Spelling it out then wrapped to three lines and the name block clips at two —
   **use the element's word-name** (`pyridoxine`).
7. **A CSS grid locks every row to its tallest cell.** An 11-item group beside a 6-item group left a
   five-line hole, twice. `columns` + `break-inside: avoid` packs instead.
8. **An animated readout must not appear before it is true.** A "100 mg" label riding a sweeping
   needle read 100 while sitting over ~32 mg. Delay past the settle.
