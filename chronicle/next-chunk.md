# ★ LESSONS THAT WILL COST A SESSION IF LOST

1. **Human stakes beat mechanism.** See the block above. This is the big one.
2. **A caption can overclaim as easily as a number.** A B12 caption said the deficiency list had not
   been reordered while it had been sorted by severity — his is alphabetical. Quietly reordering a
   source's list and saying you had not is the same class of error as a wrong dose. Say whose order
   it is.
3. **`stroke-dasharray` must be ≥ the path length.** 300 on a 444-unit path made the pattern WRAP and
   paint a second segment at the far end — a figure showing the swallowed dose arriving, in a figure
   whose whole claim is that it does not.
4. **A round shape can assemble a face.** An axial wrist cross-section — two symmetric circles, a
   central ellipse, radiating strokes — rendered as a cartoon cat. If a figure has a symmetric pair
   of circles above a central mark, look at it before anything else.
5. **A redesign inherits none of the old checks.** The replacement nerve pinched symmetrically,
   drawing it pulling AWAY from the bone at the moment it is meant to be crushed against it.
6. **`elementFromPoint` takes VIEWPORT coordinates.** Scrolled off-screen, every hit test returns
   null and the probe reads zero — including for its own control. **A probe that reads zero for its
   negative control is broken, not passing.**
7. **Print each element's own numbers.** A clip sweep printed the description's dimensions beside a
   verdict about a different element, turning one design fact into 20 fake defects.
8. **The tracker's display face is a TRIAL FONT that watermarks digits.** `vitamin b6` rendered with
   a "TRIAL FONT / 177studio.com" badge over the numeral, and the spelled-out `vitamin b six` then
   wrapped to three lines against a two-line name block. **Use the element's word-name** —
   `pyridoxine`, `cobalamin`.
9. **A CSS grid locks every row to its tallest cell.** `columns` + `break-inside: avoid` packs.
10. **An animated readout must not appear before it is true.** A "100 mg" label riding a sweeping
    needle read 100 while sitting over ~32 mg.
11. **Where Wallach names a sign but not a number, draw no number.** B6's toxicity end is left open
    under the words "no dose given" rather than given an invented threshold.
