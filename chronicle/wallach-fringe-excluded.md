## The policy (agreed 2026-06-24)

1. **Editorial exclusion is for EXTREME cases only** — fringe, unfalsifiable,
   reputationally-risky, or off-mission (not a nutrient/health-coverage claim).
   The default is to extract faithfully; exclusion is the rare exception.
2. **Every exclusion is run by Luneth — EVERY TIME.** The agent never decides to
   exclude silently. Each candidate is surfaced for his call and recorded here.
3. **Excluded ≠ deleted.** Faithful to Wallach (§00.A holds): the claim + its
   verbatim + location are preserved here so Luneth has the complete picture for
   study. It simply does not enter `eden/corpus/` or any app surface.
4. **Format mirrors the corpus:** a plain-language summary, then Wallach's own
   words (verbatim) + book/line locator — so this catalog is as scannable as the
   corpus itself.

## Rare Earths: Forbidden Cures (1994)

### Ch.6 "The Verdict" — mineral deficiency → violent / criminal behavior

Wallach's most fringe thesis: that violent crime and serial murder are downstream
of mineral deficiency, illustrated with two killers. Excluded as fringe +
unfalsifiable + reputationally toxic for a health app; off-mission (criminology,
not nutrient coverage).

- **Ed Gein (cannibalism as mineral-deficiency pica).** Summary: Wallach claims
  Gein's depleted-farm diet caused severe mineral deficiencies that "probably
  produced his bizarre cravings (pica) for human flesh."
  > verbatim (≈ line 7946): "Geins' primary food source was grown on his played out farm - his minerally depleted food supply resulted in severe mineral deficiencies in him which probably produced his bizarre cravings (pica) for human flesh."

- **Waneta Hoyt (serial infanticide as mineral deficiency).** Summary: Wallach
  attributes Hoyt's diabetes to chromium/vanadium deficiency and her shift "from
  that of a 'good Christian farm wife' to that of a sociopath serial killer as a
  result of mineral deficiencies."
  > verbatim (≈ line 7967): "her behavior and judgment were changed from that of a \"good Christian farm wife\" to that of a sociopath serial killer as a result of mineral deficiencies."

- **General thesis.** Wallach frames "remineralizing our younger generation" as
  the answer to crime, vs jails/police/Prozac/psychiatrists (≈ lines 7977–7982).

### Ch.7 "Genetic Potential" — "emotional defects" list (homosexuality)

Summary: in a list of preventable birth/developmental "defects," Wallach catalogs
"homosexuality" alongside learning disabilities, antisocial behavior, and
hyperactivity as an "emotional defect." Excluded: dated + offensive social claim,
not a nutrient/health-coverage claim.

> verbatim (≈ line 12346): "10 % percent of our babies are born with emotional (i.e.- learning disabilities, homosexuality, antisocial behavior, hyperactivity, etc.) and or chromosomal and or physical neural tube defects ( i.e.-Down's Syndrome, spina bifida, anencephally, hydrocephalus, cerebral palsy,etc.)"

_(Note: the legitimate, in-scope half of this same passage — that physical neural-tube
birth defects are preventable via nutrition — WAS extracted to the corpus as
WAL-CLM-RARE-000015. Only the "emotional defects / homosexuality" framing is excluded.)_
