## 1. The stance (what the app must convey)

Wallach's health model is **TWO PRONGS, inseparable**:
1. **Get all 90 essential nutrients.** (What we have built so far — coverage, essentials, regimen, the whole app until now.)
2. **Remove the bad foods — above all GLUTEN (wheat, barley, rye, oats) — so the body can actually ABSORB the 90.**

Prong 2 is NOT optional garnish; it is HALF the model. Without it, prong 1 underperforms drastically:
- Gluten damages the intestinal villi → malabsorption → the 90 nutrients you take don't get absorbed → supplementing is far less effective.
- **"You are not what you eat — you are what you absorb."** (Wallach, Epigenetics — the mantra for this whole workstream.)
- Luneth's field knowledge (grounded in the same corpus): with absorption compromised you may need **2x–5x** the dose just to reach baseline — so the REAL play is not "megadose forever," it is to push the user HARD to **eliminate gluten + bad foods and make it a LIFESTYLE.**
- **Partial is not enough:** 90% gluten-free is a great start but NOT sufficient — full elimination is required, because residual gluten keeps leaky gut / malabsorption going.

The app must make this **inescapable and persuasive**, so a user cannot walk away thinking "90 essentials and I'm good." The correct message is "90 essentials AND bad foods OUT — together, as a lifestyle, or it doesn't fully work."
