# Nitrogen — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 5 sealed claims · mineral (dietary, coverage_kind=dietary) · target: none — no Wallach maintenance amount stated (honest gap) · solo-header: **marginal (workable, lead with the Kwashiorkor mechanism)**.

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

The whole pack is really about ONE thing: nitrogen reaches the body as **protein**, and Wallach's entire treatment is a protein story. Two books, one chapter each (rare-earths Ch11, epigenetics Ch18), no doses, no interactions. It groups cleanly into two angles.

**Angle A — what nitrogen/protein IS and DOES (the nine jobs).**
- `WAL-CLM-RARE-000182` (definition): nitrogen is "a structural atom in protein, nucleic acids (RNA, DNA) and a wide variety of organic molecules"; dietary N as protein "furnish[es] the amino acids for synthesis of tissue protein and other special metabolic functions."
- `WAL-CLM-RARE-000183` (mechanism, functions 1–3): (1) repair worn-out tissue — the anabolic process; (2) build new tissue — muscle, infant/childhood/teen growth, pregnancy; (3) an emergency source of heat and energy, "albeit more expensive in biological terms than fat or carbohydrate."
- `WAL-CLM-RARE-000184` (mechanism, functions 4–9): (4) essential secretions and fluids — "enzymes, hormones, mucus, milk, semen"; (5) blood-plasma proteins maintain osmotic fluid balance — "hypoproteinemia results in edema"; (6) acid-base balance of blood and tissues; (7) transport of minerals, fats, vitamins; (8) immunoglobulins (antibodies); (9) a nitrogen pool for synthesizing amino acids and new proteins.
- `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000101` (deficiency_sign): the same 9-function framing restated as prose, and the deficiency list — this is the epigenetics twin of the rare-earths material.

**Angle B — what happens when it runs short (Kwashiorkor).**
- `WAL-CLM-RARE-000185` (deficiency_sign): "Classic protein deficiency results in infertility, poor growth, lowered immune status, edema and Kwashiorkor (potbellied, thin children of third world countries)." deficiency_signs in the pack: `lowered_immunity`, `poor_growth` (both → RARE-000185, high confidence). conditions_treated: edema, infertility, kwashiorkor.
- `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000101` carries the identical list (infertility, poor growth, lowered immune status, edema, Kwashiorkor).
- The MECHANISM behind the potbelly is already in the pack — function (5) in RARE-000184: plasma proteins hold fluid in balance, so low protein → hypoproteinemia → edema. That is the one bridge that makes Angle B a real illustration and not just a symptom list.

Note on faithfulness: the claim_texts add plain-language glosses (e.g. edema = "fluid swelling — because blood-plasma proteins normally hold body fluid in balance") — every such gloss is grounded in RARE-000184 function 5, not invented. No numbers appear anywhere in this pack, so there is no claim_text/verbatim number mismatch to flag.

## 2. Header concepts (2 honest concepts — the material does not support 4)

### Concept 1 — "The potbelly paradox" (the Kwashiorkor mechanism) ★ recommended
- **The hook:** a starving child looks *swollen*, not shrunken — the belly is full of fluid, not food. Wallach's explanation: protein is what holds body fluid in place, so when protein runs out, the fluid leaks out into the tissues. One counterintuitive image that carries the whole "why nitrogen matters" argument.
- **Layout shape:** a single annotated figure with one short mechanism caption beneath it, then the deficiency roll (infertility · poor growth · lowered immunity · edema · Kwashiorkor) as a plain inline list — NOT a beats/quote chassis. The figure IS the header; the list is a footer.
- **Illustration (one idea):** two side-by-side vessel/silhouette states. Left: plasma proteins (dots) inside the blood vessel *holding* fluid in the bloodstream. Right: proteins gone, fluid seeping out into surrounding tissue → the swollen belly. Fewest elements: one vessel wall, dots that leave, fluid that crosses. Route every label OFF the arrows (no stroke through text — the #1 rejection cause). No many-station diagram.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-RARE-000184` function 5 ("Blood plasma proteins maintain osmotic fluid balance (hypoproteinemia results in edema)") + `WAL-CLM-RARE-000185` / `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000101` (the deficiency list incl. Kwashiorkor, "potbellied, thin children of third world countries").
- **Why it wows / best UX:** it's the one visually striking, emotionally sticky thing in the whole pack, and Wallach supplies the exact mechanism, so nothing has to be invented. Turns a dry "protein deficiency" list into a why-this-happens picture.

### Concept 2 — "Nine jobs of one atom" (the function ledger)
- **The hook:** you think of protein as muscle. Wallach lists **nine** jobs it does — and only one of them is muscle. Nitrogen is the atom behind enzymes, hormones, antibodies, milk, semen, fluid balance, transport, and the raw material for making more of itself.
- **Layout shape:** a clean numbered ledger (1–9) — a typographic list, not a diagram. Optionally cluster the nine into the natural groupings (build/repair · secretions · balance · defense · pool) but the honest shape here is just a well-set list; the material is a list.
- **Illustration (one idea):** minimal — a single nitrogen glyph (N) at the head feeding a simple 9-row ledger, OR no figure at all (a header can be an annotated list; see Rule 0). Do NOT draw 9 output stations radiating from an atom — that is the rejected many-station diagram.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-RARE-000183` (functions 1–3) + `WAL-CLM-RARE-000184` (functions 4–9) + `WAL-CLM-RARE-000182` (the definition: structural atom in protein, RNA, DNA).
- **Why it wows / best UX:** the "nine, and only one is muscle" reframe is genuinely surprising and 100% grounded. Weakness: it's inherently a list, so it risks reading as a wall of text — keep it tight, and it plays second fiddle to Concept 1's image.

_No third concept is proposed. The "nitrogen = the atom that makes a molecule organic/alive" definition angle (RARE-000182) is real but thin, and it is already absorbed as the opening line of Concept 2. Forcing it into a standalone third concept would be padding._

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

- **Everything is protein.** The element is nominally "nitrogen (mineral)" but every claim is a protein claim. That's fine and grounded — Wallach explicitly equates dietary nitrogen with protein (`RARE-000182`) — but the header must be honest that it's teaching protein, not molecular nitrogen.
- **No dose, no target.** Honest gap; §4 covers it.
- **Two near-duplicate deficiency claims.** `RARE-000185` and `EPIGEN-000101` state the same deficiency list; neither is superseded. Use them as one anchor, not two.
- **No interactions, no symptoms beyond the two deficiency signs.** Thin on breadth, but the depth on the two angles is real.
- **Verdict: MARGINAL solo header — workable, lead with Concept 1.** Unlike a truly thin element (a single bare stance), nitrogen has (a) a vivid, fully-mechanistic illustration in the Kwashiorkor/edema story and (b) a genuine second concept in the nine-jobs list. That's enough for a real, if modest, bespoke header. It is NOT strong (no dose, single-topic, only 5 claims), so do not attempt four concepts.
- **If Luneth prefers to group:** the natural home is a **protein / amino-acids** concept page (amino acids are canon essentials, and nitrogen's whole story is "dietary N → amino acids," `RARE-000182`). Folding nitrogen in there would lose nothing factually. My recommendation is a marginal solo header because the potbelly mechanism is strong enough to stand alone — but grouping is a defensible call and not padding-driven.
