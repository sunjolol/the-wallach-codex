## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

### Angle A — the precursor cascade (the spine)
Phenylalanine's whole identity in the pack is that it sits at the *top* of a conversion chain. Two mechanism claims lay it out:

- **`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000053`** — the full branch: phenylalanine → **tyrosine** → the monoamine signaling molecules **dopamine, norepinephrine (noradrenaline), epinephrine (adrenaline)**, plus the skin/hair pigment **melanin** ("this procedure requires the cofactor copper"). Same claim: the inborn error **phenylketonuria (PKU)** is the inability to metabolize phenylalanine — which Wallach says is "falsely believed to be genetically transmitted" — managed by *avoiding* phenylalanine (including the sweetener **aspartame**); and the **DL-phenylalanine** form is marketed as a **sleep aid, antidepressant, and analgesic (pain reliever)**.
- **`WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067`** — from his classic-essential-amino-acid table: "Phenylalanine precursor of **thyroxin and epinephrine**" (a thyroid hormone + adrenaline). Companion rows: tryptophan → niacin + serotonin; methionine → choline + creatine phosphate.

So the grounded product set is: **tyrosine → dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine, melanin** (EPIGEN) and **thyroxin, epinephrine** (DDDL). That is the entire mechanistic palette — do not add any pathway not in these two claims.

### Angle B — one amino acid, many conditions (because it feeds mood + pigment)
Seven protocol claims put phenylalanine into treatments, and they cluster exactly on the cascade's products (mood/drive molecules + pigment):

| Condition | Claim ID | Phenylalanine form + dose (from verbatim) |
|---|---|---|
| Bipolar disorder | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000159` | **DL**-phenylalanine, **5 g twice daily** |
| Depression | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000244` | **dl**-phenylalanine, **2 g three times daily** (with l-tryptophan + l-tyrosine each same) |
| Insomnia | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000322` | **dl**-phenylalanine, **250 mg three times daily** |
| Parkinsonism (drug-induced) | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000396` | **dl**-phenylalanine, **100 mg three times daily** |
| Multiple sclerosis / ALS | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000371` | **d**-phenylalanine, **500 mg three times daily** |
| Vitiligo | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000462` | **phenylalanine**, **50 mg/kg per day** (with copper 2 mg/day) |
| Inflammation | `WAL-CLM-LETS-000320` | claim_text says D-phenylalanine 1.5 g/day — **NOT in the verbatim (see §5); do not use** |

### Angle C — the FORM maps to the PURPOSE (a real, striking pattern in the data)
Read down the form column above and a pattern appears that is entirely grounded:
- **DL-phenylalanine** → the **mood / mind / sleep** conditions (bipolar, depression, insomnia, parkinsonism). This matches EPIGEN-000053's own note that the DL form is "marketed as a sleep aid, antidepressant, and analgesic."
- **D-phenylalanine** → the **nerve / pain** conditions (MS/ALS `LETS-000371`; and inflammation in the claim_text, though unverbatim'd).
- **plain phenylalanine** → **vitiligo** `LETS-000462`, the one pigment condition, paired with copper — the melanin branch of the cascade.

This is not an interpretation imposed on the data; it is what the seven verbatims literally say, form-letter by form-letter.

### Angle D — the melanin closed loop (vitiligo)
The tightest self-consistent thread in the pack: EPIGEN-000053 says phenylalanine → tyrosine → **melanin, requiring copper**. `LETS-000462` treats **vitiligo (loss of skin pigment)** with **phenylalanine 50 mg/kg/day + copper 2 mg/day**. Mechanism and protocol close on each other — the pigment the cascade builds, and the deficiency of that pigment, treated with the cascade's two inputs.

### The contrarian note (PKU)
`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000053` — phenylketonuria, which Wallach frames as "falsely believed to be genetically transmitted," is the one case where the move is to *remove* phenylalanine (and aspartame). A grounded twist, not the header's lead, but a strong Worth-Knowing beat.

## 2. Header concepts (3 — the honest number the material supports)

### Concept 1 — "The forerunner molecule" (the cascade) ★ lead
- **The hook.** Phenylalanine makes almost nothing directly — it is the *raw material* the body converts into the chemicals that drive mood, alertness, and colour. One input, many outputs.
- **Layout shape.** A single source node on the left, a branch out to its products on the right. Not beats, not a quote chassis — an annotated fork/tree that IS the argument. Text lives at the leaves.
- **Illustration (one idea).** One labelled starting node **phenylalanine**, a first split to **tyrosine**, then a fan of four leaves: **dopamine · norepinephrine · epinephrine · melanin** (route the melanin leaf through a small **+ copper** tag), with **thyroxin** shown as a second direct branch off phenylalanine. Fewest elements: one trunk, one intermediate, the product leaves. No stroke crosses any label — the fan spreads so lines land in the gaps between words.
- **Anchored by.** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000053` (tyrosine → dopamine, norepinephrine, epinephrine, melanin+copper); `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067` (thyroxin, epinephrine). Every leaf is a named product in these two claims — nothing invented.
- **Why it wows / best UX.** It answers "what does this actually DO?" in one glance, and it sets up the entire condition list: a reader who sees "makes your drive molecules and your pigment" already understands why the same amino acid shows up under depression *and* vitiligo. Highest information-per-pixel, fully bespoke, impossible to mistake for a template.

### Concept 2 — "Same molecule, sorted by letter" (form → purpose)
- **The hook.** Wallach doesn't reach for one phenylalanine — he picks the **form** to fit the job: **DL** for the mind, **D** for the nerves, plain for pigment.
- **Layout shape.** Three labelled lanes (DL / D / plain), each carrying its grounded conditions. A sorting diagram, not a chart of numbers.
- **Illustration (one idea).** Three horizontal bands headed **DL- · D- · phenylalanine**; under DL: bipolar, depression, insomnia, parkinsonism; under D: MS / ALS; under plain: vitiligo (+ copper). The EPIGEN line "sleep aid · antidepressant · analgesic" sits as the caption on the DL band.
- **Anchored by.** Forms/conditions from `LETS-000159` (DL, bipolar), `LETS-000244` (dl, depression), `LETS-000322` (dl, insomnia), `LETS-000396` (dl, parkinsonism), `LETS-000371` (d, MS/ALS), `LETS-000462` (plain, vitiligo); DL-form uses from `EPIGEN-000053`.
- **Why it wows.** It surfaces a pattern a reader would never notice scrolling the protocol list — genuinely a "huh, I didn't know that" moment, and 100% grounded in the verbatims. Risk: the D-lane is thin (one verbatim'd condition), so the three bands are visually uneven — a real design constraint, not a dealbreaker.

### Concept 3 — "The pigment loop" (vitiligo / melanin)
- **The hook.** The colour in your skin is *built* from phenylalanine (through tyrosine, with copper) — so where colour is missing, Wallach reaches for the same two inputs.
- **Layout shape.** A short two-step figure that closes on itself: the build path on top, the deficiency + treatment on the bottom, meeting at "melanin."
- **Illustration (one idea).** phenylalanine → tyrosine → **melanin** (+ copper tag), with a paired line: **vitiligo = melanin missing → phenylalanine 50 mg/kg + copper 2 mg**. One clean loop, no clutter.
- **Anchored by.** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000053` (melanin path + copper cofactor); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000462` (vitiligo: phenylalanine 50 mg/kg/day, copper 2 mg/day).
- **Why it wows.** The tightest mechanism-to-protocol closure in the whole pack. But it is narrower than Concept 1 (it uses one condition), so it is better as a *section within* Concept 1's page than as the header's spine.

*(No fourth concept. A fourth would be padding — the PKU/aspartame contrarian note and the thyroid branch are real but belong in Worth-Knowing beats under Concept 1, not as a standalone header idea.)*

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

**Flags:**
- ⚠ **`WAL-CLM-LETS-000320` (inflammation) — claim_text/verbatim mismatch.** The claim_text lists "D-phenylalanine ... each at 1.5 grams per day," but the supplied `verbatim` is truncated at "zinc at 50 mg t.i.d" and contains **no phenylalanine dose at all**. Per the grounding rule (trust the verbatim), the inflammation dose is **not usable** in the header. Inflammation may still be named as a condition only if sourced from a fuller verbatim later; for now, omit its number.
- No `deficiency_signs` in the pack — so a "signs of shortage" beat has nothing to stand on. Do not invent deficiency signs. (The lede's "ripples into mood/drive/colour" is an inference from the *products*, phrased as consequence-of-the-cascade, not as a listed deficiency sign — keep it framed that way.)
- No page/chapter for the two mechanism claims and three protocols (`null`); citations compose from `book_id` as usual — fine, just noted.
- `dose` field is `null` on every claim; all doses live inside the verbatims/claim_texts, so they must be pulled by claim ID at render (R3), never hand-typed.

**SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict: SOLO — strong.**
Phenylalanine clears the bar for a bespoke solo header comfortably. It has (a) a clean, one-idea mechanism (the precursor cascade) that is visually native, (b) a genuinely surprising secondary pattern (form → purpose), and (c) a self-closing mechanism↔protocol thread (melanin/vitiligo). That is more grounded, distinct material than several first-tier trace minerals carry. It should **not** be folded into an "amino acids" group — doing so would bury the one thing that makes it legible (its cascade). If a grouping ever were forced, its natural neighbours are the other precursor aminos in `DDDL-000067` (tryptophan → niacin/serotonin; methionine → choline/creatine) — but the recommendation is **solo**.
