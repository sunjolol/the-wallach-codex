## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

The whole pack is **two claims that state ONE fact** — carbon is the essential structural element/atom of all organic molecules — restated across two different books. There is no dose, no deficiency sign, no condition, no symptom, no interaction. This section is short because the material is short; padding it would be a defect.

**The single angle — carbon is the structural backbone of every organic molecule.**
- **WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000049** (definition, HIGH). Verbatim: *"Carbon functions as an essential structural element for all living organisms and organic molecules (i.e.- carbohydrates, fats, amino acids, vitamins, enzymes, etc.)."* This is the only clean, high-confidence, verbatim-backed statement in the pack.
- **WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000073** (mechanism, MEDIUM). Verbatim: *"Carbon functions as an essential structural atom for all organic molecules."* This is the SAME claim, near word-for-word, from a second book. It adds nothing new in its verbatim.

**What the claim_texts assert but the verbatims do NOT (see §5 — flagged, do not use as quoted Wallach).**
- IMMORT-000049 claim_text elaborates: carbon is "the center of the 'organic' world," carbon atoms "link into long chains that form the backbone (spine)" of biological molecules, and "DNA itself is one such long carbon-chain molecule." **None of that phrasing is in the verbatim** — the verbatim only lists carbohydrates/fats/amino acids/vitamins/enzymes as the things carbon structures.
- EPIGEN-000073 claim_text carries the only numbers in the pack: carbon present at *"around 454,000 parts per million in land plants and 465,000 in land animals, with roughly 280,000 in bone,"* plus "one of the few elements known since antiquity." **None of these figures appear in the verbatim.** The verbatim is a single sentence with no numbers at all. Per grounding rule 5, these numbers are NOT quotable as Wallach's words.

So the trustworthy palette is exactly one sentence, stated twice: *carbon is the essential structural element/atom of all organic molecules — carbohydrates, fats, amino acids, vitamins, enzymes.*

## 2. Header concept (ONE honest concept — the material supports no more)

Forcing this into 4 concepts would invent 3. Here is the single concept the two claims genuinely support, plus one clearly-flagged conditional extension.

### A. The backbone of everything organic
- **Name** — Carbon = the frame every biomolecule is built on.
- **The hook** — Every building block of the body — carbohydrates, fats, amino acids, vitamins, enzymes — is carbon-based; carbon is the one structural element organic life is assembled from.
- **Layout shape** — A single hub-and-family figure: one central "C" atom node with the five named biomolecule classes radiating off it as labelled tiles. NOT a chassis of eyebrow→beats→quote — just the one relationship, shown once. Fewest elements: one centre, five labels, no line routed through any label.
- **Illustration** — One idea: a central carbon node with five clean spokes to `carbohydrates · fats · amino acids · vitamins · enzymes` (the exact five in the verbatim). No arbitrary chemistry props, no ball-and-stick clutter — the point is "all of these share one frame," not a molecular diagram.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000049 (verbatim lists exactly these five classes) + WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000073 (the second-book restatement, "essential structural atom for all organic molecules"). Both verbatims say the same thing; the header states it once and lets the two-book sourcing be its weight.
- **Why it wows / best UX** — It is true, it is high-confidence, and it is legible in one glance. But be honest: it is a *category* statement, not a health lever — there is no deficiency, no dose, no "first visible sign" the reader acts on. It informs; it does not grip the way selenium/cancer or copper/grey-hair grips.

**Conditional extension (ONLY if the concentration numbers are ratified into a verbatim first).** IF WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000073's numbers (454,000 ppm land plants · 465,000 land animals · 280,000 bone) are re-sourced into an actual verbatim, a small three-bar "how much of living tissue is carbon" figure becomes possible. **As the pack stands, those numbers are claim_text only and NOT usable** — do not build this on the current data.

- **Only 2 claims, and they are the SAME claim twice.** Both verbatims say "carbon functions as an essential structural element/atom for all organic molecules." This is one fact double-sourced, not two facts. That is the whole grounded palette.
- **No target / no dose / no deficiency signs / no conditions / no symptoms / no interactions.** Every one of those pack fields is empty. There is no health-lever, no "first sign," no protocol — nothing a reader acts on.

Use the five classes; do not attribute the backbone/DNA phrasing to Wallach's quoted words.
- **No superseded claims.** All `superseded_by` null.
- **SOLO-vs-GROUP VERDICT: TOO THIN for a bespoke solo header.** One verbatim fact, double-sourced, with no dose/sign/condition, cannot carry a full "how it works" header the way a 10-claim element (oxygen) or a first-tier nutrient can. On such a concept page carbon gets one clean tile (the five-biomolecule-class fact, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000049), which is exactly what the material supports — no more, no less. This matches the "group thin elements into concepts" pattern.
