# Oxygen — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 10 sealed claims · mineral (category accent: blue) · target: NONE — honest gap, oxygen carries no maintenance dose (blueprint §7.1). Single sourced book: rare-earths (Rare Earths: Forbidden Cures, 1994), Ch 11 + Ch 9.

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity — the four-minute element (the essentiality claim).**
Oxygen is the structural atom of water and of every organic compound of biological interest, required for respiration by all organisms except anaerobes. Wallach's survival ladder: **30 days without food · 3–7 days without water · ~4 minutes without oxygen** — "the most critical of all elemental factors for the maintenance of human life." (WAL-CLM-RARE-000195, definition, high confidence). This is the identity spine — the only definition-kind claim in the pack.

**The thinning air — atmospheric oxygen decline.**
Wallach cites USGS figures for a long slide in atmospheric oxygen. **Verbatim-supported numbers only: 21% by the 1950s → ~19% today.** (WAL-CLM-RARE-000196, mechanism, high). ⚠ The claim_text also carries a fuller arc — ~50% oxygen 75 million years ago → 38% (a level paleontologists theorize helped end the dinosaurs) → 21% → 19% — but the **verbatim quotes ONLY the "21% in the 1950s / 19% today" pair.** The 50% / 38% / 75-million-year / dinosaur figures are NOT in this claim's verbatim (see §5). Cause of decline per claim_text: more oxygen-consumers + fossil-fuel burning, fewer producers (rain forests, aquatic algae).

**The anaerobic counter-revolution — low oxygen breeds disease.**
Wallach argues falling oxygen created a "relative anaerobic state" in which pathogens (most of them anaerobic) flourish. Verbatim: pathogenic organisms "flourish and reproduce with more vigor in the absence of oxygen" (gangrene organisms, Type A Streptococcus) or survive in cells weakened by low-oxygen environments (viruses, yeast, fungus, cancer). (WAL-CLM-RARE-000197, mechanism, **medium** confidence). ⚠ The dramatic disease roster in the claim_text — HIV, Epstein-Barr, CMV, Herpes II, Hanta, Candida, E. coli toxic shock, flesh-eating Type A Strep, "oxygen counter revolution" — is claim_text framing; the verbatim names only gangrene + Type A Strep + the virus/yeast/fungus/cancer category (see §5).

**Warburg — the cancer cell that dies in oxygen.**
Dr. Otto Warburg (Max Planck Institute, two-time Nobel laureate) determined the cancer cell's metabolism is fermentative and anaerobic while the normal cell is aerobic. Verbatim: "cancer cell's ferment sugar under anaerobic conditions and die in the presence of oxygen." (WAL-CLM-RARE-000198, mechanism, high). This is the pack's single most provocative, quotable line.

**The body's own oxygen weapon — neutrophils, peroxide, catalase, singlet oxygen.**
Neutrophils (white blood cells) destroy microbes, parasites, and cancer cells with hydrogen peroxide made by peroxisomes; excess H₂O₂ is broken by the enzyme catalase into water and **singlet oxygen (O)**. (WAL-CLM-RARE-000199, mechanism, high). Singlet (atomic) oxygen kills cells on direct contact OUTSIDE the circulatory system (culture, test tube, wound), yet inside the living body its lone free electron, within nanoseconds, either pairs with a carcinogen's free electron or grabs another singlet oxygen to become O₂ — "the required stuff of respiration." (WAL-CLM-RARE-000200, mechanism, high). A genuine two-faced-molecule story: killer outside, respiration inside.

**Oxygen as medicine — since the Civil War.**
Oxygen delivered as hydrogen peroxide has been used topically, intravenously, and orally since the Civil War, and 50+ years in Europe for cancer, circulatory disease, arteriosclerosis, emphysema, asthma, and gangrene — and recently for long-term stroke, where dormant "sleeping beauty" cells around the stroke site reactivate under several atmospheres of oxygen in a hyperbaric chamber. (WAL-CLM-RARE-000202, protocol, high). Food-grade H₂O₂ in proper dilution on an empty stomach (or IV) absorbs through stomach/duodenal walls into the blood and immediately breaks into water + singlet oxygen. (WAL-CLM-RARE-000201, mechanism, high).

**The therapeutic window — a precise Goldilocks band (curio + precision number).**
Citing Dr. Renate Viebahn's *The Use of Ozone in Medicine* (22 peer-reviewed articles on ozone vs cancer cells), Wallach describes a bell-shaped "therapeutic window" for ozone: **20–100 µg/ml of blood** — less is ineffective, more damages normal cells. (WAL-CLM-RARE-000203, prognosis, high). Ed McCabe's caveat: the rare cases where ozone aggravates a condition trace to mineral-deficient diets — a minerally deficient body lacks the antioxidant-enzyme systems to use oxygen correctly. This is the ONE precise numeric range in the whole pack.

**Oxygen in a protocol list (thin, contextual).**
Oxygen (O₂) appears in the dementia mineral-replacement list alongside Ga, Ge, Se, Ca, Cr, Va, Li, B-1, B-3 (no sugar, no caffeine). (WAL-CLM-RARE-000282, protocol, high). Supports the "interacts_with" web but is not header-lead material.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A. The Four-Minute Element
- **Name** — Survival ladder.
- **The hook** — You can last a month without food and days without water, but only ~4 minutes without oxygen — it is THE most critical element.
- **Layout shape** — A vertical descending scale / three-rung ladder: three time-markers dropping from a long span to a razor-thin one, the last one visually collapsed to almost nothing to dramatize the cliff.
- **Illustration** — Three stacked bars (or three descending tick-marks on one axis), NOT boxes-with-arrows: **30 days** (long) · **3–7 days** (short) · **~4 minutes** (a sliver). One idea, three elements, no line routed through any label — labels sit to the side of each bar.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-RARE-000195 ("30 days without food, 3 to 7 days without water… only four minutes without gaseous Oxygen"; "the most critical of all elemental factors").
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Universally visceral and instantly legible; it defines oxygen's identity in one glance and needs no prior knowledge. It leans on the pack's single high-confidence definition claim, so it is the safest-grounded of the four.

### B. The Thinning Air
- **Name** — Falling oxygen line.
- **The hook** — The atmosphere itself is losing oxygen — 21% in the 1950s, ~19% today — and Wallach reads that drop as a driver of modern disease.
- **Layout shape** — A single descending line/timeline across the block, reading left-to-right through time, ending on today's figure as the emphasized number.
- **Illustration** — A clean downward-sloping line with two anchored, verbatim-safe points: **21% (1950s)** and **~19% (today)**. The dinosaur-era arc (50%→38%) can appear ONLY as a small optional "did-you-know" caption if the designer wants it, clearly separated — because it is NOT verbatim-backed (see §5). Keep the core line to the two safe points; no stroke crossing a label.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-RARE-000196 (verbatim: "During the 1950's the percentage of oxygen in our atmosphere dropped to 21% and today only 19%… is oxygen!"). Bridges to WAL-CLM-RARE-000197 (falling oxygen → anaerobic disease).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Surprising and counter-intuitive; a "wait, what?" number the reader has never seen. Distinct from every shipped header (no falling-timeline exists). Best paired with a one-line "so what" bridge to the anaerobic-disease idea.

### C. Dies in the Presence of Oxygen
- **Name** — Aerobic vs anaerobic two-state.
- **The hook** — Cancer cells ferment sugar without oxygen and DIE when oxygen reaches them — Warburg's finding, Wallach's frame.
- **Layout shape** — A two-state contrast: two cells (or two panels) side by side — the normal aerobic cell thriving in oxygen, the fermentative cancer cell shown collapsing/dying as oxygen arrives. A single pivot idea, not a busy diagram.
- **Illustration** — Two simple cell glyphs: LEFT "normal cell — aerobic, uses oxygen"; RIGHT "cancer cell — ferments sugar, dies in oxygen." The oxygen molecules could be a few dots entering the right cell as it fades. Two figures, minimal marks, labels beside not through.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-RARE-000198 (Warburg; verbatim "cancer cell's ferment sugar under anaerobic conditions and die in the presence of oxygen"). Support: WAL-CLM-RARE-000197 (anaerobic organisms flourish without oxygen), WAL-CLM-RARE-000203 (ozone vs cancer cells).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — The most provocative, most quotable claim in the pack, with a Nobel-laureate attribution that lends weight. High emotional charge — best for a reader who lands on the cancer angle. Cite Warburg by name to anchor authority.

### D. The Therapeutic Window
- **Name** — The oxygen Goldilocks band.
- **The hook** — Oxygen has been medicine since the Civil War — but it works only inside a narrow band: too little does nothing, too much harms.
- **Layout shape** — A single bell-curve figure with a highlighted middle band, flanked by "too little / too much" shaded shoulders — the one precise number in the corpus made visual.
- **Illustration** — One bell curve; the peak/plateau band labeled **20–100 µg/ml of blood (ozone)**; left shoulder "less = ineffective," right shoulder "more = damages normal cells." One idea, few elements; the band is the whole point. (Optional secondary beat: the "used since the Civil War / hyperbaric 'sleeping-beauty' stroke cells" therapy heritage as a caption line, NOT a second figure.)
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-RARE-000203 (bell-shaped window, "20 to 100 u/ml of blood… less is ineffective — more can be damaging"; Viebahn, 22 articles; McCabe's mineral-deficiency caveat). Support: WAL-CLM-RARE-000202 (Civil War / Europe 50 yrs / hyperbaric stroke), WAL-CLM-RARE-000201 (food-grade H₂O₂ therapy).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — The only figure the corpus can support with a real, precise numeric range — a rarity for an element with NO daily target. A bell curve reads instantly and feels clinical/credible. Distinct in both layout and figure from A/B/C and from all six shipped headers.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

**There is no daily number for oxygen — and that is the honest, correct state.** `target.kind` is `unspecified`: the Wallach framework states no maintenance amount, because oxygen is not a supplement you dose in milligrams — you breathe it. Every number in the corpus is either **environmental** (atmospheric oxygen ~19% today, down from 21% in the 1950s — WAL-CLM-RARE-000196) or **therapeutic and clinician-administered** (the ozone therapeutic window of 20–100 µg/ml of blood — WAL-CLM-RARE-000203), never a personal daily target. So the "why this number" slot should say plainly: oxygen carries no maintenance dose in this framework; its numbers describe the air we breathe and clinical oxygen therapy, not a pill to take. Do NOT fabricate a per-day figure.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **No target / no dose.** `target.kind: unspecified` — an honest gap. The header cannot show a daily number; §4 handles this honestly. This makes oxygen unusual among the "minerals."
- **No deficiency signs.** `deficiency_signs` is empty. There is no "first visible sign" angle (unlike copper's grey hair or zinc's nail spots). Do not invent one.
- **⚠ claim_text > verbatim mismatch, WAL-CLM-RARE-000196.** The claim_text narrates 50% (75M years ago) → 38% (dinosaur extinction) → 21% → 19%, but the **verbatim quotes only "21% (1950s) / 19% (today)."** Per grounding rule 5, trust the verbatim: Concept B must build on the 21%→19% pair; the 50/38/dinosaur figures may only be used as clearly-separated "did-you-know" text if used at all, and a designer should be told they are claim_text framing, not quoted Wallach words.
- **⚠ claim_text > verbatim, WAL-CLM-RARE-000197 (medium confidence).** The disease roster (HIV, EBV, CMV, Herpes II, Hanta, Candida, E. coli toxic shock, flesh-eating Strep) and the phrase "oxygen counter revolution" are claim_text; the verbatim names only gangrene + Type A Strep + the virus/yeast/fungus/cancer category. Use the verbatim category, not the specific disease list, in any headline text. This is the pack's only medium-confidence claim.
- **No superseded claims.** All `superseded_by` are null; no newest-number conflict to resolve.
- **Third-party attributions are load-bearing.** The strongest claims rest on named outside figures (Warburg — 000198; Viebahn + McCabe — 000203). That is faithful to Wallach (he cites them), but the header should attribute them, not present the findings as Wallach's own bench work.
- **Solo header verdict: YES, it merits a bespoke solo header.** Despite the no-dose oddity, oxygen has a genuinely rich, self-contained 10-claim narrative (essentiality, atmospheric decline, Warburg, singlet-oxygen mechanism, oxygen therapy, a precise therapeutic window) — more than enough distinct material for four real concepts. It should NOT fold into another element. Note it is single-sourced (all 10 claims from rare-earths).
- **Concept wished-for but unsupported:** a clean "where oxygen lives in the body / how much of you is oxygen" abundance map (calcium-style) is NOT supported — the pack gives no body-composition percentage for oxygen. Do not build one.
