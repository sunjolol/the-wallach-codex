# Hydrogen — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 12 sealed claims · mineral · target: no Wallach maintenance amount stated (honest gap). solo-header: MARGINAL-to-STRONG (one genuinely distinctive concept — the H⁺ / acid-base reframe — plus real supporting material).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

Hydrogen is unusual for a "mineral" entry: the pack is 12 claims but almost none are dose/deficiency material. It clusters into four angles, one of which is genuinely strong.

**Angle A — Acid-base balance IS hydrogen (the distinctive Wallach reframe; the spine).**
This is the one idea here that only Wallach's framing gives you, and it repeats across three books:
- The core reframe: "The regulation of the acid-base balance in the human body is in fact the regulation of the hydrogen ion (H+) levels of cellular and extracellular fluids." — EPIGEN-000087, IMMORT-000150. Healthy blood pH 7.36–7.44; hydrogen is a major constituent of water (≈70% of the body) and of all organic molecules. IMMORT-000150 / EPIGEN-000087.
- The thresholds (a clean, hard number set): pH < 7.30 = acidosis; > 7.44 = alkalosis; < 6.8 or > 7.8 = **rapidly fatal**; intracellular pH 6.0–7.4. IMMORT-000151 (also RARE-000135, and inside EPIGEN-000087).
- The clearance machinery + its timescales: the body controls H+ by dilution, buffering (fractions of a second), respiration (minutes), and kidney (hours→days). IMMORT-000152, EPIGEN-000087. The kidney excretes metabolic H+ as 60% ammonium / 40% weak acids / trace free ions — and "it is the amount of free hydrogen ions in the urine that determines the urine's pH." IMMORT-000152.
- What pushes pH down: rapid metabolism (hyperthyroid/Graves') or reduced blood flow (heart attack) raise CO₂ and acidify blood. IMMORT-000151. Disease states generating/retaining H+: emphysema, diabetes, anxiety, chloride loss, cystic fibrosis, Addison's. EPIGEN-000087.

**Angle B — The hydrogenation warning (a strong, characteristic Wallach stance).**
- Pumping hydrogen gas into liquid oils ("hydrogenation") hardens them into margarine and makes trans fats. Once sold by "the medical trade" as a safe butter substitute to *prevent* arteriosclerosis, atherosclerosis and stroke — "now known to be the direct cause of these diseases." Fried foods, super-heated carbs (breads, French fries, croutons), margarines and trans fats produce the inflammation that damages the arterial lining (intima) → clogged arteries, clots, thrombosis, stroke. IMMORT-000155. (Conditions: arteriosclerosis, atherosclerosis, stroke, thrombosis.)

**Angle C — The one practical, food-based tip.**
- Cystitis (bladder infection) can often be controlled by acidifying the urine with unsweetened cranberry juice; meat acidifies urine/saliva, vegetables alkalinize them. IMMORT-000153, RARE-000136. This is the only operational protocol in the pack and it ties directly back to Angle A (free H+ sets urine pH).

**Angle D — Chemistry & history color (educational, not operational).**
- Simplest atom (one proton, one electron); 93% of all atoms in the universe; diatomic, colorless, odorless, flammable, burns with oxygen to form water (Greek *hydro* "water" + *genes* "creator"). IMMORT-000147.
- A significant part of living molecules — carbohydrates, sugars, fats, proteins, enzymes, DNA, RNA. IMMORT-000154.
- Discovered by Cavendish (1766, zinc + hydrochloric acid); eccentric — never spoke to women, fired servants he saw. IMMORT-000148.
- Lighter than air → lift gas → the Hindenburg fire (1937) ended flammable-hydrogen airships. IMMORT-000149.

## 2. Header concepts (3 honest concepts)

### Concept 1 — "Your pH is a hydrogen count" (THE lead)
- **The hook.** The reframe that makes a reader stop: the acid-base balance every clinic measures is, underneath, just *how many hydrogen ions* are floating in your fluids. Wallach says it flatly — regulating pH "is in fact the regulation of the hydrogen ion (H+) levels." The wow is that an abstract number (pH) becomes a literal count of the universe's simplest atom.
- **Layout shape.** A single horizontal pH scale as the hero, annotated with Wallach's exact thresholds, with the H⁺ reframe as the line beneath it. No beats-chassis; the scale IS the illustration and the argument.
- **Illustration (one idea).** A horizontal band from ~6.8 to ~7.8. Marked zones: a green safe window 7.36–7.44, acidosis below 7.30, alkalosis above 7.44, and the two hard outer walls at 6.8 and 7.8 labelled "rapidly fatal." Density of small H⁺ dots increases toward the acidic end to make "more hydrogen = more acidic" visible at a glance. Route every label ABOVE or BELOW the band, never a tick-stroke through the numbers (element-headers Rule 2 stroke-through trap).
- **Anchored by.** IMMORT-000151 (7.30 / 7.44 / 6.8 / 7.8 / intracellular 6.0–7.4), EPIGEN-000087 + IMMORT-000150 (the H+ reframe; 7.36–7.44; ≈70% water). Optional second row: clearance timescales — buffer (sub-second) → breath (minutes) → kidney (hours–days), IMMORT-000152.
- **Why it wows / best UX.** It is the only concept here that is distinctively Wallach and carries a crisp, memorable, hard-number payload. A pH strip is instantly legible to any lay reader and needs no decoding.

### Concept 2 — "The same reaction that hardens margarine hardens arteries"
- **The hook.** Hydrogen isn't just structural — it's what industry *pumps into oil* to make margarine, and Wallach's charge is blunt: the "safe butter substitute" sold to prevent artery disease is the direct cause of it.
- **Layout shape.** A two-state before/after: liquid oil + H₂ → hardened fat, paired with a short "sold as prevention / now known to cause" reversal line. A single figure plus one emphatic claim, no quote required.
- **Illustration (one idea).** A simple transformation: a flask of pouring oil, an H₂ arrow, a solid pat of margarine — then the same solidifying motif echoed as plaque narrowing an artery. One visual metaphor (hydrogen hardens things), used twice. Keep it to ~4 elements; avoid the many-station diagram Luneth rejects.
- **Anchored by.** IMMORT-000155 (hydrogenation → trans fats → inflammation of the intima → arteriosclerosis/atherosclerosis/thrombosis/stroke; "direct cause of these diseases").
- **Why it wows / best UX.** It's the most emotionally charged, contrarian claim in the pack and it's self-contained in one sealed claim. Risk: it's arguably a *fats/trans-fat* story more than a *hydrogen-the-nutrient* story, so it may read better as a "Worth knowing" block than as the hero.

### Concept 3 (minimal fallback) — "The element you're 70% made of"
- **The hook.** Hydrogen is the simplest atom and 93% of all atoms in existence, and you are ≈70% water — so more of you, by atom count, is hydrogen than anything else.
- **Layout shape.** A single big-stat opener (93% / 70%) with one line tying it to the body, leading into the acid-base material below.
- **Illustration (one idea).** A body silhouette filling with H atoms, or a single oversized "H" with the two stats set beside it. Minimal.
- **Anchored by.** IMMORT-000147 (93% of atoms; simplest atom), EPIGEN-000087/IMMORT-000150 (≈70% water).
- **Why it wows / best UX.** Cheapest, safest, most universal hook — but it's generic chemistry, not distinctively Wallach. Use only if Concept 1's pH strip is judged too clinical for a hero.

*(Angle D's Cavendish/Hindenburg trivia is genuine and charming but is entity-page "Worth knowing" color, not header material. Do not build a fourth concept from it — that would be padding.)*

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

There is **no daily target to justify.** `target.kind` is `dietary` and the pack states plainly: "Wallach framework — no maintenance amount stated (honest gap; blueprint §7.1)." Hydrogen carries no dose in any of the 12 claims (`dose: null` throughout).

So the "why this number" slot should NOT invent a dietary number. If a number-anchored tip is wanted, use the **pH window** as the header's numeric spine instead — it is real and sealed: healthy blood 7.36–7.44, acidosis < 7.30, alkalosis > 7.44, fatal < 6.8 / > 7.8 (IMMORT-000151, IMMORT-000150). Frame it as "why these thresholds," not "why this dose." Never present a hydrogen intake amount.

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

**Flags:**
- **No dose, no deficiency signs, no target.** `deficiency_signs: []`, `interacts_with: []`, `target` is an honest gap. This is not a supplement-you-take element; it's a structural/physiology element. The header must not imply a recommended intake.
- **Heavy duplication across books.** The pH reframe + thresholds appear in IMMORT-000150, IMMORT-000151, EPIGEN-000087, RARE-000135; the cranberry tip in IMMORT-000153 and RARE-000136. That's ~12 claims but ~4 distinct ideas. Prefer the fullest statement (EPIGEN-000087 and IMMORT-000151 carry the most complete numbers); don't let the count read as depth.
- **No claim_text/verbatim number mismatch of concern.** EPIGEN-000087's claim_text lists disease states (emphysema, diabetes, etc.) not in its short verbatim, but those are qualitative, not fabricated numbers; still, anchor header numbers to IMMORT-000151/000150 verbatims, which carry them directly.
- **Concept 2 (hydrogenation) is arguably off-element** — it's a trans-fat/arterial story that happens to name hydrogen. Legitimate to feature, but it belongs as much to the fats narrative.

**Verdict: MARGINAL-to-STRONG for a SOLO header.** This is NOT too thin to fold away. Unlike a true 1–2-claim orphan, hydrogen has one genuinely distinctive, wow-capable, hard-numbered Wallach concept (the H⁺/acid-base reframe with the pH-threshold ladder) plus two real supporting angles. It can carry a bespoke solo header built on Concept 1. It does NOT need grouping. Keep it lean — one strong figure (the pH strip), the reframe line, and the hydrogenation warning as a secondary block — and do not manufacture a fourth section from the Cavendish/Hindenburg trivia.
