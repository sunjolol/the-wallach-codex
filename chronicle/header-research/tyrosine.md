# Tyrosine — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 6 sealed claims (2 are the same statement across two books) · amino acid · target: no Wallach maintenance amount — honest gap, but two clinical-lever doses exist · solo-header: marginal (leaning solo, on the strength of ONE bespoke fork concept).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Angle A — One of the three amino acids Wallach ADDS to the classic essential list (signature disease: goiter).**
- `WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058` (definition, Immortality p.113): "To the classic list of essential amino acids, we would add arginine, taurine, and tyrosine… over the long haul these three help prevent certain specific diseases" — respectively cancer and PAD (arginine), macular degeneration (taurine), **goiter (tyrosine)**.
- `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066` (quote, DDDL): the SAME statement in DDDL — arginine→cancer, taurine→macular degeneration, **tyrosine→goiter**. (Effectively a duplicate of IMMORT-000058; first-person "I would add." Two books, one fact. Prefer both as cross-book corroboration, not two separate ideas.)
- Key phrase: **"To the classic list… I would add… tyrosine."** Signature disease it guards: **goiter**.

**Angle B — The thyroid raw material: iodine + tyrosine → thyroxin.**
- `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000023` (mechanism, DDDL): "In combination with the amino acid tyrosine, iodine is manufactured into the thyroid hormone thyroxin." The claim_text notes thyroxin "sets the body's metabolic rate," iodine intake is usually low, salt-cutting has pushed goiter and hypothyroidism "toward epidemic levels," and iodine is lost freely in sweat.
- This is the MECHANISM behind Angle A's goiter claim: tyrosine is literally one of the two building blocks of the thyroid hormone.

**Angle C — Brain precursor: dopamine + norepinephrine, and the stress lever.**
- `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000057` (mechanism, Epigenetics ch18 p.640): "Tyrosine is a precursor for the synthesis of neurotransmitters, and particularly for dopamine and norepinephrine." Also a receiver of phosphate groups in cell signalling. Wallach reports studies showing benefit for **colds, stress, fatigue, loss of a loved one or divorce, prolonged work hours, and sleep deprivation** — reducing stress-hormone levels and stress-related weight loss, improving cognitive/physical performance — **but its effect is less than L-Dopa because tyrosine hydroxylase is the rate-limiting enzyme.**

**Angle D — Clinical protocol doses (levers, not maintenance targets).**
- `WAL-CLM-LETS-000244` (protocol, Let's Play Doctor ch10 — depression): l-tyrosine **2 gm three times daily** (alongside dl-phenylalanine + l-tryptophan at the same dose), within a large depression protocol.
- `WAL-CLM-LETS-000396` (protocol, Let's Play Doctor ch10 p.159 — drug-induced Parkinsonism): L-tyrosine **100 mg/day** (alongside dl-phenylalanine, octacosanol, leucine, methionine, etc.).
- Note the two doses differ by ~60× (2000 mg t.i.d. vs 100 mg/day) — because they are condition-specific clinical levers, NOT a daily maintenance amount.

**What the pack does NOT contain:** no deficiency signs, no maintenance/RDA-style target, no "how much per day for everyone" number.

## 2. Header concepts (honestly supported by the material)

### Concept A — "One amino acid, two organs" (THE fork) ★ lead
- **The hook:** Tyrosine is the single raw material that feeds two completely different systems — the thyroid and the brain. Combine it with iodine and it becomes thyroxin, the hormone that sets your metabolic rate; feed it to a nerve and it becomes dopamine and norepinephrine. One molecule, two destinations.
- **Layout shape:** A single central node (tyrosine) with two divergent paths — NOT a 1-2-3 beat stack. Left path resolves to the thyroid (thyroxin → metabolic rate, goiter guarded); right path resolves to the brain (dopamine/norepinephrine → mood, stress). The two paths are the whole figure; prose is one short line under each.
- **Illustration (one idea):** A fork. One labelled source at the apex, two clean routes diverging to two labelled endpoints. No third path, no crossbars. Keep labels off the stroke of each route (element-headers Rule 2 — a line through a label is the #1 rejection cause).
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000023` (tyrosine + iodine → thyroxin), `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000057` (tyrosine → dopamine + norepinephrine), goiter from `WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058` / `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** It turns a thin element into a genuinely memorable one-glance truth. Two mechanism claims from two different books converge on one clean visual. It is bespoke (no beats/quote chassis) and it is the ONE idea in this pack that couldn't be told about any other amino acid.

### Concept B — "Half of the thyroid hormone" (the goiter story)
- **The hook:** Wallach reads a swollen thyroid — goiter — as a shortage story, and tyrosine is one of its two missing pieces. Thyroxin is built from exactly two things: iodine and tyrosine.
- **Layout shape:** An assembly — two labelled inputs (iodine, tyrosine) joining into one output (thyroxin), with goiter named as what a shortage produces. No beats.
- **Illustration (one idea):** Two pieces snapping into one hormone. Simplest possible: two labelled tokens → one labelled product.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000023` (the combine → thyroxin mechanism), `WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058` / `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066` (tyrosine guards goiter).
- **Why it wows:** Concrete and physical — you can SEE why tyrosine matters for the thyroid. Weakness: it tells only half of what the pack supports (drops the brain/stress angle entirely), so it under-uses the material relative to Concept A.

### Concept C — "The three Wallach added to the list"
- **The hook:** The textbook essential-amino-acid list stops short; Wallach appends three — arginine, taurine, tyrosine — each standing guard over one disease. Tyrosine's post is goiter.
- **Layout shape:** A closed classic list with three appended entries, tyrosine highlighted and tied to its disease.
- **Illustration (one idea):** A list with a visible seam — "the classic set" then "+ 3", tyrosine's row lit and pointing to goiter.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058`, `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066`.
- **Why it wows / caveat:** Good framing, and it's the strongest cross-book fact (stated twice). BUT it is really a story about the TRIO, not about tyrosine specifically — arginine and taurine each have their own headers where this same graphic would fit. Better used as a shared eyebrow/opener line inside Concept A than as tyrosine's whole header.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

**There is no maintenance number to justify — state this honestly.** `target.kind = dietary_with_clinical_lever`; the pack carries "no maintenance amount stated (honest gap; blueprint §7.1)." Tyrosine is obtained largely through diet; Wallach states no everyday per-person target.

What the pack DOES have is two **clinical-lever** doses, condition-specific, not maintenance:
- Depression protocol: **l-tyrosine 2 gm three times daily** (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000244`).
- Drug-induced Parkinsonism protocol: **L-tyrosine 100 mg/day** (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000396`).

Honest "why this number" copy would say: *no Wallach daily target — tyrosine is a dietary amino acid; the amounts he names are therapeutic levers for specific conditions (2 g ×3/day in depression; 100 mg/day in Parkinsonism), which is why they differ so widely.* Do NOT present either dose as a maintenance target.

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

**Flags:**
- **Two near-duplicate claims.** `IMMORT-000058` and `DDDL-000066` are the same "add three to the classic list" statement in two books. Treat as ONE idea corroborated twice, not two concepts. Neither carries a number its verbatim omits — both verbatims are clean.
- **No deficiency signs, no maintenance target.** The header cannot lean on "signs of a shortage" or "your daily number."
- **The two protocol doses are 60× apart** and belong to different conditions — never fan them into a single per-day figure (`collective_doses` / dose-fanning risk). They are levers.
- **Concept C is really about the trio,** not tyrosine alone — don't let it become the whole header.

**SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict: MARGINAL — recommend SOLO, on the strength of Concept A only.**
Tyrosine is thin on the usual header fuel (no signs, no target, one signature disease stated as a repeated one-liner). But it has ONE thing most thin elements don't: two independent, book-sourced mechanism claims (`DDDL-000023` thyroid, `EPIGEN-000057` brain) that converge into a genuinely bespoke "one molecule, two organs" figure impossible to tell about any other element. That fork is a real wow and lifts it over the bar for a solo header.
If Luneth would rather group it: the natural home is a **conditionally-essential / added-by-Wallach amino-acids concept page** with **arginine and taurine** — the exact trio of `IMMORT-000058` — where the "three added to the classic list" story is the shared spine and each gets a short panel (tyrosine→goiter, arginine→cancer/PAD, taurine→macular degeneration). That grouping is well-supported and would be the honest fallback if the fork figure doesn't land in mockups.
