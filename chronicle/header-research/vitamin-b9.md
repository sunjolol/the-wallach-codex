# Folate (Vitamin B9) — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 24 sealed claims · vitamin (water-soluble B) · target: 400 mcg/day (Wallach, Epigenetics 2014).

Category is **vitamin** — the shipped vitamin accent (orange family) applies. All facts below trace to a claim ID in the pack; nothing here comes from outside the corpus.

---

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

### Angle 1 — Core identity: the DNA-and-blood vitamin, paired with B12
Folic acid (folate, B9) is **required to synthesise DNA, RNA and red blood cells**, and it works **interdependently with vitamin B12** — a shortage of *either* produces the same **megaloblastic, macrocytic anemia** (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`; anemia also in the deficiency lists `WAL-CLM-LETS-000010`, `WAL-CLM-RARE-000239`). This "one working pair" relationship is the cleanest mechanism in the pack. B12 is also listed in `interacts_with`.

### Angle 2 — The standout mechanism: the birth-defect "fingerprint" (the wow)
Wallach's signature claim: **spina bifida and cleft palate are not true "genetic" defects** but the visible **"fingerprint" of a simple nutritional deficiency** — chiefly folic acid and/or zinc (plus B12) in the mother's diet. He says he *produced* these defects in animals by feeding pregnant mothers deficient diets, and **prevented up to 98% of them by supplying the proper nutrients before conception** (`WAL-CLM-DDDL-000042`). The word "fingerprint" is Wallach's own ("a repeatable 'fingerprint' on a specific gene").
- **Meningocele** (severe anencephaly / spina bifida) — brain or cord exposed because vertebrae form improperly; caused by deficiency of folic acid, B12, or zinc + vitamin A in early pregnancy; **treatment is limited to surgery, so prevention is the goal** (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000359`).
- **Neural tube defects** (hydroencephalocele, spina bifida) named directly under folate deficiency (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`, `WAL-CLM-LETS-000010`).
- **Miscarriage** — usually deficiencies of vitamin A, zinc, **folic acid**, selenium and complete proteins, "essential to maintenance of pregnancy"; baseline program **six months before conception** (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000365`).
- **Pilonidal cyst** — a midline congenital defect linked to zinc, vitamin A, or **folic acid** (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000401`).
- Anencephaly, cleft palate, birth_defects also in `conditions_treated`.

### Angle 3 — The most striking deficiency signs & the malabsorption origin
The deficiency list is long and vivid (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000010`): **megaloblastic anemia, anorexia, apathy, birth defects, GI upset/diarrhea, dyspepsia, fatigue, geographic tongue, growth retardation, headache, insomnia, memory loss, paranoia, vitiligo, weakness.** Shorter corroborating lists: `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041` (impaired cell division, birth defects, failure to thrive, anemia, diarrhea, bleeding gums, weight loss, irritability); `WAL-CLM-RARE-000239` (anemia, poor growth, birth defects).
- **The origin is not just diet — it is malabsorption.** Wallach says folate deficiency "can result from a diet lacking in green leafy vegetables **and from malabsorption problems** (gluten intolerance, celiac disease, leaky gut syndrome, IBS)" (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`). This ties to his whole thesis: the gut that can't absorb is the hidden cause.

### Angle 4 — The signature visible tell: geographic tongue
**Geographic tongue** (benign migratory glossitis) is a **red flag** that you are **not absorbing** B-3, B-2, B-6, B-5, B-12, folic acid, or zinc — "irregular denuded areas on the top and sides of the tongue," usually from celiac-like changes in the small intestine (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000284`). It appears again in the raw deficiency list (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000010`). This is folate's copper-gray-hair equivalent: **a single, map-like sign you can literally see.**

### Angle 5 — Signature protocols & the enormous dose swing
Folate appears in a striking range of therapeutic doses, far above the 400 mcg maintenance target:
- **Canker sores / aphthous stomatitis** — folic acid **5 mg three times daily** (`WAL-CLM-DDDL-000080`, `WAL-CLM-LETS-000143`).
- **Vitiligo** — folic acid **1 mg/day** (the *lowest* therapeutic dose) (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000462`).
- **Epilepsy** — folic acid **15–25 mg/day**, which Wallach says "can also be curative" alongside B6 (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000267`).
- **Gout** — folic acid **10–75 mg/day** (the *widest / highest* range) (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000288`).
- **Crohn's** 5–10 mg t.i.d. (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000238`); **anemia** 15 mg for 20 days (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000137`); **dementia** 3–5 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000243`); **dumping syndrome** 3–5 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000255`); **hepatitis** 5–10 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000298`); **IBS** 5–25 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000323`); **liver disease/cirrhosis** 15–25 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000348`); **psoriasis** 15–25 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000409`); **seborrheic dermatitis/dandruff** 15–25 mg/day (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000430`).
- The spread: **from 400 mcg (nourish) up to 75 mg (treat)** — roughly a **~190-fold** swing within one nutrient.

### Angle 6 — Curio / surprising facts
- **Discovery year 1946.** Wallach's deficiency table prints "Folic acid (Folacin) — 1946" (`WAL-CLM-RARE-000239` verbatim). A dated, factual curio — folate is a relatively late-discovered vitamin.
- **The green-leaf origin.** The dietary source Wallach names is **green leafy vegetables** (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`) — a natural "where it comes from" image. (Note: the corpus does NOT state the folate/foliage etymology — do not claim it.)
- **Curative language.** Wallach uses the word "curative" for folate in epilepsy (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000267`) — unusually strong for a vitamin.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A. "The Fingerprint" — not genetic, a shortage you can prevent
- **Name:** The Fingerprint.
- **The hook:** Spina bifida and cleft palate aren't genetic bad luck — they're the *fingerprint* of a folate (+zinc/B12) shortage in the mother, and Wallach says up to **98%** were prevented by feeding the nutrients *before conception*.
- **Layout shape:** A **reveal / reframe** — a single strong "labeled genetic → actually a repeatable deficiency fingerprint" turn, resolving on the prevention number. No beats-and-quote chassis; the whole block is one argument that lands on 98%.
- **Illustration:** ONE fingerprint graphic, its ridges reading as the focal object, with a single quiet annotation — "a repeatable fingerprint on one gene" (Wallach's own phrase). Fewest elements; no lines crossing the label. (Alternative single figure: a spine motif with one open vertebral gap — but the fingerprint is more distinctive and avoids the clinical spine cliché.)
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000042` ("fingerprint", 98%, prevented before conception); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000359` (meningocele — prevention is the only lever, surgery the only treatment); `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041` / `WAL-CLM-LETS-000010` (neural tube defects / spina bifida in the deficiency list).
- **Why it wows / best UX:** This is the single most Wallach-distinctive, emotionally resonant claim in the pack, and it carries a hard number (98%) with a clear call to action (before conception). Highest impact of the four.

### B. "The Pair" — folate + B12 build the blood together
- **Name:** The Pair.
- **The hook:** Folate builds DNA and red blood cells hand-in-hand with B12 — run short of *either* and the very same anemia appears.
- **Layout shape:** A **two-halves / interlock** composition — folate and B12 shown as two interdependent parts producing one output (a red blood cell), with the shared failure (megaloblastic anemia) as the payoff line. Symmetric two-column, not a stacked beat list.
- **Illustration:** ONE figure of two interlocking pieces (two halves meeting, or two links of a chain) that together yield a single red blood cell; a break in either half → the anemia. One idea, ~3 elements.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041` (DNA/RNA/red-blood-cell synthesis; "works interdependently with B12"; deficiency of either → megaloblastic macrocytic anemia); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000010` (megaloblastic anemia in the sign list); `interacts_with: vitamin-b12`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Teaches the one clean mechanism the corpus fully supports, and explains *why* the two vitamins are always discussed together. Distinct from every shipped header (none is an interdependence-pair).

### C. "Read on the Tongue" — the visible first sign of a gut that can't absorb
- **Name:** Read on the Tongue.
- **The hook:** A folate shortage writes itself on the surface — a map-like tongue — and it usually starts not with a poor diet but with a gut that can't absorb.
- **Layout shape:** A **first-visible-sign** layout (the copper-gray-hair pattern, but its own figure): the striking sign up top, then the surprising cause (malabsorption, not just diet) as the turn.
- **Illustration:** ONE tongue with irregular "geographic" denuded patches — a genuinely map-like single figure, sparsely labeled off the shape (never a line through the labels).
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000284` (geographic tongue = red flag you're not absorbing folate/B-vitamins/zinc; celiac-like small-intestine change); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000010` (geographic tongue in the deficiency list); `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041` (deficiency from malabsorption: gluten intolerance, celiac, leaky gut, IBS — "diet lacking in green leafy vegetables and from malabsorption problems").
- **Why it wows / best UX:** A concrete, memorable, *lookable* sign — the same "first visible tell" instinct that made copper's gray hair work, but a fresh figure. Carries Wallach's absorption thesis in one image.

### D. "One Vitamin, Two Jobs" — the ~190-fold dose swing
- **Name:** One Vitamin, Two Jobs.
- **The hook:** The same nutrient sits at **400 micrograms** to keep you well and climbs to **75 milligrams** to treat disease — one substance spanning nourish-to-pharmacologic.
- **Layout shape:** A single sweeping **scale / ladder** with a small number of labeled stops (not a bar chart) — "nourish" at one end, "treat" at the other, a handful of named conditions between. One axis, ~4–5 ticks, labels set clear of the line.
- **Illustration:** ONE continuous scale line with sparse marked stops: 400 mcg (daily) · vitiligo 1 mg · canker sores 5 mg · epilepsy 15–25 mg · gout up to 75 mg. Keep to ~4 stops to avoid the many-station clutter the playbook forbids.
- **Anchored by:** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000123` (400 mcg daily); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000462` (vitiligo 1 mg/day); `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000080` (canker sores 5 mg t.i.d.); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000267` (epilepsy 15–25 mg/day, "curative"); `WAL-CLM-LETS-000288` (gout 10–75 mg/day).
- **Why it wows / best UX:** The dose spread is objectively surprising and 100% grounded; it reframes "vitamin" as also a therapeutic tool. Risk: must be designed as an elegant scale, not the "plain chart" style Luneth has rejected — flagged so the build session watches for it.

## 3. Proposed lede (PROPOSAL — Luneth ratifies)

**Primary:** "A B-vitamin from green leafy vegetables that builds DNA, RNA and red blood cells alongside vitamin B12 — run short of either one and the very same anemia appears, which is why Wallach treats folate and B12 as one working pair." (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`)

**Alternate (birth-defect leaning, use only if concept A is *not* the lead so it doesn't restate A's beat):** "The green-leaf vitamin the body needs to copy its own DNA — so central to building new cells that Wallach reads a shortage in the mother as spina bifida in the child." (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`, `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000042`)

Both state what folate *is* and sit above "At a glance." The primary avoids restating any single concept's opening beat (it's the mechanism identity, not the fingerprint, tongue, or dose-swing hook).

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

target.kind is **wallach**, a plain number: **400 mcg/day**, drawn straight from Wallach's Epigenetics (2014) daily multiple-vitamin program (`source_claim_id: WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000123`, verbatim "Folic acid 400 mcg"). It is a water-soluble vitamin dosed directly in micrograms, so there is **no IU conversion and no body-weight scaling** — the chain is simply: take Wallach's stated daily-multiple amount, no range to reduce (provenance `original_low` = `upper_taken` = 400), no rounding needed.

Honest note for the tip: Wallach's *earlier* book (Let's Play Doctor, 1995) listed a **1,000 mcg** "true supplement need" for folic acid (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000052`), with 15–20 mg as a 30-day pharmacologic dose. The app uses the **newest** figure (400 mcg, 2014) per favor-newest-Wallach-number. Worth flagging that 400 mcg happens to match the *government RDA* column reprinted in the 1995 table — so the "why this number" should be honest that the maintenance target settled at 400 mcg from his latest program, not present it as a derived computation.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Dose-source split, not a mismatch.** 400 mcg (2014, `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000123`) vs 1,000 mcg (1995, `WAL-CLM-LETS-000052`). Newest wins → 400. No claim_text-vs-verbatim number inflation was found; `WAL-CLM-LETS-000052`'s claim_text carefully explains its three-column figures and matches its verbatim. No superseded_by flags are set on any claim in the pack.
- **No dedicated "what is folate" basics/discovery claim** beyond function. The closest is the "Folacin — 1946" table entry (`WAL-CLM-RARE-000239`) and the green-leaf source line (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`). A richer etymology/history angle is NOT supported — do not invent the folate/foliage word origin; the corpus never states it.
- **Green-leaf source is thin** (single claim, `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`), so a full "where it comes from / source-map" header (calcium-style) would be underpowered on its own. It works only as a supporting detail inside another concept (e.g. the lede, or a corner of concept C).
- **Deep cellular mechanism is stated but not elaborated** — "synthesis of DNA, RNA and red blood cells" is as far as the corpus goes (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000041`). Concept B should not over-claim biochemistry the pack doesn't contain (no methylation/homocysteine detail exists in these claims).
- **Solo header: YES, clearly merited.** 24 claims, a distinctive birth-defect thesis with a hard number, a clean B12-pair mechanism, a lookable first sign (geographic tongue), and a real dose story. This element does not need to fold into another.
- **A concept I wished for but the corpus doesn't support:** a "methyl donor / gene-switch" mechanism header (folate as the epigenetic methyl source). The claims tie folate to birth defects as a *deficiency fingerprint* but never describe DNA methylation or gene-switching as folate's mechanism — so that (real-world) angle must NOT be built.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**A — "The Fingerprint."** It is the most Wallach-distinctive claim in the pack, emotionally resonant, carries a hard grounded number (98% prevented before conception, `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000042`), and delivers a clear takeaway a skim-reader keeps. **C ("Read on the Tongue")** is the strong runner-up and the safest single-figure build (a fresh copper-style first-visible-sign); pair A's reframe with C's tongue figure if a mix is wanted.
