# Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin) — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 12 sealed claims · vitamin (orange) · target: 50 mg/day (upper of Wallach's 10–50 mg Epigenetics range; his Base Line program lists the same 50 mg maintenance "true supplement need").

---

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity / what it does (mechanism).**
Riboflavin is a cofactor for the flavin coenzymes — flavin mononucleotide (FMN) and flavin adenine dinucleotide (FAD) — needed for oxidation-reduction and energy production, helps convert tryptophan to niacin, and maintains healthy skin, mucous membranes, the cornea, and nerve sheaths. `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`.
⚠ CAUTION (see Gaps): every mechanism fact in this angle lives only in that claim's **claim_text**. Its **verbatim** excerpt is ONLY the deficiency list — the FMN/FAD / tryptophan→niacin / cornea-nerve-sheath text is not in the quotable span. State the mechanism, but the pull-quote for this claim is the deficiency list, not the mechanism.

**The tongue is the dashboard (the signature, riboflavin-specific "first visible sign").**
Deficiency rewrites the tongue vividly and specifically: **magenta tongue** and **geographic tongue** — "erosions and swelling of tongue ('geographic' tongue)" — plus soreness/burning. `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`, `WAL-CLM-RARE-000234` (both give magenta + geographic tongue), `WAL-CLM-LETS-000029` (geographic tongue). Geographic tongue = "irregular denuded areas on the top and sides of the tongue" — a map-like pattern, which is literally why it's called *geographic*. `WAL-CLM-LETS-000284`.

**The mouth corners crack (cheilosis).**
Cheilosis = cracks at the corners of the mouth and nostrils / nasolabial folds; angular stomatitis; burning lips. Wallach states plainly: "Cheilosis (angular stomatitis) is the result of a vitamin B-2 (riboflavin) deficiency." `WAL-CLM-LETS-000214` (dose: B-2 **75 mg three times daily**), `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`, `WAL-CLM-RARE-000234`, `WAL-CLM-LETS-000029` (cheilosis).

**The eyes / light (the cornea angle).**
Riboflavin maintains the cornea; a shortage brings **photophobia** (light sensitivity), **lacrimation** (tearing), "capillary 'injection' of cornea" (bloodshot cornea), blurred vision, itching/burning/red eyes, and **cataracts**. `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036` (cornea), `WAL-CLM-LETS-000029` (blurred vision, cataracts, photophobia, eyes), `WAL-CLM-RARE-000234` (photophobia, lacrimation, capillary injection). Cataract = "a clouding of the eye's lens … the most common cause of blindness in older people." `WAL-CLM-LETS-000207`.

**The dose gap (the number story).**
Wallach's Base Line row prints three figures in column order — the government RDA, his maintenance "true supplement need," then his 30-day pharmacologic dose: "RIBOFLAVIN **1.6 mg  50 mg  200 to 500 mg**." The 1.6 mg is the RDA, reprinted only to argue against it — never his recommendation. `WAL-CLM-LETS-000064`. His Epigenetics daily-multiple gives a **10–50 mg** range. `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000113`. So: government ~1.6 mg → Wallach maintenance 50 mg (~30×) → short-term corrective 200–500 mg.

**Cystic fibrosis (the selenium-trio curio).**
Wallach's theory: cystic fibrosis can be an *acquired environmental disease* produced by a deficiency of **selenium, zinc, and riboflavin**, worsened by diets low in vitamin E and high in polyunsaturated fatty acids. `WAL-CLM-RARE-000010`. (Ties riboflavin to the same disease anchoring the shipped selenium header.)

**Malabsorption red flag (the B-complex family angle).**
Geographic tongue, "like allergic 'shiners,' is a red flag that you are not absorbing vitamin B-3, B-2, B-6, B-5, B-12, folic acid, or zinc" — usually from celiac-disease-like changes in the small intestine. `WAL-CLM-LETS-000284`. The sign isn't just *low intake* — it's *failed absorption* of the whole B-family.

**Where it shows up in protocols (breadth, mostly as a B-complex team member).**
B-2 appears in Wallach's programs for: anemia (50 mg twice daily) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000137`; anxiety/panic (50 mg t.i.d.; panic attacks hit women twice as often as men) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000142`; cataracts (50 mg b.i.d.) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000207`; depression (50 mg t.i.d.; Wallach holds food allergies + hypoglycemia + PMS ≈ 90% of diagnosed depression) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000244`; cheilosis (75 mg t.i.d. — the one B-2-*solo* protocol) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000214`; geographic tongue (via B-complex IM + baseline) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000284`.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A. "Read the tongue" — the signature first-visible-sign
- **Name:** Read the tongue.
- **The hook:** A riboflavin shortage rewrites the tongue — it turns *magenta* and grows a *map-like* pattern of denuded patches (that's literally why doctors call it "geographic"). The tongue is riboflavin's dashboard.
- **Layout shape:** A single hero illustration (one organ, centred) with two short callouts, opening into a one-line "why the tongue" note. No beats, no chassis.
- **Illustration:** ONE tongue silhouette (an organ shape, deliberately NOT a face) tinted magenta, with a few irregular "geographic" map-patch outlines on its surface; two clean labels routed to empty margin — "magenta" and "map-like patches ('geographic tongue')." One idea, ~4 marks.
- **Anchored by:** magenta + geographic tongue `WAL-CLM-RARE-000234`, `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`; geographic tongue `WAL-CLM-LETS-000029`; "irregular denuded areas on the top and sides of the tongue" `WAL-CLM-LETS-000284`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** The single most distinctive, unmistakable, *specific-to-riboflavin* sign in the corpus — no other shipped header owns the tongue, and copper's "first visible sign" was hair, so this is a fresh figure in the same beloved genre. A skim-reader instantly gets a body-reading they can check in a mirror.

### B. "1.6 → 50 → 500" — the government-vs-Wallach dose gap
- **Name:** The dose gap.
- **The hook:** The government's number is 1.6 mg. Wallach's is 50 mg to maintain and 200–500 mg to correct — roughly thirty times the official figure.
- **Layout shape:** A three-tier proportional ladder (RDA → maintenance → corrective), each tier a labelled band, closing on a one-line reading. Not a plain axis chart — a stepped magnitude stack where the *size jump itself* is the message.
- **Illustration:** Three stacked/scaled tiers sized to their numbers so the 1.6 mg tier reads as a sliver against the 50 mg and 200–500 mg tiers. Labels sit inside each tier, never crossed by a rule.
- **Anchored by:** "RIBOFLAVIN 1.6 mg 50 mg 200 to 500 mg" — RDA / true supplement need / 30-day pharmacologic `WAL-CLM-LETS-000064`; the 10–50 mg daily-multiple range `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000113`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Cleanest number grounding in the whole pack and it *is* the "why this number" story made visual — the header and the target-provenance tip reinforce each other. The 30× gap is a genuine "wait, what?" beat.

### C. "What it becomes" — riboflavin → the cell's energy coenzymes
- **Name:** What it becomes.
- **The hook:** Riboflavin's real job is invisible: the vitamin *becomes* the flavin coenzymes (FMN and FAD) that run oxidation-reduction and energy in every cell — and it turns tryptophan into niacin.
- **Layout shape:** A single left-to-right transformation figure with one side-branch; a short "so a shortage burns the linings that turn over fastest" bridge line under it. Few nodes.
- **Illustration:** riboflavin → FMN / FAD → "energy + redox" as three linked nodes, with a small side-arrow "tryptophan → niacin." Maximum ~5 nodes/arrows; arrows routed clear of every label.
- **Anchored by:** cofactor for FMN + FAD, oxidation-reduction and energy production, converts tryptophan to niacin, maintains skin/mucous membranes/cornea/nerve sheaths — `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Answers "what does it actually DO," which the sign-based concepts skip. ⚠ Weakness: rests on **claim_text only** — the quotable verbatim for this claim is the deficiency list, so a designer cannot pull a mechanism quote by ID (see Gaps). Strong as a stated-mechanism figure, weak if the design wants a mechanism pull-quote.

### D. "Light hurts" — the cornea / eyes angle
- **Name:** Light hurts.
- **The hook:** Riboflavin guards the cornea; run short and the eyes go light-shy and bloodshot — photophobia, tearing, blurred vision, and, over time, the clouded lens of a cataract.
- **Layout shape:** A single eye figure with a small "signs" cluster to one side and a closing line naming the long-run stake (cataract = the most common cause of blindness in the old). Distinct from A's tongue and C's flow.
- **Illustration:** One eye with a few light rays angled toward it (the "photophobia" idea) and a faint bloodshot cornea tint; 2–3 margin labels — "photophobia," "tearing," "bloodshot cornea." Light rays routed to the eye, never through the labels.
- **Anchored by:** maintains the cornea `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`; photophobia / lacrimation / capillary "injection" of cornea `WAL-CLM-RARE-000234`; blurred vision + cataracts + photophobia `WAL-CLM-LETS-000029`; cataract = clouding of the lens, "most common cause of blindness in older people" `WAL-CLM-LETS-000207`.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** A second vivid, checkable body-reading distinct from the tongue, with a real long-term stake (blindness) to land the "why care." Pairs the everyday sign (light sensitivity) with the dramatic endpoint.

*(A, B, C, D are distinct in BOTH layout and figure: single-organ tongue · proportional dose ladder · transformation flow · single-eye light figure. None is the selenium chassis.)*

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

Daily target **50 mg** (`target.kind = wallach`). It is the **upper end** of the 10–50 mg range Wallach lists for B2 in his Epigenetics daily multiple supplement program (`source_claim_id WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000113`; provenance `original_low 10 → original_high 50 → upper_taken 50`). No IU conversion and no body-weight scale apply — a milligram vitamin taken straight at the top of Wallach's own range. It is independently corroborated: his Base Line Nutritional Supplement Program lists the identical **50 mg** as riboflavin's maintenance "true supplement need" (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000064`), where the same row prints the government RDA at just **1.6 mg** (reprinted only to argue against) and a 30-day corrective dose of **200–500 mg**. So 50 mg is Wallach's maintenance figure — about thirty times the RDA, and well below his short-term therapeutic ceiling.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **⚠ Mechanism lives in claim_text, not verbatim (`WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000036`).** The FMN/FAD cofactor role, oxidation-reduction/energy, tryptophan→niacin, and "maintains cornea/nerve sheaths" are all in the **claim_text**; the sealed **verbatim** excerpt is ONLY the deficiency list ("Riboflavin-deficiency health problems include: …"). Per the trust-the-verbatim rule, a header may STATE the mechanism (it's a sealed claim) but there is **no mechanism verbatim to pull by ID** — so Concept C cannot show a mechanism quote, only the deficiency list. This is the single most important flag for a designer.
- **No "riboflavin is yellow" fact.** A colour-of-the-vitamin angle would strengthen the magenta-tongue concept, but the pack never states riboflavin's own colour (only *magenta tongue* as a sign). Do NOT inject the yellow-pigment/"flavus" fact — it is world knowledge, not in the corpus. Flagged as wished-for-but-absent.
- **No narrative anecdote / patient story.** Every protocol claim is a dose list, not a story. The closest thing to a "did-you-know curio" is the cystic-fibrosis selenium-trio (`WAL-CLM-RARE-000010`); there is no biography/discovery/history material for a curio-driven header.
- **claim_text vs verbatim numbers: clean.** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000064` claim_text (1.6 / 50 / 200–500) matches its verbatim exactly, including the correct column-order explanation. No number mismatch anywhere in the pack. No superseded claims (all `superseded_by: null`).
- **B-2 is usually a team member, not a soloist.** In anemia/anxiety/cataract/depression protocols B-2 is one of a dozen items — good for a "B-complex family" note, weak as a standalone protocol beat. The one B-2-solo protocol is cheilosis at 75 mg t.i.d. (`WAL-CLM-LETS-000214`) — a dose that exceeds the 50 mg maintenance target and is worth a footnote if a dose figure appears.
- **Solo header: YES.** 12 claims, a distinctive and specific sign cluster (tongue + mouth + eyes), a clean 30× dose story, and a stated mechanism. Riboflavin comfortably merits a bespoke solo header; no reason to fold it into another element. (Its many interactions are almost all the B-complex family — a possible cross-link, not a merge.)
