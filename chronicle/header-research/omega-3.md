# Omega-3 — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 92 sealed claims · fatty acid (essential) · target: SHARED essential-fatty-acid budget, no solo omega-3 number (wallach_collective, 9 g/day for the EFA group · WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity — one of only TWO fats the body cannot build.**
Of all the fats, Wallach names only two as classically essential — alpha-linolenic acid (the omega-3) and linoleic acid (an omega-6) — because the body cannot manufacture them and must take them in from the diet (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053). He counts omega-3 among the body's 90 essential nutrients (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197). Three polyunsaturated fats — linoleic, linolenic and arachidonic — are *grouped* as "essential fatty acids," but only two are genuinely essential, because the body can build arachidonic acid itself from linoleic (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000063, WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053). Frame in his words: these are "far more than fuel" — structural raw material for cell membranes and prostaglandins (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197).

**The name is a literal address — the third carbon.**
The "omega" number marks where the fat's first double bond sits, counted from the "tail" or methyl end of the chain; in an omega-3 that first double bond is at the **third carbon** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204). Omega-6 and omega-9 are named the same way for bonds farther along; the longer-chain omega-3s DHA and EPA belong to the same family (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204). This is the single most omega-3-specific, most under-known fact in the pack, and no shipped header uses molecular structure.

**Not fuel — the raw material for the body's local hormones.**
EFAs are the raw material the body uses to manufacture **prostaglandins**, which help regulate blood pressure, heart rate, vascular dilation, blood clotting, bronchial (airway) dilation, and central-nervous-system (brain and spinal cord) function (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000064, WAL-CLM-RARE-000110). They also maintain cell-wall integrity and lower serum cholesterol (WAL-CLM-RARE-000110). Wallach widens this to a whole signaling family: eicosanoids, endocannabinoids (mood, behavior, inflammation), lipoxins (from omega-6) and **resolvins from omega-3** that downregulate inflammation (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000399).

**The balance: omega-3 vs omega-6.**
Every EFA function requires a proper **ratio** between the omega-3 and omega-6 forms (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398). His model of that balance is the traditional/tribal diet, which contained **nearly equal amounts** of omega-3 and omega-6, with only about **4% of calories** from polyunsaturated oils (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000202, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398).

**Deficiency — the body shows it first on the surface.**
"Dry skin means essential fatty acids" — dry skin is cosmetically minor but signals a potentially serious EFA deficiency that can lead to cardiovascular disease (WAL-CLM-LETS-000254). The full deficiency map (Let's Play Doctor Table 6-1) lists: acne, alopecia, arthritis, atrophy of the endocrine glands, diarrhea, dry brittle hair, eczema, endocrine dysfunction, fatty degeneration of the liver, gallstones, growth retardation, immunologic dysfunction, impaired wound healing, infertility, kidney dysfunction, a positive sweat test, and xerosis (WAL-CLM-LETS-000011). In infants specifically: poor growth, eczema, and lowered resistance to infection (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000065, WAL-CLM-RARE-000111) — and, per the Immortality list, lowered IQ, seizures and learning disabilities (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053). At the deeper end, deficiency produces thrombosis (stroke, coronary thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, DVT), skin disease, respiratory disease, and depression — with low omega-3 (DHA) tied to increased **depression and suicide** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000059).

**Signature theses & standout results.**
- Cystic fibrosis is, in Wallach's view, a selenium **and** essential-fatty-acid deficiency in the fetus/newborn; the "sweat test" turns positive over the first months of life *as the infant loses the essential fatty acids* the mother provided (WAL-CLM-LETS-000471, WAL-CLM-LETS-000240). NOTE: this is shared with selenium (see Gaps).
- Multiple sclerosis: the myelin sheath is made almost exclusively of cholesterol; high fish oil (cod-liver/salmon) plus flaxseed oil is said to raise survival by 50% (WAL-CLM-LETS-000371).
- Alzheimer's: DHA and EPA omega-3 support the neurotransmitters of memory; a Johns Hopkins double-blind 10-year study on ~5,000 people over 65 (published 2004) showed diet + supplementation can cut Alzheimer's risk by **78%** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000016).
- EPA in fish/flax oil raises protective HDL cholesterol; refined sugar drives HDL down (WAL-CLM-LETS-000488).

**Diagnostic curio.**
Elevated **hair** levels of sodium, potassium and chloride indicate an EFA deficiency — the skin glands malfunction and lose electrolytes instead of conserving them; the false "elevations" normalize once minerals and EFAs are restored (WAL-CLM-RARE-000334).

**History curio ("vitamin F").**
The two essential fatty acids were identified in **1923** and originally listed as "**vitamin F**"; in **1929** rat studies showed they were more properly classed as fats than vitamins, so the label was dropped (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000397).

**Astonishing reach (the materia-medica breadth).**
~50 of the 92 claims are Let's Play Doctor Ch10 protocol entries where omega-3 (usually "essential fatty acids 5 g t.i.d." or flaxseed/salmon oil) appears as one line of a larger regimen — spanning ~90 conditions from acne to stroke, cardiomyopathy, PMS, depression, psoriasis, brittle nails, hangnails (WAL-CLM-LETS-000011 anchors the deficiency spine; representative protocols WAL-CLM-LETS-000123, -000204, -000254, -000291, -000371, -000402). The breadth is real but individually thin — see Gaps.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A — "Count to three" (the name is a map)
- **Name** — The Third Carbon.
- **The hook** — The "3" in omega-3 is a literal address: the first kink in the fat sits three carbons from the tail, and that exact spot is one the body can't build — so it has to come from the diet.
- **Layout shape** — A single annotated molecule figure as the centrepiece, with a short pivot line beneath it turning the fact into meaning ("a bond the body can't make → must be eaten"). No beats, no big-number stat, no pull quote — one figure carrying the whole idea.
- **Illustration** — ONE clean zig-zag carbon chain. Mark the methyl/"tail" end, count the first three carbons, and highlight the double bond at carbon 3 (the one accent). Everything else de-emphasised. Few elements; the count must NOT run a line through the "3" label — set the number beside the bond, not on the stroke.
- **Anchored by** — first double bond at the third carbon, counted from the tail/methyl end (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204); omega-3 = alpha-linolenic, one of only two the body cannot synthesize (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, WAL-CLM-RARE-000109); DHA/EPA in the same family (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Answers a question almost everyone has silently carried ("why is it *called* omega-3?") and no other essential's header can. Purely structural, so it is maximally distinct from all six shipped headers. A single figure = clean, rule-friendly, hard to clutter.

### B — "Two of the ninety" (what actually counts as essential)
- **Name** — The Only Two.
- **The hook** — Of all the fats you eat, only two are truly essential — and omega-3 is one of them; even the fat usually grouped alongside them isn't, because the body makes it itself.
- **Layout shape** — A "where it sits" comparison: omega-3 and omega-6 held up as the essential pair, with arachidonic acid shown and then struck through ("the body builds this one"). A tight caption ties it to the count of 90 essentials. Trade-off/tile archetype — not a beats chassis.
- **Illustration** — Three simple fat tiles: two lit (omega-3 ALA, omega-6 linoleic), one dimmed/struck (arachidonic) with a tiny "made from linoleic" tag. One idea, three elements.
- **Anchored by** — only two classically essential: ALA (omega-3) + linoleic (omega-6) (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197); three are *grouped* as EFA but arachidonic is built from linoleic, so only two are strictly essential (WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-DDDL-000063, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053); omega-3 among the 90 essentials (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Corrects a real misconception (people assume "essential fats" is a long list) and the strike-through of arachidonic is a genuine "huh" beat. Distinct visual grammar (comparison tiles) from A, C and D.

### C — "Not fuel — a factory"
- **Name** — Raw Material.
- **The hook** — Omega-3 isn't burned for energy; it's the stock the body turns into the local hormones that quietly set blood pressure, clotting, airway width and the nervous system.
- **Layout shape** — A single input-to-outputs transformation: one raw-material block becoming a small, tight set of governed body functions. One figure, a one-line frame above it ("far more than fuel"), nothing else.
- **Illustration** — ONE fat block → a prostaglandin node → **four** labelled dials only (blood pressure · clotting · airways · nerves). Resist adding more; keep it a switchboard, not a station map. Route no connector through a label.
- **Anchored by** — EFAs are the raw material for prostaglandins that regulate blood pressure, heart rate, vascular + bronchial dilation, blood clotting and CNS (brain/spinal cord) function (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000064, WAL-CLM-RARE-000110); resolvins from omega-3 downregulate inflammation (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000399); "far more than fuel," maintains membranes, lowers cholesterol (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, WAL-CLM-RARE-000110).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Reframes "a fat" as a control system — the single most counter-intuitive idea in the pack. Highest clutter risk of the four (flag: cap outputs at four).

### D — "Level again" (the balance that modern diets lost)
- **Name** — The Balance.
- **The hook** — Omega-3 only works in balance with omega-6, and the diet we evolved on kept them nearly equal — a balance modern eating has tipped.
- **Layout shape** — A balance-beam figure as the hero, captioned by the traditional-diet numbers; a short line names why balance matters (every EFA function depends on the ratio). Iconic-object archetype.
- **Illustration** — ONE balance scale: omega-3 pan vs omega-6 pan, sitting level, tagged "traditional diet — nearly equal." A single small note: "only ~4% of those calories were polyunsaturated." Two pans, one beam — few elements.
- **Anchored by** — all EFA functions require a proper omega-3:omega-6 ratio (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398); traditional/tribal diets held nearly equal omega-3 and omega-6, ~4% of calories from PUFAs (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000202).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — The balance scale is the most visually distinct shape of the four and instantly legible. UX caveat: it is inherently a *pair* story, so it must be built to complement (not duplicate) the omega-6 header — see Gaps.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

Honest situation: **there is no omega-3-only target.** `target.kind` is `wallach_collective` — omega-3 shares a single essential-fatty-acid budget with omega-6. The source is WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115: EFAs "should be consumed at the rate of 3 percent of your total daily calorie consumption or supplemented at the rate of 9 grams per day in capsule form." That 9 g/day is **one combined amount for the essential fatty acids together — not 9 g of omega-3 and 9 g of omega-6**. So the coverage figure shown against omega-3 is a shared group budget (3% of daily calories, or 9 g/day supplemented), and the header should say so plainly rather than imply a per-element dose. No IU factor, body-weight scale, or rounding applies here — it is a flat, collective amount stated directly by Wallach.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Collective target, not a solo number.** The 9 g/day belongs to the EFA group (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115); omega-3 has no independent Wallach dose. The "why this number" must present a shared budget, never a fabricated per-element figure. Consistent with the settled position that the EFA collective dose is a singleton on DDDL-000115.

- **"Three EFAs" vs "two essential" — handle carefully.** The pack lists three fatty acids as EFA (linoleic, linolenic, arachidonic — WAL-CLM-DDDL-000063, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000394) and WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197's claim_text even says he "counts three essential fatty acids among the 90," yet the same claim and WAL-CLM-RARE-000109 / WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053 state only **two** are genuinely essential (arachidonic is built from linoleic). Do not print "three essential" flatly. Concept B turns this exact nuance into its hook — the safest way to use it.

- **Omega-9 slotting is an outlier.** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000394 is the one place Wallach groups omega-9 in and lists omega-3 as "DHA and EPA"; it runs against his dominant two-essential stance. Do not build on the omega-9 grouping.

- **Cystic fibrosis overlaps selenium's shipped header.** WAL-CLM-LETS-000471 / -000240 frame CF as a selenium **and** EFA deficiency, but the shipped **selenium** header already owns cystic fibrosis + Keshan. Do not make CF the omega-3 figure — it would echo selenium. The genuinely EFA-specific sliver (the sweat test turning positive *as the newborn loses its fatty acids*) could be a single supporting line at most, not the concept.

- **Omega-3 and omega-6 share most of this pack.** Many claims are joint EFA claims; the omega-6 header will draw from a near-identical palette (and the balance/ratio material, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398 / -000202, belongs to both). Recommend deliberate differentiation: give omega-3 the "3"/third-carbon naming (A), the depression/brain + resolvins angle, and the deficiency-surface story; let omega-6 own lipoxins. If concept D (balance) is chosen, coordinate so the two headers complement rather than duplicate.

- **Materia-medica breadth is wide but thin.** ~50 near-identical "EFA 5 g t.i.d." protocol lines across ~90 conditions. Their collective reach is striking, but a list-everything figure would be the exact cluttered-diagram failure the rules forbid. Use WAL-CLM-LETS-000011 (the deficiency map) as the spine if reach is wanted, not a 50-item fan.

- **Strong alternate concepts the corpus also supports** (offered if any of A–D is rejected): (i) **"Dry skin is the first tell"** — a first-visible-sign header parallel to copper's premature-gray, grounded in WAL-CLM-LETS-000254 + WAL-CLM-LETS-000011; (ii) **"The brain fat"** — low omega-3/DHA tied to depression and suicide (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000059) + the Alzheimer's 78% Johns Hopkins result (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000016); (iii) **"Vitamin F, 1923"** — the did-you-know history curio (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000397), best as a small secondary beat rather than a whole header.

- **Solo header: warranted.** Omega-3 is a canon essential with distinctive, self-supporting material (naming, essentiality, prostaglandin function, deficiency map, brain link). It merits its own bespoke header — but should be designed as a deliberate PAIR-complement to omega-6, given the shared dose and shared ratio story.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**A — "Count to three."** It is the one angle unique to omega-3 (no other essential and no shipped header can use it), it answers the universal unspoken question "why is it called omega-3?", it grounds cleanly and entirely in WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204 + WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, and a single annotated molecule is the most clutter-resistant figure of the four. Pair it with the essentiality payoff (one of only two the body can't build) and lede #2.
