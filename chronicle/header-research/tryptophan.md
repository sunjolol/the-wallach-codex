# Tryptophan — header research dossier

> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 7 sealed claims · amino acid · target: no Wallach maintenance amount stated (honest gap; dietary_with_clinical_lever) · solo-header: marginal.

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Angle A — the precursor / raw-material story (the ONE substantive angle; the rest is dosing).**
- Tryptophan is a precursor of **niacin (vitamin B3)** and **serotonin** — stated verbatim in Wallach's table of classic essential amino acids "the body cannot make for itself and must take in from the diet each day." `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067` (verbatim: "Tryptophan precursor of niacin and serotonin").
- Same fact restated, plus that serotonin "can be converted to the sleep hormone melatonin" — but ⚠ **melatonin appears ONLY in the claim_text, NOT in the verbatim** of `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056`. The verbatim there covers absorption→depression and "sold as sleep aids" only. Treat serotonin + niacin as verbatim-solid; treat the melatonin biochemistry as a claim_text gloss, not a quotable fact.
- Its metabolite 5-HTP "has been suggested for seizure disorders and depression" — claim_text of `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056`; also NOT in that claim's verbatim. Gloss only.

**Angle B — the gut-absorption gate (a genuine, distinct mechanism, verbatim-solid).**
- "fructose malabsorption, lactose intolerance, and gluten intolerance cause a reduced state of tryptophan absorption from the intestine, therefore causing reduced levels of tryptophan in the blood and clinical depression." `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056` (verbatim). Sleep-aid use: "Tryptophan supplements are sold as sleep aids" (same verbatim).

**Angle C — the clinical dosing lever (5 protocols; tryptophan is one ingredient among many, but the dose SPREAD is striking).** Across conditions Wallach's L-tryptophan dose ranges over 20×:
- Anxiety / panic: **L-tryptophan 10 grams three times daily** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000142` (verbatim).
- Depression (incl. manic): **l-tryptophan 2 gm three times daily** (with dl-phenylalanine + l-tyrosine each 2 gm t.i.d.) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000244` (verbatim).
- Raynaud's disease: **L-tryptophan 500 mg three times daily** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000415` (verbatim).
- Insomnia: **L-tryptophan 1,000 mg three times daily** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000322` (verbatim).
- Inflammation: **L-tryptophan 1.5 grams per day** (with D-phenylalanine + DL-valine) `WAL-CLM-LETS-000320` (claim_text; the sealed verbatim for this claim stops before the tryptophan line — see flag §5).

No deficiency signs recorded. Conditions treated: anxiety, depression, inflammation, insomnia, Raynaud's. Books: dddl-3e-2011, epigenetics, lets-play-doctor.

## 2. Header concepts (honestly, the material supports ONE strong + ONE supporting)

### Concept 1 — "One amino acid, three destinations" (the precursor fork) — LEAD
- **The hook.** Tryptophan is not a destination, it is a *source*. The body cannot make it, yet from this one dietary amino acid it builds serotonin (mood), niacin/B3 (a whole vitamin), and — the sleep connection Wallach flags — a sleep aid. The wow is that a single molecule you must eat becomes three unrelated-looking things.
- **Layout shape.** An annotated illustration as the centrepiece, with a one-line lede-pivot above it and nothing forced beneath — no beats, no big stat, no pull-quote required. Let the figure carry the whole block.
- **Illustration (one idea, fewest elements).** A single input node "TRYPTOPHAN (must come from diet)" branching to exactly TWO clean labelled endpoints — **SEROTONIN** (mood) and **NIACIN · vitamin B3**. Two branches only, because those are the two the verbatim supports. Route each arrow so it never crosses the other's label. Do NOT draw the melatonin/5-HTP branch as fact (claim_text-only); if it appears at all it is a faint dotted "→ sleep aid" tail off serotonin, captioned as use not biochemistry.
- **Anchored by.** `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067` (verbatim: "Tryptophan precursor of niacin and serotonin") is the spine; `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056` supplies the "must come from diet / sold as sleep aids" framing.
- **Why it wows / best UX.** It is the only genuinely bespoke, non-template shape the pack supports, and it teaches the single most memorable fact about tryptophan in one glance. A branching diagram is instantly legible and impossible to confuse with any mineral header.

### Concept 2 — "The gut can starve your mood" (the absorption gate) — SUPPORTING / alt
- **The hook.** You can eat enough tryptophan and still run low — because fructose malabsorption, lactose intolerance, and gluten intolerance throttle its absorption, dropping blood levels and, in Wallach's reading, producing clinical depression. The lever is the gut, not the plate.
- **Layout shape.** A short cause-chain read left-to-right: three gut conditions → a narrowed "absorption" gate → low blood tryptophan → depression. One line of lede above; no quote block needed.
- **Illustration.** A simple pinch/gate: three intolerance labels feeding an intestinal passage that visibly narrows, one arrow out the far side to "low blood tryptophan → depression." One idea, no crossing strokes through labels.
- **Anchored by.** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056` (verbatim, entirely).
- **Why it wows / best UX.** It is counter-intuitive (intake ≠ level) and it is 100% inside one verbatim, so it is the safest-grounded concept. Weaker as a *header* only because it is a single linear fact — better as a supporting beat inside Concept 1 than as the whole block.

### (Not proposed) Concept from the dose spread
The 500 mg → 10 g t.i.d. range is visually tempting, but tryptophan is one ingredient inside multi-item protocols there, not the subject, and turning it into a header stat would over-elevate a supporting role. Noted, not recommended.

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

**Grounding flags (must respect at design time):**
- **Melatonin & 5-HTP are claim_text-only.** `WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000056`'s verbatim does NOT contain "melatonin" or "5-hydroxytryptophan." Do not render either as a Wallach fact/quote. Serotonin + niacin ARE in verbatim (`WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067`).
- **Inflammation dose is claim_text-only.** `WAL-CLM-LETS-000320`'s sealed verbatim stops at "zinc at 50 mg t.i.d" and never reaches the tryptophan line; the "1.5 grams per day" lives only in claim_text. Prefer the four verbatim-backed doses (anxiety/depression/insomnia/Raynaud's) for any figure.
- **No deficiency signs** in the pack — a "deficiency" beat would have to be invented. Don't.
- Two protocol claims (`LETS-000142`, `LETS-000244`, `LETS-000322`, `LETS-000415`) have `dose: null` at the claim level even though the verbatim carries the number inline; pull doses by reading the verbatim, not the `dose` field.

**Solo-vs-group verdict: MARGINAL — a minimal bespoke solo header is justified, but only just.**
The pack is thin in *subject* claims: only 2 of 7 claims are genuinely ABOUT tryptophan (the two precursor claims); the other 5 are protocols where it is one line among many. BUT those 2 claims yield one genuinely distinct, non-template, wow-capable illustration (the precursor fork) plus a solid supporting mechanism (the absorption gate). That is enough for a single-figure minimal header — no beats, no forced quote — and it will not read as padded.
- If Luneth prefers to consolidate, the natural home is a **"classic essential amino acids"** concept page grouping tryptophan with **phenylalanine, methionine, and tyrosine** — they share `WAL-CLM-DDDL-000067`'s precursor table verbatim and co-occur in the same depression/inflammation protocols. Tryptophan would be the strongest single tile in that group.
- It should NOT get a first-tier bespoke header on the scale of selenium/copper; the material cannot fill one honestly.
