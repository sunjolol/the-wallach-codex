# Niacin — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 25 sealed claims · vitamin (B3) · target: 100 mg/day (upper of Wallach's 10–100 mg Epigenetics daily-multiple range).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

### Core identity / mechanism — the coenzyme that releases energy from ALL food
Niacin (vitamin B3) is the working part of the coenzymes **NAD (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide)** and **NADP (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide phosphate)**, which drive the **oxidation-reduction reactions that release energy from carbohydrates, fats, and proteins** — all three macronutrients (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037). This is the single mechanism claim and it is strong: niacin sits at the metabolic centre where every food group is burned for energy. No other claim in the pack restates the biochemistry, so EPIGEN-000037 is the sole anchor for the "what it does" angle.

### The standout deficiency — pellagra and the "three Ds"
The signature deficiency picture is **pellagra**, which Wallach frames with the classic mnemonic the **"three Ds": diarrhea, dermatitis, dementia** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037; restated WAL-CLM-RARE-000235 as "dermatitis, diarrhea, dementia and retardation"). This is the most memorable, most uniquely-niacin fact in the whole pack — a single deficiency that attacks the gut, the skin, and the mind at once.

### Deficiency signs — a long, vivid list (gut + skin + mind)
Two big deficiency tables plus the pellagra claim converge:
- **The vivid physical markers:** "**beef tongue**" — a swollen, sore, red tongue (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037, WAL-CLM-RARE-000235); **scaly, itchy / dark-pigmented dermatitis** and **skin pigmentation** (EPIGEN-000037, RARE-000235, WAL-CLM-LETS-000019); **diarrhea** (RARE-000235); **muscular weakness** and **anorexia / loss of appetite** (all three claims).
- **The mental/neuro markers (large cluster):** confusion / mental confusion, depression, crying jags & emotional lability, fatigue, headache, insomnia, irritability, memory loss / poor memory (WAL-CLM-LETS-000019). Plus canker sores, nausea, halitosis (bad breath), dyspepsia, limb pains, skin eruptions / eczema (LETS-000019). The deficiency map for niacin is unusually broad — it reads as a whole-body-and-mind collapse.

### The therapeutic workhorse — one dose Wallach reaches for again and again
Across the materia-medica protocols, **time-release niacin at 450 mg** (three or four times daily) is Wallach's recurring therapeutic dose — it appears in a striking number of conditions:
- anxiety / panic (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000078, WAL-CLM-LETS-000142), arthritis / rheumatoid arthritis (WAL-CLM-LETS-000146), bipolar disorder (WAL-CLM-LETS-000159, 450 mg q.i.d.), cataracts (WAL-CLM-LETS-000207), high cholesterol (WAL-CLM-LETS-000219), dementia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000243, 450 mg q.i.d.), dysmenorrhea (WAL-CLM-LETS-000258, 50–100 mg t.i.d.), headache (WAL-CLM-LETS-000293, 450 mg time-release q.i.d.), hysteria (WAL-CLM-LETS-000310), insomnia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000322, incl. niacinamide 1,000 mg at bedtime), organic brain syndrome (WAL-CLM-LETS-000388), palpitations (WAL-CLM-LETS-000394), tachycardia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000443), tardive dyskinesia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000445).
- **Almost always specified as time-release** — that qualifier recurs in nearly every protocol (a design hook: WHY time-release ties to the flush, below).

### The mind vitamin — "organic brain syndrome"
Wallach's umbrella term **"organic brain syndrome"** groups a set of nutritionally-driven mental/behavioral disorders — pellagra, alcoholism, dementia, depression, hyperkinesis, hypoglycemia, diabetes, PMS, paranoia, schizophrenia, learning disabilities — and names **niacin (B-3) deficiency first** among their causes (WAL-CLM-LETS-000388). Niacin recurs as a treatment across the mental conditions specifically: bipolar (LETS-000159), dementia (LETS-000243), hysteria (LETS-000310), anxiety/panic (LETS-000142, DDDL-000078), insomnia (LETS-000322). Wallach also lists B-3 in a dementia mineral-replacement panel alongside gallium, germanium, selenium, etc. (WAL-CLM-RARE-000282).

### The two-dose gap (a genuine number story)
Maintenance is tiny; therapy is enormous:
- **Maintenance target:** 10–100 mg/day (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000114); an older base-line figure of 50 mg "True Supplement Need" (WAL-CLM-LETS-000059).
- **Clinical / pharmacologic:** the same LETS-000059 row gives a **30-day pharmacologic dose of 2,000 to 6,000 mg** (time-release) — and the materia-medica protocols routinely use 450 mg t.i.d. (≈1,350 mg/day). So the therapeutic dose runs roughly 13–60× the maintenance floor.
- **Watch the RDA trap (already flagged in the pack):** LETS-000059's verbatim row reads "NIACIN 18 mg 50 mg 2,000 to 6,000 mg" — the first figure (18 mg) is the government RDA that Wallach reprints only to argue against; it is NOT his recommendation. Do not surface 18 mg as a Wallach number.

### Curios / surprising facts (grounded)
- **The niacin "flush"** — the characteristic skin flushing, listed with liver impairment as the sign of niacin **toxicity / excess** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000020). This is the natural companion to "why time-release" — the flush is why Wallach specifies time-release tablets in nearly every protocol (the flush claim + the recurring "time-release" qualifier support the connection; state it only as far as those claims go).
- **Glucose tolerance factor (GTF):** in the diabetes regimen Wallach insists the B-complex "include niacin which is part of the GTF 'glucose tolerance factor'" (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000049) — niacin has a blood-sugar role, not just an energy/skin/mind one.
- **"Beef tongue":** a memorable, drawable single sign (EPIGEN-000037, RARE-000235).

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### Concept A — "The Three Ds" (deficiency triad)
- **Name:** The Three Ds.
- **The hook:** Run low on niacin and the body fails in three directions at once — the gut, the skin, and the mind.
- **Layout shape:** Three side-by-side (or stacked) panels, one per D — **Diarrhea · Dermatitis · Dementia** — each a short label + a one-line plain gloss, unified under the word "pellagra." A small footnote strip for the other vivid signs ("beef tongue," darkened skin). No beats-chassis; the triad IS the structure.
- **Illustration:** Three large letterform "D"s, each captioned with its domain (gut / skin / mind) — one idea, three elements, nothing crossing any label. Optionally a single small tongue glyph for "beef tongue" in the footnote, kept off the main triad.
- **Anchored by:** three Ds = WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037 ("the 'three Ds' (diarrhea, dermatitis, dementia)") and WAL-CLM-RARE-000235; "beef tongue" / skin pigmentation = EPIGEN-000037, RARE-000235; pellagra as the named deficiency = EPIGEN-000037, RARE-000235, and named in the organic-brain-syndrome umbrella LETS-000388.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** The most memorable and most uniquely-niacin fact in the pack — a clean mnemonic a reader keeps. Distinct from all six shipped headers (none use a triad-of-symptoms). Instantly legible, zero clutter.

### Concept B — "The furnace" (mechanism: energy from all three foods)
- **Name:** What burns your food.
- **The hook:** Niacin is the working part of the coenzyme that releases energy from every carbohydrate, fat, and protein you eat.
- **Layout shape:** A single converging figure — three labelled fuels (carbohydrate · fat · protein) feeding into one coenzyme node (NAD / NADP), out to "energy." A short caption naming the oxidation-reduction reaction. One figure, no separate beat list.
- **Illustration:** Three inflow labels → a central coenzyme mark → one outflow ("energy"). Few elements, converging arrows routed AROUND every label (never through). This is the "single richly-annotated figure" archetype.
- **Anchored by:** entirely WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037 — NAD & NADP, "oxidation-reduction reactions that release energy from carbohydrates, fats, and proteins."
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Answers "what does it actually DO" in one glance, and the all-three-macronutrients detail is genuinely striking (few vitamins touch every food group). **Caveat:** rests on a SINGLE claim — see Gaps; strong but thin, and the coenzyme names risk being jargon-heavy for a lay reader.

### Concept C — "Two doses" (maintenance vs clinical + the flush)
- **Name:** The tiny dose and the big one.
- **The hook:** Your daily need is a pinch; Wallach's therapeutic dose is dozens of times larger — and that's why it has to be time-release.
- **Layout shape:** A two-lane proportion comparison — a small maintenance bar (10–100 mg) against a towering clinical bar (450 mg × 3, up to 2,000–6,000 mg) — with a single callout: "why time-release? the flush." Number-forward, no pull-quote needed.
- **Illustration:** Two proportion bars at true scale (the visual gap IS the point), each labelled with its number and purpose. Minimal — two bars, two labels, one callout.
- **Anchored by:** maintenance 10–100 mg = WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000114; older 50 mg base-line + 2,000–6,000 mg pharmacologic = WAL-CLM-LETS-000059 (remember: the 18 mg is the rejected RDA, not Wallach's); 450 mg t.i.d. clinical = e.g. WAL-CLM-LETS-000388, LETS-000243, DDDL-000078; the flush / why-time-release = WAL-CLM-LETS-000020 (niacin "flush," toxicity) + the recurring "time-release" qualifier across the protocols.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** A real, grounded number drama and a satisfying "aha" (the flush explains the time-release note readers see everywhere). **Caveat:** must NOT imply 450 mg is the daily target — the header's "why this number" is 100 mg maintenance; keep the clinical bar clearly labelled "therapeutic, not daily."

### Concept D — "Wallach's workhorse for the mind" (therapeutic roster / one tool, many minds)
- **Name:** The mind's default.
- **The hook:** When something goes wrong in the mind, niacin is the nutrient Wallach reaches for first — the same time-release dose, over and over.
- **Layout shape:** A compact roster — niacin as one recurring name down a short list of mental/behavioral conditions it treats (anxiety, dementia, bipolar, insomnia, hysteria, schizophrenia via the OBS umbrella), framed by Wallach's "organic brain syndrome." A tight list, not a many-spoke diagram.
- **Illustration:** A single clean vertical "recurring-dose" motif — the same 450 mg time-release chip appearing beside each condition name — reinforcing "one tool, many minds." Deliberately avoid a cluttered hub-and-spoke; a repeated small mark down a column reads instantly.
- **Anchored by:** organic brain syndrome umbrella + niacin named first among causes = WAL-CLM-LETS-000388; per-condition treatment use = anxiety WAL-CLM-LETS-000142 / DDDL-000078, dementia LETS-000243, bipolar LETS-000159, hysteria LETS-000310, insomnia LETS-000322; deficiency-side mental signs (confusion, depression, memory loss) = LETS-000019.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Captures the pack's most distinctive PATTERN — the sheer breadth of niacin's mental use — which no single-fact header conveys. **Caveat:** thematically adjacent to A's "dementia" D; keep A about DEFICIENCY collapse and D about THERAPEUTIC breadth so they don't read as the same idea. Risk of feeling list-heavy if not tightly curated.

(grounds: mechanism EPIGEN-000037; three Ds EPIGEN-000037 / RARE-000235)

"The coenzyme vitamin that turns food into energy and steadies the mind — needed in only milligrams to prevent pellagra, yet the one Wallach reaches for again and again, in far larger doses, across anxiety, dementia and the sleepless." (grounds: mechanism EPIGEN-000037; maintenance mg EPIGEN-000114; therapeutic breadth LETS-000388, LETS-000142, LETS-000243, LETS-000322) — Must not restate a concept's opening beat; if Concept A ships, prefer lede 2 (energy+mind) so the lede doesn't pre-spend the three-Ds hook.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

Wallach's daily niacin target is **100 mg**. It comes from his Epigenetics (2014) daily multiple vitamin-and-mineral program, which lists niacin at a **range of 10–100 mg/day** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000114); the app takes the **upper end of that range, 100 mg**. Niacin is dosed natively in milligrams, so there is no IU conversion and no body-weight scaling factor — the target is simply the top of Wallach's stated maintenance range, rounded to the figure he printed. (An older base-line program in Let's Play Doctor, 1995, put the maintenance "True Supplement Need" at 50 mg — WAL-CLM-LETS-000059 — but the newer Epigenetics range governs. Both are maintenance figures; keep them separate from Wallach's much larger *therapeutic* doses of 450 mg and up, which are for specific conditions, not a daily target.)

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Mechanism rests on a single claim.** The entire "what niacin does" story (NAD/NADP, energy from carbs/fats/proteins) is WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000037 alone. Concept B is strong but thin — one claim carries it. No corroborating mechanism claim exists in the pack.
- **No discovery / etymology / history / food-source claims.** Nothing on why it's called niacin, who found it, or which foods carry it. A "curio" header of the vitamin-A ancient-Egypt type is NOT supported — do not invent one.
- **The RDA number is a trap.** WAL-CLM-LETS-000059's verbatim opens with "NIACIN 18 mg …" — that 18 mg is the *government RDA Wallach reprints to argue against*, not his recommendation. Any concept touching numbers (esp. C) must never surface 18 mg as a Wallach figure. Pack's claim_text already flags this.
- **Two maintenance figures (100 mg vs 50 mg).** EPIGEN-000114 (10–100 mg, 2014) vs LETS-000059 (50 mg, 1995). Not a contradiction to hide — newest wins for the target (100 mg); the 50 mg is a legitimate older figure worth a footnote, not a header centerpiece.
- **Clinical vs maintenance dose confusion is the biggest design risk.** 450 mg t.i.d. and 2,000–6,000 mg are *therapeutic*, not the daily target. Concept C is compelling BUT must label the big bar unambiguously or it will read as "take 1,350 mg a day," which the corpus does not say.
- **A and D overlap on the mind/brain theme.** Both touch dementia. Keep them cleanly separated (A = deficiency collapse / pellagra; D = therapeutic breadth) or pick one — do not ship two brain-themed mockups that read as reskins.
- **Toxicity is thin but usable.** Only WAL-CLM-LETS-000020 (flush + liver impairment). Enough for a callout (Concept C's flush note), not enough for a full deficiency-vs-toxicity two-lane header on its own.
- **Solo header verdict:** YES — niacin clearly merits its own bespoke header. 25 claims, a strong unique mechanism, the vivid pellagra triad, an unusually broad deficiency map, and a large therapeutic footprint. It should not fold into or alongside another element.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**Concept A — "The Three Ds."** It is the most memorable, most uniquely-niacin idea in the pack, is anchored by three independent claims (EPIGEN-000037, RARE-000235, LETS-000388), is instantly legible with zero clutter, and is structurally unlike all six shipped headers. Concept B (the furnace) is the strongest runner-up for a "what it does" angle but rests on a single claim — pair or alternate it with A rather than lead on it.
