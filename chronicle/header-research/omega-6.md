# Omega-6 (Linoleic Acid) — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 19 sealed claims · fatty acid (essential) · target: SHARED essential-fatty-acid budget, no solo omega-6 number (wallach_collective, 9 g/day for the EFA group · WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115).

_Coordination note: an omega-3 dossier already exists (`chronicle/header-research/omega-3.md`) and this pack shares ~two-thirds of its claims with it. omega-3 has already claimed the "third-carbon naming" (its concept A), the prostaglandin "factory" (its C) and the balance scale (its D), and explicitly recommends that **omega-6 own the lipoxins angle**. The concepts below are chosen to be omega-6-specific and to complement, not duplicate, omega-3's set._

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity — linoleic acid, one of only TWO fats the body cannot build.**
Of all fats, Wallach names only two as classically essential: alpha-linolenic acid (the omega-3) and **linoleic acid, an omega-6** — the body cannot manufacture them and must take them from the diet (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197, WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053). He counts three essential fatty acids among the body's 90 essential nutrients (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197). Three polyunsaturated fats — linoleic, linolenic, arachidonic — are *grouped* as "essential fatty acids," but only two are strictly essential because the body can build arachidonic acid itself from linoleic (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000063, WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053). His frame: these are structural raw material for cell membranes and prostaglandins, not fuel (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197).

**★ The omega-6 family spans the WHOLE essentiality spectrum (the most omega-6-specific angle).**
Omega-6 alone contributes three of the named fatty acids, sitting across the full range of "essential": **linoleic acid is strictly essential** (must be eaten — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197), while **arachidonic acid and gamma-linolenic acid (GLA) are both omega-6 fats Wallach classes as "conditionally essential"** — fats the body can normally make on its own but that must come from the diet under certain conditions (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000450). Arachidonic acid, specifically, is built by the human from linoleic acid (WAL-CLM-RARE-000109). No other omega family in this pack straddles both "must eat" and "usually make your own" with three named members — omega-3 has only alpha-linolenic (strict) and DHA (conditional). This is omega-6's own, un-shared story.

**The name is an address — but the exact carbon is NOT stated for omega-6.**
The "omega" number marks where the fat's first double bond sits, counted from the "tail" or methyl end of the chain (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204). For omega-3 that bond is at the third carbon; omega-6 and omega-9 are named the same way for bonds "farther along" (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197). **CAUTION:** the pack states the third carbon for omega-3 but never states "the sixth carbon" for omega-6 — do not print a specific carbon number for omega-6 as a Wallach fact (see Gaps). omega-3 already owns the molecular-naming concept regardless.

**Not fuel — the raw material for the body's local hormones, and omega-6 owns the lipoxin line.**
EFAs are the raw material the body uses to make **prostaglandins**, which help regulate blood pressure, heart rate, vascular dilation, blood clotting, bronchial (airway) dilation, and central-nervous-system function (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000064, WAL-CLM-RARE-000110); they also maintain cell-wall integrity and lower serum cholesterol (WAL-CLM-RARE-000110, WAL-CLM-DDDL-000065). Wallach widens this to a whole signaling family — eicosanoids, endocannabinoids (mood, behavior, inflammation), lipoxins and resolvins — and specifies that **lipoxins (a class of eicosanoid derivatives via the lipoxygenase pathway) come from omega-6 EFAs**, while resolvins come from omega-3 (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000399). So omega-6 is not just a structural fat: it is the starting material the body converts into specific inflammation- and signaling-regulating molecules.

**The balance: omega-6 works only in ratio with omega-3.**
Every EFA function requires a proper **ratio** between the omega-3 and omega-6 forms — getting enough omega-6 is not the whole story; it must be in the right balance with omega-3 (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398). His model of that balance is the traditional/tribal diet, which contained **nearly equal amounts** of omega-3 and omega-6, with only about **4% of calories** from polyunsaturated oils (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000202). _(Shared with omega-3, which took this as its concept D — coordinate.)_

**Deficiency — a shortage shows first on the surface, then reaches deep.**
The full deficiency map (Let's Play Doctor Table 6-1) lists: acne, alopecia, arthritis, atrophy of the endocrine glands, diarrhea, **dry brittle hair**, eczema, endocrine dysfunction, fatty degeneration of the liver, gallstones, growth retardation, immunologic dysfunction, impaired wound healing, infertility, kidney dysfunction, a positive sweat test (cystic fibrosis, anorexia), and **xerosis (abnormally dry skin)** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000011). At the deeper end, deficiency produces **thrombosis** (cerebral stroke, coronary thrombosis, pulmonary embolism, DVT), skin disease (dry/cracked skin, dermatitis, eczema, psoriasis, rosacea, acne), respiratory disease (asthma, chronic bronchitis, unremitting cough), and depression (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000059). In infants specifically: poor growth, eczema, and lowered resistance to infection (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000065, WAL-CLM-RARE-000111) — and per the Immortality list, lowered IQ, seizures and learning disabilities (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053).

**Curio 1 — the animal evidence.**
In animals, EFA deficiency produces atherosclerosis — Wallach cites it seen in a **sheep's aorta and a pheasant's coronary arteries** (WAL-CLM-RARE-000111). A concrete, unusual detail unique to this pack.

**Curio 2 — the hidden hair-mineral tell (a diagnostic).**
Elevated **hair** levels of sodium, potassium and chloride indicate an EFA deficiency: the shortage makes the skin glands malfunction and lose electrolytes instead of conserving them; these false "elevations" return to normal once proper mineral and EFA supplementation is restored (WAL-CLM-RARE-000334).

**Curio 3 — "vitamin F," 1923.**
The two essential fatty acids were identified in **1923** and originally listed as "**vitamin F**"; in **1929** laboratory rat studies showed they were more properly classed as fats than vitamins, so the vitamin label was dropped (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000397).

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A — "The whole spectrum" (omega-6 runs from must-eat to make-your-own)
- **Name** — The Spectrum.
- **The hook** — Omega-6 isn't one fat with one rule: linoleic acid you MUST eat, while its cousins arachidonic and GLA the body usually makes itself — so a single family straddles the whole line of "essential."
- **Layout shape** — A horizontal essentiality axis as the hero, labelled "must be eaten" at one end and "the body usually makes it" at the other, with three omega-6 members placed along it and a short pivot line beneath ("only omega-6 spans the whole range"). No beats chassis, no big-number stat, no pull quote — one axis carrying the idea.
- **Illustration** — ONE clean gradient bar/axis with exactly three nodes: **linoleic** (locked to the "must eat" end, the one accent), **arachidonic** and **GLA** toward the "usually make" end, arachidonic tagged "built from linoleic." Few elements; do not route the axis line through a node label — set labels above/below the line.
- **Anchored by** — linoleic strictly essential (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197); arachidonic + GLA conditionally essential, both omega-6 (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000450); arachidonic built from linoleic (WAL-CLM-RARE-000109).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — This is the ONE angle omega-6 owns outright and that omega-3 cannot use (omega-6 has three named members across the spectrum; omega-3 has two). Distinct from omega-3's "Only Two" tiles: that was a cross-family two-vs-three; this is a within-omega-6 gradient. A single labelled axis is maximally clutter-resistant.

### B — "Vitamin F" (the essential fat that was almost a vitamin)
- **Name** — Vitamin F.
- **The hook** — Omega-6 was discovered in 1923 and filed under the vitamins as "vitamin F" — until 1929 rat studies proved it was a fat, and the letter was quietly retired.
- **Layout shape** — A did-you-know CURIO card leading (parallel to vitamin A's ancient-Egypt liver curio), then a tight identity line resolving what it really is (one of only two fats the body can't make). History-first, not beats-first.
- **Illustration** — ONE period nutrition label / stamp reading "**Vitamin F**" with a hand-struck "1929 → fat" correction over it. One idea, two or three marks — a stamp and its cancellation. Do not draw a stroke through the readable letters (route the cancel-mark to the margin or a corner).
- **Anchored by** — identified 1923, listed "vitamin F"; 1929 rat studies reclassed the two fatty acids as fats, label dropped (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000397); it is one of the two classically essential fats (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — A genuine, dateable "huh" fact almost no reader knows, and it explains a leftover oddity (why "F"?). Curio-led layout is a distinct grammar from A, C and D, and omega-3 deliberately left this for a header rather than a footnote. Warmly human, low clutter risk.

### C — "From fat to signal" (omega-6 becomes the body's messengers — the lipoxin line)
- **Name** — The Messenger.
- **The hook** — Omega-6 isn't burned for energy; the body converts it into signalling molecules — and lipoxins, the ones that ride the lipoxygenase pathway, are omega-6's own product.
- **Layout shape** — A single input-to-output transformation: one omega-6 block converting into a small, tight set of named signal molecules, with a one-line frame above ("not fuel — a starting material"). One figure, nothing else.
- **Illustration** — ONE omega-6 fat block → a short branch to a FEW labelled products: **lipoxins** (the accent, tagged "omega-6's own") plus prostaglandins, with eicosanoids/endocannabinoids named as the family. Cap the outputs — a switchboard, not a station map. Route no connector through a label.
- **Anchored by** — lipoxins come from omega-6 EFAs via the lipoxygenase pathway; resolvins come from omega-3 (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000399); EFAs are the raw material for prostaglandins governing blood pressure, clotting, airways, CNS (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000064, WAL-CLM-RARE-000110); functions require the omega-3:omega-6 ratio (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Reframes "a fat" as a control substance, and the lipoxin distinction is the exact angle omega-3's dossier reserved FOR omega-6, so the pair reads as complementary (omega-3 → resolvins, omega-6 → lipoxins). **UX caveat:** highest clutter/overlap risk of the four — it shares prostaglandin material with omega-3's "factory" concept, so at design time it must lean on the lipoxin distinction, not re-draw omega-3's four prostaglandin dials.

### D — "Where it shows first" (dry brittle hair and cracked skin are the outward tell)
- **Name** — The First Tell.
- **The hook** — Run low on omega-6 and it surfaces first where you can see it — dry, brittle hair and dry, cracked skin — long before the deeper trouble.
- **Layout shape** — A first-visible-sign hero (parallel in spirit to copper's premature-gray, but a different sign and figure), captioned by a short widening line naming what the same shortage reaches deeper (thrombosis, respiratory disease, depression). Iconic-detail archetype, not a beats fan.
- **Illustration** — ONE close detail: a single dry, splitting hair strand and a patch of dry/cracked skin (xerosis) — the visible tells — as the figure; the deeper conditions named only as a short caption list, NOT drawn as a cluttered body-map. Two focal marks, few elements.
- **Anchored by** — dry brittle hair + xerosis (abnormally dry skin) in the deficiency map (WAL-CLM-LETS-000011); dry/cracked skin, dermatitis, eczema, psoriasis in EFA deficiency (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000059); the deeper reach — thrombosis/stroke, respiratory disease, depression (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000059).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Turns an abstract "essential fat" into something a reader can check on their own hair and skin, and the surface-to-deep widening gives a satisfying payoff. Distinct illustration family (a detail texture) from A (axis), B (stamp) and C (transformation). Copper owns gray hair; omega-6 owns dry brittle hair + dry skin, so no collision with the shipped set.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

Honest situation: **there is no omega-6-only target.** `target.kind` is `wallach_collective` — omega-6 shares a single essential-fatty-acid budget with omega-3 (and, in the grouping, arachidonic). The source is WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115: EFAs "should be consumed at the rate of 3 percent of your total daily calorie consumption or supplemented at the rate of 9 grams per day in capsule form." That 9 g/day is **one combined amount for the essential fatty acids together — not 9 g of omega-6 by itself.** So the coverage figure shown against omega-6 is a shared group budget (3% of daily calories, or 9 g/day supplemented), and the header must say so plainly rather than imply a per-element dose. No IU factor, body-weight scale, or rounding applies — it is a flat, collective amount stated directly by Wallach. Consistent with the settled position that the EFA collective dose is a singleton on WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Collective target, not a solo number.** 9 g/day belongs to the EFA group (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000115); omega-6 has no independent Wallach dose. The "why this number" must present a shared budget, never a fabricated per-element figure.

- **Do NOT print a specific carbon number for omega-6.** The pack states the first double bond is at the *third* carbon for omega-3 (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000204, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197) and says omega-6/omega-9 are named the same way for bonds "farther along," but it NEVER states "the sixth carbon" for omega-6. "Count to six" would be an inference, not a Wallach fact — and omega-3 already owns the molecular-naming concept anyway. That is why no molecule/naming concept is proposed here for omega-6.

- **"Three EFAs" vs "two essential" — handle carefully.** The pack both lists three fatty acids as EFA (linoleic, linolenic, arachidonic — WAL-CLM-DDDL-000063) and states only two are genuinely essential because arachidonic is built from linoleic (WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000053, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197). Concept A turns this exact nuance into an asset (linoleic strict; arachidonic + GLA conditional) — the safest way to use it. Do not print "three essential" flatly elsewhere.

- **Omega-9 slotting is an outlier — do not build on it.** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000394 is the one place Wallach groups omega-9 (and cholesterol) into the EFA list and lists omega-3 as "DHA and EPA"; it runs against his dominant two-essential stance. Its own claim_text flags the tension. Leave omega-9 out of any figure.

- **Heavy overlap with omega-3 — deliberate differentiation required.** ~two-thirds of this pack is shared EFA material, and omega-3's dossier already claimed third-carbon naming, the prostaglandin factory and the balance scale. To keep the pair from reading as duplicates: give **omega-6 concept A (the essentiality spectrum) and concept C (lipoxins)** — both genuinely omega-6-owned per omega-3's own recommendation — and treat the balance/ratio material (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000398 / -000202) as a supporting line, not omega-6's hero (omega-3 has the balance scale).

- **GLA claim is `confidence: medium`.** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000450 (GLA + arachidonic conditionally essential) is the only medium-confidence claim used, and it anchors concept A's second half. The verbatim clearly supports it ("This group includes: arachidonic acid... gamma-linolenic acid"), so it is usable, but flag it for Luneth's eye given A leans on it.

- **Deficiency breadth is wide but individually thin.** The reach across ~22 conditions is real but a list-everything figure would be the cluttered-diagram failure the rules forbid; concept D uses WAL-CLM-LETS-000011 + -000059 as a first-sign spine, not a fan, deliberately.

- **No omega-6 EXCESS claim.** A modern "too much omega-6" story is a real-world commonplace but is NOT in this pack — Wallach only states traditional diets were *near-equal* and low-PUFA (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000202). Do not imply omega-6 excess; there is no claim for it.

- **Solo header: warranted, but design as a pair-complement.** Omega-6 is a canon essential with at least one fully self-supporting, un-shared angle (the essentiality spectrum) plus its own curios (vitamin F, animal atherosclerosis, hair-mineral tell). It merits its own bespoke header — but should be built as a deliberate complement to the omega-3 header given the shared dose and shared ratio story.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**A — "The whole spectrum."** It is the single angle omega-6 owns outright and omega-3 cannot copy (three named omega-6 members straddling strictly-essential → conditionally-essential), it grounds cleanly in WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000197 + WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000450 + WAL-CLM-RARE-000109, and a labelled gradient axis is the most clutter-resistant figure of the four. Pair it with lede #1 (which does not restate its opening beat). Concept B (vitamin F) is the strongest backup and the most human.
