# Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine) — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 50 sealed claims · vitamin (orange accent) · target: 100 mg/day (upper of Wallach's 25–100 mg daily-multiple range, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000116).

_Grounding note: every fact below cites a claim ID from the sealed pack. Nothing here comes from outside the pack. The pack holds 50 claims across 5 books (dddl-3e-2011, epigenetics, immortality, lets-play-doctor, rare-earths); ~40 are Let's Play Doctor Ch10 materia-medica protocols in which B6 is one ingredient among many, so the B6-CENTRIC material is a smaller, sharper set — flagged in §5._

---

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

### A. Core identity — what B6 IS
Wallach's one biochemical definition: vitamin B6 (pyridoxine) is converted to **pyridoxal phosphate**, the **coenzyme for transamination and other reactions in amino-acid, lipid, and nucleic-acid metabolism, and for glycogen phosphorylase** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000039). It is a water-soluble B vitamin (tagged "water-soluble-vitamins", EPIGEN-000039/000108). This single claim is the only "mechanism-of-action" statement in the pack — it is the identity anchor, but it is thin (one claim), so it feeds the lede more than a whole illustration.

### B. The nerve-and-brain signature (the dominant deficiency cluster)
B6 deficiency reads overwhelmingly as a **neurological/mental** picture. Wallach's deficiency lists name: **depression and mental confusion, seizures and convulsions, ataxia (instability), head tic (Tourette's syndrome), peripheral neuritis** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000039; WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000108; WAL-CLM-RARE-000237). The Let's Play Doctor Table 6-1 list adds **neurologic symptoms, seizures, dizziness, irritability, nervousness** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000027). This is the standout theme of the whole element: B6 = the vitamin the nervous system runs short of.

### C. The bold protocol — "B6 alone will frequently 'cure' epilepsy"
The single most striking claim in the pack: **"Vitamin B-6 alone will frequently 'cure' epilepsy at 50-100 mg t.i.d."** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267) — one vitamin, on its own. Supporting nerve-storm protocols: convulsions treated with B6 100 mg b.i.d. (WAL-CLM-LETS-000233); absence attacks (petit mal) with B6 100–300 mg/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000120). Related deficiency signs (seizures/convulsions) at EPIGEN-000039, LETS-000027, RARE-000237.

### D. B6 instead of the scalpel — carpal tunnel
Wallach treats **carpal tunnel syndrome with B6 rather than the conventional wrist surgery**: B6 75 mg t.i.d. + zinc + cartilage (WAL-CLM-LETS-000205), and for numbness/tingling in both hands B6 200–300 mg t.i.d. + B12 (WAL-CLM-LETS-000383). Carpal tunnel also appears as a B6 deficiency sign (EPIGEN-000039, EPIGEN-000108). A clean "the vitamin, not the operation" angle.

### E. The two-edged dose — too little AND too much both hit the nerves (a genuine U-curve)
A rare, grounded symmetry:
- **Too little** → peripheral neuritis, ataxia/instability, "neurologic symptoms," carpal-tunnel numbness (WAL-CLM-RARE-000237; WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000108; WAL-CLM-LETS-000027).
- **Too much** → **"electric shock" sensations and paresthesia (numbness and tingling)** — Wallach's explicit toxicity list (WAL-CLM-LETS-000028) — and long-term epilepsy dosing "can cause some numbness and tingling in the face or hands" (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267).

Both ends of the dial produce nerve/tingling symptoms; the working window (25–100 mg maintenance, EPIGEN-000116 / LETS-000063) sits in the middle. B6 is one of the few water-soluble vitamins Wallach gives an actual toxicity ceiling — a striking, uniquely-B6 story.

### F. The hidden drain — everyday drugs steal your B6
- **The pill:** "15 to 20 percent of women taking oral contraceptives show the symptoms of pyridoxine deficiency" — **malaise, depression, and glucose intolerance** (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000009).
- **Blood-pressure drugs:** antihypertensives Propranolol (Inderal), Methyldopa (Aldomet), Reserpine — flagged for mineral replacement of **Ca, Mg, Omega-3, B6** (WAL-CLM-RARE-000286).

A relatable "your prescription is quietly costing you a nutrient" reveal.

### G. The pregnancy / maternal story
- **Morning sickness = B6 deficiency:** "'Morning sickness' … in the first trimester … is thought to be due to pyridoxine deficiency" (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000008); treated with B6 + B-complex 25 mg t.i.d. (WAL-CLM-LETS-000369).
- **Pregnancy toxemia / eclampsia:** B6 100 mg/day, **dropping to 50 mg at birth if breast-feeding, since high B6 reduces breast-milk production** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000407) — a genuine curio.
- **Cerebral palsy:** attributed to a **preconception deficiency of zinc and B6 in the mother** — with "no treatment since the damage occurred … during the formation of the brain" (WAL-CLM-LETS-000210). Striking but sombre (an untreatable outcome).

### H. Skin & mucous-membrane signs (the visible layer)
Deficiency also shows on skin and mouth: **seborrheic dermatitis / itchy scaly skin, cheilosis (cracked lip corners), geographic tongue, facial oiliness, acne, oral mucous-membrane lesions** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000027; WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000039/000108). Protocol appearances: cradle cap B6 10–25 mg (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000055 / WAL-CLM-LETS-000237), dandruff/seborrheic dermatitis (WAL-CLM-LETS-000242 / 000430), dry skin (WAL-CLM-LETS-000254), acne (WAL-CLM-LETS-000123).

### I. Neuro-psychiatric & drug-induced conditions (B6 as a recurring co-treatment)
B6 appears across the psychiatric/movement-disorder protocols: **tardive dyskinesia** B6 200 mg q.i.d. (WAL-CLM-LETS-000445), **drug-induced Parkinsonism** B6 100 mg t.i.d. (WAL-CLM-LETS-000396), **bipolar disorder** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000159), **anxiety/panic** B6 100 mg t.i.d. (WAL-CLM-DDDL-000078 / WAL-CLM-LETS-000142), **autism at "five times the RDA for weight and age"** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000151), depression (WAL-CLM-LETS-000244), dementia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000243), hysteria (WAL-CLM-LETS-000310).

### J. Women's / hormonal
**PMS** — a named B6 deficiency sign (EPIGEN-000108) and treated with B6 100 mg (WAL-CLM-LETS-000402); **dysmenorrhea** B6 50–100 mg t.i.d. (WAL-CLM-LETS-000258).

### K. The dose spread (for the "why this number" tip and any dial figure)
- **Maintenance target used by the app: 100 mg/day** = upper of the Epigenetics daily-multiple range **25–100 mg** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000116).
- **"True Supplement Need" 50 mg/day** in the Let's Play Doctor Base Line program (WAL-CLM-LETS-000063).
- **Pharmacologic 200–500 mg/day** (30-day), same Base Line row (WAL-CLM-LETS-000063).
- **Therapeutic condition doses commonly 100 mg t.i.d. (=300 mg/day)** across Ch10 protocols (e.g. epilepsy LETS-000267, arthritis LETS-000146, joint pain LETS-000326).

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### Concept A — "The dial with two danger zones" (the two-edged nerve vitamin)
- **Name:** Both-ends-tingle dial.
- **The hook:** B6 is the rare vitamin where **too little AND too much both attack the nerves** — the safe amount lives in a window between two tingling extremes.
- **Layout shape:** A single horizontal dose gauge spanning the block, with the identity line above it and a one-line resolution beneath. No beats, no pull-quote — the figure carries the whole idea.
- **Illustration:** One horizontal gauge/track, three zones, minimal marks. Left flag: **TOO LITTLE → peripheral neuritis, ataxia, numbness**. Centre band (lit): **25–100 mg — the working window**. Right flag: **TOO MUCH → "electric shock" sensations, paresthesia**. One idea, ~5 elements. (Route the zone labels ABOVE/BELOW the track, never through it — Rule ★.)
- **Anchored by:** deficiency-side nerve signs WAL-CLM-RARE-000237, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000108, WAL-CLM-LETS-000027; toxicity-side WAL-CLM-LETS-000028 ("electric shock" sensations, paresthesia) + WAL-CLM-LETS-000267 (long-term "numbness and tingling in the face or hands"); window 25–100 mg WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000116; 50 mg maintenance / 200–500 mg pharmacologic WAL-CLM-LETS-000063.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Genuinely unusual for a vitamin — most people think water-soluble vitamins have no ceiling. The figure teaches the target number as a natural consequence of the story (why 100, not 500), which no other concept does as cleanly. Distinct from every shipped header (a static gauge, not a rail/fork/timeline).

### Concept B — "One vitamin, on its own" (the epilepsy-cure hero)
- **Name:** B6-alone hero.
- **The hook:** A single vitamin that Wallach says will frequently **"cure" epilepsy** — and quiet the whole family of nerve storms.
- **Layout shape:** A bold statement-led hero: the epilepsy claim as the dominant centrepiece, with a tight satellite cluster of the other "storms" it calms. Quote-forward, NOT a beats chassis.
- **Illustration:** Minimal — two small side-by-side snapshots of a brain-wave (EEG) line: left **jagged/chaotic (seizure)**, right **calm/steady**. Two panels, no connecting timeline stroke, labels sit under each panel (kept distinct from selenium's single decline-rail).
- **Anchored by:** WAL-CLM-LETS-000267 ("Vitamin B-6 alone will frequently 'cure' epilepsy at 50-100 mg t.i.d."); satellite conditions — convulsions WAL-CLM-LETS-000233, absence attacks (petit mal) WAL-CLM-LETS-000120, Tourette's head tic WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000108 / WAL-CLM-RARE-000237; seizure deficiency sign WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000039.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** The single most jaw-dropping line in the pack. Boldness of "one vitamin cures epilepsy" is instantly memorable; the satellite cluster shows it is a pattern (B6 = the anticonvulsant vitamin), not a one-off.

### Concept C — "The hidden drain" (drugs steal your B6)
- **Name:** Prescription drain.
- **The hook:** Everyday medications quietly deplete B6 — **15–20% of women on the pill already show the deficiency**, often without knowing why.
- **Layout shape:** A two-part "hidden cost" panel: the culprit drug(s) on one side, the resulting B6 deficit + its symptoms on the other, with a short named list of offenders. No cycle, no dial.
- **Illustration:** A single draining-reservoir motif (a B6 level dropping) OR one clean "drug − B6 = symptoms" equation card. One idea, few elements.
- **Anchored by:** WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000009 (oral contraceptives → 15–20% show deficiency: malaise, depression, glucose intolerance); WAL-CLM-RARE-000286 (antihypertensives Inderal / Aldomet / Reserpine → replace Ca, Mg, Omega-3, B6).
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Highly relatable and actionable — millions take these drugs. Reframes B6 from an abstract vitamin into "the thing your prescription is costing you." Distinct angle: no other concept touches drug-nutrient depletion.

### Concept D — "The pregnancy vitamin" (the maternal arc)
- **Name:** Maternal B6 arc.
- **The hook:** The vitamin the first trimester runs short of — Wallach reads **morning sickness itself as a B6 deficiency**, and follows B6 from before conception to birth.
- **Layout shape:** A simple three-stage life-stage arc — **preconception → first trimester → birth/nursing** — each stage carrying B6's role, ending on the breast-milk nuance. A human timeline, not a mechanism diagram.
- **Illustration:** A single spare arc/ribbon with three labelled nodes (few elements; labels off the ribbon line). No faces, no busy scene.
- **Anchored by:** preconception — cerebral palsy from maternal zinc + B6 deficiency WAL-CLM-LETS-000210; first trimester — morning sickness = B6 deficiency WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000008 / WAL-CLM-LETS-000369 (B6 + B-complex 25 mg t.i.d.); birth/nursing — pregnancy toxemia/eclampsia B6 100 mg, drop to 50 mg at birth if breast-feeding (high B6 reduces breast milk) WAL-CLM-LETS-000407.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** A distinct, human, emotionally resonant story with a real curio (B6 suppresses breast-milk). Reframes a near-universal pregnancy experience. Note the preconception node is sombre (untreatable outcome) — handle with restraint.

_Distinctness check: A = static dose gauge; B = twin EEG-snapshot quote-hero; C = drain/equation cost panel; D = three-node life-stage arc. Four different layouts and four different illustrations — no shared skeleton, none reusing a shipped header's figure._

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

The daily target is **100 mg**, and it is the **top of Wallach's own everyday range, not a converted or scaled figure**. In Epigenetics his daily multiple-vitamin program lists vitamin B6 at **25–100 mg** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000116); the header takes the **upper of that range (100 mg)** — no IU conversion and no body-weight scaling, since the source is already stated in mg. For calibration: his Let's Play Doctor Base Line program sets a lower **"true supplement need" of 50 mg/day** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000063) and reserves **200–500 mg/day for short 30-day pharmacologic use** (same row). So 100 mg sits at the top of the daily maintenance window — above the 50 mg baseline, below the therapeutic and pharmacologic doses. _(target.kind = wallach, a plain single number; provenance original_low 25 / original_high 100 / upper_taken 100.)_

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Protocol-heavy, B6-centric-light.** ~40 of 50 claims are Let's Play Doctor Ch10 materia-medica protocols where B6 is one line in a long multi-nutrient stack (arthritis, cataracts, dementia, headache, etc.). They prove B6's breadth (the `conditions_treated` list is 50+ long) but are NOT B6-specific mechanism material — a header built on them would drown in ingredient lists. The genuinely B6-CENTRIC claims are the smaller set the four concepts lean on: EPIGEN-000039 (identity), LETS-000267 (epilepsy), LETS-000028 (toxicity), IMMORT-000009 (pill), the deficiency lists, and the pregnancy trio.
- **Two epigenetics deficiency claims are two halves of ONE list.** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000039 (page 620) and WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000108 (page 622) are a single deficiency list split across pages; 039's verbatim is truncated ("Nausea / Vomiting") while 108 carries the rest (PMS, seborrheic dermatitis, carpal tunnel, TMJ, ataxia, Tourette's, seizures). When quoting the full deficiency cluster at design time, pull BOTH.
- **No superseded claims; no number-vs-verbatim inflation on the target.** The 100 mg target is faithfully the upper of 25–100 (EPIGEN-000116). Note there are TWO competing maintenance figures (50 mg TSN vs 100 mg) — the app uses 100; state it, don't blend them.
- **Messy OCR in a couple of protocol verbatims** (not used for the target). WAL-CLM-LETS-000402 (PMS) reads "essential fatty acids at 5 mg t.i.d." (a likely typo for 5 gm) and "100 mg B-6 q 4 d" — do not quote those numbers; use the clean 100 mg B6 figure only. Cosmetic OCR only, no impact on the concepts.
- **Concept wished-for but unsupported:** a clean single "how pyridoxal phosphate actually works" mechanism illustration — the pack has only ONE mechanism claim (EPIGEN-000039), too thin for a bespoke figure, and a "one molecule, many enzyme jobs" diagram would risk the rejected cluttered-many-station look. Kept as lede material, not a concept.
- **Tone flag on Concept D / cerebral palsy:** WAL-CLM-LETS-000210 ends "there is no treatment." Real and grounded, but sombre — if D is chosen, frame the preconception node with restraint (a reason B6 matters BEFORE conception), not as a scare.
- **Solo header: YES.** B6 clearly merits its own bespoke header — a strong, distinctive identity (the nerve/brain vitamin), a jaw-dropping single-vitamin protocol, a uniquely-B6 toxicity ceiling, and a relatable drug-depletion story. It should NOT fold into another element. (Its huge `interacts_with` list — the whole B-complex plus many minerals and amino acids — reflects that it constantly co-appears in protocols, but that is co-treatment, not a merge case.)

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**Concept A — "The dial with two danger zones."** It is the most uniquely-B6 idea in the pack (a water-soluble vitamin with a real toxicity ceiling is genuinely surprising), it is fully grounded on both sides (deficiency nerve signs + the explicit toxicity list), and it is the only concept that teaches the 100 mg target as a natural payoff of its own story — making the header both wow and self-explaining. **Concept B (the epilepsy "cure" hero) is the boldest single hook** and the strongest runner-up if Luneth wants maximum shock over self-teaching.
