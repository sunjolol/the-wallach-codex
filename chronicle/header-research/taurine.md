# Taurine — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 6 sealed claims (≈2 identity + 3 shared protocol line-items) · amino acid · target: no Wallach maintenance amount stated (honest gap; coverage_kind = dietary_with_clinical_lever). solo-header: marginal.

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity — an amino acid Wallach ADDED to the essential list.**
Wallach appends three amino acids to the classic list of essentials — arginine, taurine, and tyrosine — and says that "over the long haul" each helps prevent a specific disease. Taurine's is **macular degeneration** (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058, verbatim; restated in WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066, verbatim: "add arginine, taurine, and tyrosine… these three amino acids help prevent certain specific diseases… cancer, macular degeneration, and goiter"). This is the one place taurine is the true subject of its own framework statement. Note the disease is taurine's own — arginine carries cancer/PAD, tyrosine carries goiter.

**The strongest thread — taurine and the eye.** Three distinct eye conditions attach to taurine across the pack:
- **Macular degeneration** — the disease Wallach adds taurine to the essential list to prevent (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058, WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066).
- **Cataracts** — taurine *deficiency* "can produce cataracts" (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054, verbatim).
- **Retinal damage** — taurine deficiency "can produce… retinal damage" (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054, verbatim).
Three separate eye failures, one amino acid — this is the pack's most distinctive, most bespoke-able story.

**The other verbatim-grounded roles (thin — one line each).**
From WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054's *verbatim*: taurine "is required for the maintenance of skeletal muscle and the maintenance of healthy blood pressure." That's the whole of it — two roles, one sentence, no numbers, no mechanism.

**The clinical lever — a recurring 500 mg, three times a day.** Taurine's `coverage_kind` is literally *dietary_with_clinical_lever*, and the pack bears that out: it recurs as a **500 mg t.i.d.** ingredient inside three of Wallach's protocols —
- **Absence attacks (petit mal seizures):** taurine 500 mg t.i.d., alongside choline 4 g/day, DMG 100 mg b.i.d., phosphatidyl choline + B-6 (WAL-CLM-LETS-000120, verbatim contains "taurine 500 mg t.i.d.").
- **Gallstones / gallbladder disease:** taurine 500 mg t.i.d., alongside lecithin, EFAs, vitamin E, C, selenium (WAL-CLM-LETS-000283, verbatim contains "taurine at 500 mg t.i.d").
- **Epilepsy:** taurine 500 mg t.i.d. per the claim_text — **but see the flag in §5: the epilepsy verbatim excerpt does NOT contain the taurine line** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267).
The dose is remarkably consistent (500 mg t.i.d. every time), but in each protocol taurine is one name in a long list, not the headline.

## 2. Header concepts (as many as the material honestly supports — 2)

### A — "Three ways to lose your sight" (the eye triad) — LEAD
- **The hook** — One amino acid stands between you and three separate eye diseases; run short and Wallach says cataracts, retinal damage, and macular degeneration follow.
- **Layout shape** — A single annotated eye as the centrepiece, three quiet labels reading off three regions of it, with the "why this number"/identity line beneath. Not beats, not a quote-chassis — one figure carries the whole argument, editorial and calm.
- **Illustration** — A simplified eye in cross-section (lens + retina + macula). One label per structure, each naming the failure taurine guards against: lens → *cataracts*, retina → *retinal damage*, macula → *macular degeneration*. Three labels, one shape, labels placed OFF the eye outline (no pointer stroke crossing a word). Fewest possible elements; the eye IS the diagram.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054 (verbatim: deficiency "can produce cataracts and retinal damage"); WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058 + WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066 (verbatim: taurine, of the three added aminos, prevents macular degeneration).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Taurine's material is thin everywhere EXCEPT the eye, where three independent claims converge on one organ. That convergence is genuinely surprising and visually clean — one recognisable shape, three real payoffs, all verbatim-grounded. This is the only concept that turns taurine's scattered claims into a single memorable picture.

### B — "The name Wallach added to the list" (framework curio)
- **The hook** — The textbook list of essential amino acids is missing three, Wallach says — and taurine is on his short list of additions, kept there to guard your central vision.
- **Layout shape** — A curio-led, typographic header: the classic list shown as settled, then Wallach's three additions (arginine · **taurine** · tyrosine) set apart, each tagged with the one disease it guards. Taurine emphasised as the subject; the other two present only to make the point that this is Wallach's own correction to orthodoxy.
- **Illustration** — Minimal and type-driven: a short "+ 3" appended to a compact essential-amino list, with three one-word disease tags (cancer/PAD · **macular degeneration** · goiter) beside the three names. No wired diagram — a list is the figure. Keep taurine + macular degeneration visually paired.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058 (verbatim: "To the classic list of essential amino acids, we would add arginine, taurine, and tyrosine… respectively… cancer and PAD, macular degeneration and goiter"); WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066 (same statement, newer book, verbatim).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Reframes an obscure amino acid as "the one the establishment left off the list," an honest Wallach-sourced surprise. Risk: the story is *shared* with arginine and tyrosine — this concept naturally wants to be a GROUP header ("the three Wallach added") rather than a solo taurine page. Use it solo only if taurine is kept clearly the subject and the other two stay supporting.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

**There is no Wallach maintenance amount to state — an honest gap.** `target.kind` is *dietary_with_clinical_lever* and `target.source` records "no maintenance amount stated (honest gap; blueprint §7.1)." Do NOT fabricate a daily number. The honest "why this number" copy is that Wallach never sets a daily taurine target; what he DOES give is a **clinical lever** — a therapeutic dose that appears identically across his protocols: **500 mg three times a day** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000120 absence attacks, WAL-CLM-LETS-000283 gallstones; also stated for epilepsy in WAL-CLM-LETS-000267's claim_text — see §5 verbatim flag). If a provenance line is wanted, anchor it on the two verbatim-clean protocols and frame it as a treatment dose, never as a maintenance target.

## 5. Gaps / flags + SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict

- **claim_text over-reaches its verbatim in WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054 — trust the verbatim.** The claim_text adds a stack of facts the verbatim does NOT contain: "sulfonic-acid amino acid," "conjugates bile acids," "acts as an antioxidant," "modulates calcium signaling," "central nervous system," and "doses above 2 grams per day have been shown to contribute to psoriasis." The verbatim covers ONLY: skeletal-muscle maintenance, healthy blood pressure, cataracts, retinal damage. **Build concepts on the verbatim only.** Do not use the bile-acid/antioxidant/calcium-signaling mechanism, the CNS framing, or the >2 g psoriasis warning in a header — they are unverified against the quote and injecting them is exactly the cardinal sin.
- **The epilepsy taurine dose is in claim_text but NOT in the quoted verbatim (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267).** The verbatim excerpt ends at "zinc at 50 mg t.i.d" and never reaches taurine. For the "500 mg t.i.d." lever, anchor on WAL-CLM-LETS-000120 and WAL-CLM-LETS-000283, whose verbatims DO contain the taurine line. Treat the epilepsy taurine dose as unverified until re-checked against the source page.
- **Two near-duplicate identity claims.** WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058 ≈ WAL-CLM-DDDL-000066 — the same "add arginine, taurine, tyrosine" statement. DDDL-3e-2011 is the newer book; IMMORT (2008) additionally carries the arginine→PAD detail. Neither is `superseded_by` the other; prefer citing DDDL-000066 as the newest, IMMORT-000058 for the fuller disease mapping. Both verbatims support taurine→macular degeneration.
- **`deficiency_signs: []` — no first-visible-sign claim.** Unlike copper (premature grey), taurine has no "first sign" datum. That archetype is OFF the table. Cataracts/retinal damage are deficiency *conditions*, not an early tell — do not dress them as a "first sign."
- **Taurine is rarely the sole subject.** Of six claims, three (LETS-000120/267/283) are multi-nutrient protocols where taurine is one line item, and two (IMMORT-000058/DDDL-000066) share billing with arginine + tyrosine. Only WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054 makes taurine the headline — and even it spends its verbatim on four roles at once.
- **SOLO-vs-GROUP verdict: MARGINAL — a solo header is defensible ONLY on the eye concept (A).** The eye triad is a real, distinct, fully verbatim-grounded story that no other essential owns, and it is enough to carry one bespoke page. Everything else is thin (skeletal muscle + blood pressure are one sentence; the protocol appearances are shared line-items; the framework/list story wants to be a group). **If Luneth would rather not spend a bespoke header here, the honest fallback is a GROUP page — "the three amino acids Wallach added" (arginine · taurine · tyrosine, from IMMORT-000058 / DDDL-000066)** — where taurine sits beside its two natural partners and Concept B becomes the group's spine. Recommend: solo IF the eye concept lands with Luneth; otherwise group with arginine + tyrosine. Do NOT stretch this to three or four concepts — the material honestly supports two.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**Concept A ("Three ways to lose your sight" — the eye triad).** It is the only concept that turns taurine's scattered, thin material into a single memorable picture, every element is verbatim-grounded (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000054 + WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000058/DDDL-000066), and the one-eye/three-failures figure is exactly the "one idea, fewest elements" illustration the rules ask for. If the eye concept doesn't win Luneth over, the honest move is to fold taurine into a group with arginine and tyrosine rather than pad a weak solo page.
