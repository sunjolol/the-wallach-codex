# Vitamin B5 (Pantothenic Acid) — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 10 sealed claims · vitamin (water-soluble B) · target: 100 mg/day (Wallach, upper of the Epigenetics 25–100 mg range).

All facts below are grounded in the claim pack only. Every factual element carries its claim ID. Nothing about vitamin B5 that is not in the pack is used — no etymology, no food sources, no discovery history (see Gaps).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

### A. Core identity — B5 is the raw material for coenzyme A
This is the element's true "what it IS," and it is the strongest single thing in the pack. Wallach states B5 (pantothenic acid) is **"a component of coenzyme A and of the acyl carrier protein of fatty-acid synthetase,"** essential for **releasing energy from carbohydrates, building and breaking down fatty acids, and synthesizing sterols, steroid hormones, and acetylcholine** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038). So a single vitamin sits at the hub of: energy from food, fat assembly/teardown, hormone-building, and making the nerve signal acetylcholine. Nothing else in the pack matches this claim for reach — it is the mechanism anchor for any "identity" concept.

### B. The signature deficiency sign — "burning feet" appears in ALL THREE books
The one deficiency sign Wallach lists in every source is **burning feet**:
- Epigenetics (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038) — "Burning feet."
- Let's Play Doctor (WAL-CLM-LETS-000021) — "burning feet."
- Rare Earths (WAL-CLM-RARE-000236) — "burning of feet."
Three separate books, three deficiency lists, one sign in common. This is a cross-SOURCE consilience — distinct from copper's single-book "gray hair = first sign," because here the wow is that the books *agree*.

### C. The mind/mood signs — a shortage shows up as a quarrelsome, sullen mood
Wallach repeatedly folds a personality/mood change into B5 deficiency, not just physical signs:
- "a quarrelsome and sullen attitude, depression, insomnia" (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038).
- "quarrelsome, sullen, depressed" (WAL-CLM-RARE-000236).
- "depression … nervousness" (WAL-CLM-LETS-000021).
The pack even carries `disagreeable_attitude` and `depression` as conditions treated. So the corpus supports a genuine "body-and-mind" split: burning feet / racing heart / muscle cramps on one side, a sullen, quarrelsome, nervous, depressed mood on the other.

### D. The cardiovascular + appetite signs
Beyond feet and mood: **rapid heartbeat (tachycardia)** and **fainting / light-headedness** recur across all three deficiency lists (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038, WAL-CLM-LETS-000021, WAL-CLM-RARE-000236). Appetite loss (anorexia / "loss of appetite") and GI distress (gas, diarrhea, indigestion) also recur (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038, WAL-CLM-RARE-000236). Full LPD list adds ataxia ("coordination impairment"), eczema, alopecia, hypotension, nausea & vomiting, weakness, fatigue, lowered immunity (WAL-CLM-LETS-000021).

### E. The ubiquitous teammate — B5 recurs across protocols at a steady dose
B5 is almost never the star of a protocol; it is a consistent B-complex teammate that appears again and again at a near-constant dose:
- Anemia — **B-5 50 mg three times daily** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000137).
- Anxiety / panic attacks — **B-5 50 mg three times daily** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000142).
- Bipolar disorder — **B-5 100 mg twice daily** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000159, confidence: medium).
- Cataracts — **B-5 50 mg twice daily** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000207, confidence: medium).
That recurring ~50 mg dose is itself a story: B5 is background infrastructure, present in condition after condition.

### F. Curio — geographic tongue as a malabsorption "red flag"
A distinct, surprising fact: Wallach reads **geographic tongue** (benign migratory glossitis) as a "red flag that you are not absorbing" B-3, B-2, B-6, B-5, B-12, folic acid, or zinc — usually from celiac-like small-intestine malabsorption (WAL-CLM-LETS-000284). Interesting but thin (B5 is one of seven named), so it is curio-tier, not a lead.

### Dose / target material
- Maintenance target source: **Epigenetics daily multiple, "25 – 100 mg"** (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000115). Newest source (2014).
- Older Base-Line figure: **True Supplement Need 50 mg**, plus a 30-day pharmacologic dose of **300–1,000 mg/day** (WAL-CLM-LETS-000060, 1995). The 300–1,000 mg is a short-term therapeutic dose, NOT the daily target — do not surface it as the number.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### Concept A — "Becomes coenzyme A" (the identity / assembly)
- **Name:** Becomes Coenzyme A.
- **The hook:** You spend this vitamin to build one hub molecule — coenzyme A — and that molecule then runs your energy, your fats, your steroid hormones, and a nerve signal.
- **Layout shape:** A vertical "assembly / becomes" flow, top-to-bottom: [Vitamin B5] → [Coenzyme A + acyl-carrier protein] → a short row of the four things it powers. Reads as a transformation, not a fork of equal branches — the single input at top is the point.
- **Illustration:** One central molecule node labelled "coenzyme A" with a small B5 "feeding into" it above, and exactly four short labelled outputs below: *energy from food · build & break down fats · steroid hormones · acetylcholine*. Few elements, one idea (B5 → CoA → four jobs). Keep labels off any connecting stroke.
- **Anchored by:** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038 — "a component of coenzyme A and of the acyl carrier protein of fatty-acid synthetase"; "releasing energy from carbohydrates, building and breaking down fatty acids, and synthesizing sterols, steroid hormones, and acetylcholine."
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Gives B5 a durable IDENTITY the way magnesium got chlorophyll and calcium got abundance — a reader leaves knowing *what it is for*, not just a symptom list. Must be drawn as a "becomes/assembly," NOT copper's cofactor-fork, or the two figures collide.

### Concept B — "Three books, one sign" (cross-source consilience)
- **Name:** The Sign All Three Books Share.
- **The hook:** Wallach lists B5 deficiency in three different books, and one sign is in every list — burning feet.
- **Layout shape:** Three side-by-side mini source panels (Epigenetics · Let's Play Doctor · Rare Earths), each a short deficiency list, with the single shared line — burning feet — visually pulled out / connected beneath as the common thread. An "agreement" grid converging on one row.
- **Illustration:** The three panels themselves are the figure; a single highlighted "burning feet" band ties them together at the base. No drawn foot needed (avoids clutter); if a glyph is wanted, one small heat-marked foot at the convergence point, label off the glow.
- **Anchored by:** WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038 ("Burning feet"), WAL-CLM-LETS-000021 ("burning feet"), WAL-CLM-RARE-000236 ("burning of feet"). Secondary shared signs available if a second highlighted row is wanted: tachycardia and fainting also appear in all three.
- **Why it wows / best UX:** A "first-visible sign" beat, but freshened past copper's version — the wow is that independent sources *agree*, which reads as credibility. Distinct layout (three-column agreement) unlike anything shipped.

### Concept C — "The quiet teammate" (ubiquity roster)
- **Name:** Always on the Bench, Always Playing.
- **The hook:** B5 is rarely the star — but it shows up in protocol after protocol at a steady ~50 mg, quiet background infrastructure.
- **Layout shape:** A compact condition roster: a short stacked list of conditions with B5's dose beside each — a "same face, everywhere" table read.
- **Illustration:** Minimal — the roster IS the figure: four rows (anemia · anxiety · bipolar · cataracts) each with the B5 dose, the doses aligned in one column so the near-constant 50 mg reads at a glance. One emphasis mark on the repeating dose.
- **Anchored by:** WAL-CLM-LETS-000137 (anemia, 50 mg t.i.d.), WAL-CLM-LETS-000142 (anxiety, 50 mg t.i.d.), WAL-CLM-LETS-000159 (bipolar, 100 mg b.i.d. — medium confidence), WAL-CLM-LETS-000207 (cataracts, 50 mg b.i.d. — medium confidence).
- **Why it wows / best UX:** Honest to the corpus (B5 as supporting player, not miracle cure) while still telling a real story — the repetition is the insight. Different information shape from A and B (a roster, not a mechanism or a sign).

### Concept D — "Body and mind" (two-lane deficiency split)
- **Name:** It Hits the Body and the Mood.
- **The hook:** Run short on B5 and it is not only physical — Wallach lists a quarrelsome, sullen, nervous, depressed mood right alongside burning feet and a racing heart.
- **Layout shape:** A two-lane split — a BODY column (physical signs) beside a MIND column (mood/behavior signs) — same deficiency, two faces.
- **Illustration:** Two labelled columns of short sign-chips; no drawn figure, or at most one spare divider between the lanes. Body lane: burning feet · muscle cramps · tachycardia · fainting · appetite loss. Mind lane: quarrelsome · sullen · depressed · nervous · insomnia.
- **Anchored by:** Body — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038, WAL-CLM-LETS-000021, WAL-CLM-RARE-000236. Mind — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000038 ("quarrelsome and sullen attitude, depression, insomnia"), WAL-CLM-RARE-000236 ("quarrelsome, sullen, depressed"), WAL-CLM-LETS-000021 ("depression … nervousness").
- **Why it wows / best UX:** The mood angle is genuinely surprising for a vitamin and well-supported across two/three books. The two-lane comparison is a distinct layout archetype not used by A/B/C.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **No etymology, no discovery/history, no food-source claim in the pack.** The real-world "pantothenic = from everywhere (Greek)" fact is NOT in any claim — do not use it. No "found in X food" material (and the diet-not-food thesis would bar it anyway). A history/etymology curio concept is therefore unsupported; flagged rather than invented.
- **B5 is a supporting player, not a star.** Every protocol claim (WAL-CLM-LETS-000137 / -000142 / -000159 / -000207) is multi-nutrient with B5 as one teammate at ~50 mg. Concepts must not imply B5 alone treats anemia/anxiety/bipolar/cataracts. Concept C is designed to be honest about exactly this.
- **Two protocol claims are medium confidence:** WAL-CLM-LETS-000159 (bipolar) and WAL-CLM-LETS-000207 (cataracts). Fine to cite; note the confidence if a concept leans on them.
- **Number-vs-verbatim check:** clean. EPIGEN-000115 claim_text "25 to 100 mg" matches its verbatim. LETS-000060 claim_text (50 mg TSN; 300–1,000 mg pharmacologic) matches its verbatim. No superseded_by on any claim.
- **No color/visual-signature fact** (nothing like copper's hair color or magnesium's green) — B5's distinctiveness is functional (coenzyme A) and sign-based (burning feet), not visual. Illustrations should lean on the mechanism/sign story, not a "look."
- **Solo header verdict: YES, keep it solo.** 10 sealed claims, a genuine standalone identity (coenzyme A hub), a cross-source signature sign, and a clean target. It comfortably supports its own bespoke header and should not fold into another element.
