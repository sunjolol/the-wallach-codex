# Choline — header research dossier
> status: RESEARCH (concepts only — NOT designed). 14 sealed claims · vitamin (water-soluble B-vitamin) · target: 100 mg/day (upper of Wallach's 10–100 mg Epigenetics daily-multiple range).

## 1. The material (grounded, by angle)

**Core identity — the lecithin + acetylcholine molecule.**
Wallach frames choline as a structural component of phosphatidylcholine (lecithin), which he calls essential to all cell membranes, plasma lipoproteins, and lung surfactant, and — in the central nervous system — of sphingomyelin and the neurotransmitter acetylcholine (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044). So it wears two hats: a **structural brick** (membranes, surfactant) and the **raw material for a nerve signal** (acetylcholine). GROUNDING CAVEAT: this dual-role description lives in the claim_text (summary) of EPIGEN-000044; that claim's *verbatim* is only the deficiency list, so a design-time pull-quote off this ID will NOT return the membrane/acetylcholine line (see §5).

**The dose gulf — milligrams to keep, grams to treat.**
This is choline's most distinctive numeric signature across the corpus. Maintenance is small: 10–100 mg/day (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000124). But when Wallach treats brain and muscle disease he reaches for grams:
- headache — 150 mg/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000293)
- bipolar — 250 mg twice daily (WAL-CLM-LETS-000159)
- chorea — 500 mg/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000220)
- dementia — 500–1,000 mg/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000243)
- absence attacks (petit mal) — 4 grams/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000120)
- epilepsy — 4 grams/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267)
- muscular dystrophy / Keshan — choline as soy lecithin 10–20 grams/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000373)
- vitamin-table therapeutic note — "treatment requires 20 G per day" (WAL-CLM-RARE-000242)
That is a span from tens of milligrams to ~20 grams — roughly a 200×–2,000× jump. No shipped header uses a dose ramp; this element genuinely earns one.

**The brain/movement through-line.**
Nearly every condition Wallach ties to choline is neurological or a movement disorder — consistent with the acetylcholine role: Alzheimer's (WAL-CLM-LETS-000130), dementia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000243), memory loss / Korsakoff's (WAL-CLM-LETS-000356), Huntington's & tardive dyskinesia (as deficiency signs, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044 / WAL-CLM-RARE-000242), absence attacks (WAL-CLM-LETS-000120), epilepsy (WAL-CLM-LETS-000267), chorea (WAL-CLM-LETS-000220), bipolar (WAL-CLM-LETS-000159). In the memory-loss protocol he even instructs taking choline *with* piracetam (WAL-CLM-LETS-000356), and in Alzheimer's uses "piracetam/choline" together (WAL-CLM-LETS-000130).

**Deficiency signs — two organs fail: liver and brain.**
Wallach's vitamin table lists choline-deficiency consequences that cluster cleanly into a LIVER set (fatty liver, liver cirrhosis) and a BRAIN/MOVEMENT set (Alzheimer's, dementia, tardive dyskinesia, Huntington's), plus **kidney hemorrhage** — the one mapped deficiency *symptom* (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044, high; WAL-CLM-RARE-000242, high). Fatty liver + kidney hemorrhage + the brain cluster is the whole deficiency picture.

**Curio — the arsenic odd couple.**
Wallach's poultry model: arsenic combined with the B-vitamin choline prevents 100% of perosis ("slipped tendon") in chickens, and the same arsenic deficiency in humans shows up as carpal tunnel syndrome and TMJ / repetitive-motion joint disease (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000226, confidence medium). A genuine did-you-know: choline pairs with a *poison* metal to hold tendons in place. (Note: this is primarily arsenic's story with choline as the named partner.)

**Delivery form — lecithin is how he doses it.**
Across the neuro protocols the working form is lecithin / phosphatidylcholine: "Lecithin at 2,500 mg t.i.d. ... phosphatydil choline is more efficient" (WAL-CLM-LETS-000130), lecithin 2,500 mg in dementia (WAL-CLM-LETS-000243) and chorea (WAL-CLM-LETS-000220), and "choline as soy lecithin 10–20 grams" for MD (WAL-CLM-LETS-000373). Unlike the identity mechanism, the lecithin=phosphatidylcholine link HAS verbatim support here.

## 2. Four header concepts (genuinely distinct)

### A — "Two jobs, one molecule" (dual-role identity)
- **The hook** — Choline is both a building brick and a messenger's raw material.
- **Layout shape** — A two-lane split from a single origin: one choline unit at the left, two divergent paths to the right (a STRUCTURE lane and a SIGNAL lane), each ending in a short labeled payoff. No beats/quote chassis.
- **Illustration** — One choline node that forks: upper path lands in a membrane bilayer sketch (labeled "lecithin — every cell membrane, lung surfactant"); lower path lands in a single nerve-gap / signal glyph (labeled "acetylcholine — the memory signal"). Two endpoints, one fork — few elements.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044 (phosphatidylcholine/lecithin in membranes, lipoproteins, lung surfactant; acetylcholine + sphingomyelin in the CNS). Delivery-form support: WAL-CLM-LETS-000130 (lecithin / "phosphatydil choline").
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Cleanest identity statement; a reader instantly "gets" what choline IS and why it touches both liver-lipid and brain problems. Caveat: acetylcholine half rests on claim_text, not verbatim (§5) — safe as a labeled illustration, risky as a pull-quote.

### B — "Milligrams to keep, grams to treat" (the dose gulf) — LEAD
- **The hook** — Your daily need is a pinch; when the brain fails Wallach dispenses it by the gram — up to 20 grams a day.
- **Layout shape** — A single vertical (or diagonal) log-scale ladder/ramp, a handful of labeled rungs climbing from maintenance to therapeutic. One figure, no side beats.
- **Illustration** — One ramp with ~5 rungs, each a real Wallach figure: 10–100 mg maintenance · 150 mg headache · 500–1,000 mg dementia · 4 g epilepsy/absence · 20 g therapeutic. Route all labels to one side of the ramp so no stroke crosses text.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000124 (10–100 mg) · WAL-CLM-LETS-000293 (150 mg) · WAL-CLM-LETS-000243 (500–1,000 mg) · WAL-CLM-LETS-000120 / WAL-CLM-LETS-000267 (4 g) · WAL-CLM-LETS-000373 (soy lecithin 10–20 g) · WAL-CLM-RARE-000242 ("20 G per day").
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Unique among the six shipped headers (no dose-ramp exists), and the fact is memorable, true, and singular to choline. Directly dramatizes why the "why this number" tip only tells half the story.

### C — "Where a shortage shows up" (liver + brain deficiency map)
- **The hook** — Run low and two organs fail — the liver and the brain.
- **Layout shape** — A compact body/organ figure with exactly two labeled hot zones (liver, brain) and a small third marker for kidney hemorrhage; a short caption stack, not a many-station diagram.
- **Illustration** — Simple torso-and-head silhouette; LIVER zone tagged "fatty liver · cirrhosis," BRAIN zone tagged "Alzheimer's · dementia · tardive dyskinesia · Huntington's," a small kidney dot tagged "hemorrhage." Three markers total.
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044 and WAL-CLM-RARE-000242 (both list fatty liver / (cirrhosis) / kidney hemorrhage / Alzheimer's / tardive dyskinesia / Huntington's; dementia mapped). Kidney hemorrhage is the mapped deficiency symptom (both high confidence).
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Answers "why should I care" fast and viscerally; the liver+brain pairing is surprising and comes straight from the two deficiency-sign claims. Keep the label count low to avoid the rejected many-station look.

### D — "The arsenic odd couple" (curio card)
- **The hook** — Choline teams up with a poison — arsenic — to keep tendons from slipping.
- **Layout shape** — A single curio panel: a "choline + arsenic" pairing on the left resolving to an outcome on the right, in the spirit of vitamin A's ancient-Egypt curio (a self-contained aside, not the page's spine).
- **Illustration** — A minimal "1 + 1 → result" card: choline glyph + arsenic glyph, arrow, "100% of slipped tendon prevented (poultry)"; a small human footnote "same gap in people → carpal tunnel · TMJ."
- **Anchored by** — WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000226 ("Arsenic in combination with the B-vitamin choline prevents 100% of perosis ('slipped tendon') in poultry"; human arsenic deficiency → carpal tunnel / TMJ / repetitive-motion joint disease). Confidence: medium.
- **Why it wows / best UX** — Genuinely odd and sticky, and visually unlike A/B/C. Weakness: it is mostly *arsenic's* story with choline as partner, and single-claim (medium confidence) — best as a secondary curio block, not the header's core.

## 3. Proposed lede (PROPOSAL — Luneth ratifies, do not treat as final)

1. "The B-vitamin behind lecithin and the memory signal — a structural brick in every cell membrane and lung surfactant, and the raw material your nerves build acetylcholine from, which is why Wallach ties a shortage to both a fatty liver and a failing memory." (identity + liver/brain deficiency; grounded in WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044.)
2. "A B-vitamin you need only in milligrams to stay well — yet the one Wallach dispenses by the gram when the brain is failing, because it builds both the lecithin in your cell membranes and acetylcholine, the nerve's memory signal." (leads on the dose gulf; grounded in WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000124 for the mg maintenance, WAL-CLM-RARE-000242 for gram-scale therapy, WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044 for the roles.)

Note: both draw the membrane/acetylcholine roles from EPIGEN-000044's claim_text (see §5 caveat). Neither restates concept A's or B's opening beat verbatim.

## 4. Proposed "why this number" (PROPOSAL)

The 100 mg/day target is the upper bound of Wallach's own choline range in the Epigenetics daily multiple vitamin-and-mineral program — 10–100 mg/day (WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000124, 2014). The derive takes the upper of the stated range (100 mg); choline is dosed in milligrams, so there is no IU conversion, and no body-weight scaling was applied — the posted number is simply the range's ceiling. Worth flagging in the tip: this is only the *maintenance* figure. An older book lists a higher "True Supplement Need" of 600 mg/day (WAL-CLM-LETS-000048, Let's Play Doctor, 1995), and Wallach's *therapeutic* doses run far higher still — 4 grams a day for seizures, up to 20 grams a day for the brain and muscle-disease protocols (WAL-CLM-RARE-000242, WAL-CLM-LETS-000373). The 100 mg on the card keeps you topped up; it is not the dose he treats disease with.

## 5. Gaps / flags

- **Identity mechanism is claim_text-only.** The membrane / lung-surfactant / acetylcholine / sphingomyelin description (the heart of Concept A and both ledes) lives in the *summary* of WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000044; that claim's verbatim is only the deficiency list. Design may present it as sealed-claim material, but a pull-QUOTE by that ID returns the deficiency list, not the roles. If a header wants Wallach's words on "choline builds acetylcholine," the corpus does not contain that verbatim — do not manufacture one.
- **Competing maintenance numbers, no supersede link.** EPIGEN-000124 (10–100 mg, 2014) and LETS-000048 (600 mg True Supplement Need, 1995) disagree, and neither carries `superseded_by`. The derive correctly favors the newer Epigenetics figure (per favor-newest-Wallach-number), but the header must not silently blend them — keep 100 mg for the card and treat 600 mg as historical if surfaced at all.
- **claim_text ↔ verbatim consistency: clean on the big numbers.** RARE-000242 claim_text "20 grams a day" matches its verbatim "20 G per day." No inflated-number mismatch found in this pack.
- **Chorea selenium unit note (not choline, but present in the source claim).** WAL-CLM-LETS-000220's claim_text flags the book's "500 mg" selenium misprint as micrograms — irrelevant to choline (choline there is 500 mg/day) but do not accidentally carry the selenium figure into a choline visual.
- **Curio is thin/tangential.** The arsenic partnership (Concept D) is a single medium-confidence claim (WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000226) and is really arsenic's story. Strong as a curio aside, weak as a header spine.
- **Solo header: YES.** With 14 sealed claims, a distinctive dose gulf, a clean dual identity, a coherent liver+brain deficiency picture, and a genuine curio, choline easily merits its own bespoke header and should NOT fold into another element. Inositol shares its Epigenetics dose row (10–100 mg, same EPIGEN-000124 verbatim) but is not in this pack's scope; no forced grouping needed.
- **Wished-for but absent:** no verbatim quote stating the acetylcholine/memory mechanism in Wallach's own words (only the summary), which would have made Concept A's pull-quote effortless.

## 6. Recommended lead concept

**B — "Milligrams to keep, grams to treat."** It is the one concept unique to choline and unlike any of the six shipped headers, it is fully grounded in verbatim dose figures (not claim_text), and the milligrams-to-20-grams span is the single most memorable, true fact the corpus gives this element. Pair it with lede #2 and use Concept A's dual-role fork as the natural fallback if a pure identity header is preferred.
