COVERAGE · PLATE LANGUAGE PROPOSAL

Same layout, sizing, fonts, placement. Only colour + depth change. Covered = a plate LIFTED off the machined substrate with a green rim and the white top-light; the orange tick rides the winner as a signal accent rather than being the state itself. Dormant = sunk into the substrate, quiet and unlit — never a wall of red. Rare-trace trimmed to 12 tiles per field for readability.
