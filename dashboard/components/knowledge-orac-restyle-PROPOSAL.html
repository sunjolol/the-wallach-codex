01

The score that measureshow fast you rust.

Every day, unstable molecules called free radicals corrode your cells from the inside — the same reaction that rusts iron and browns a cut apple. ORAC is the one number that says how hard a food fights back.

Look at the back of your hand

See a dark spot? You already have millions more you can’t see.

Age spots and liver spots aren’t cosmetic. They’re ceroid lipofuscin — a brown residue left behind wherever free radicals have burned through a cell. The ones on your skin are the readable surface of the same buildup happening in your brain, heart, liver and eyes. Wallach cites how much of a brain cell it fills as you age:

02
How the damage actually happens

Rust, one cell at a time

Wallach quotes the researcher Roy Walford, who called free radicals “the great white sharks in the biochemical sea.” Here is the attack, in plain terms — five steps from a single broken molecule to the spot on your hand:

01
A molecule breaks
A free radical is a molecule missing a piece. It comes from fried food, smoke, sugar — even your body burning fuel.
02
It steals from your cells
To repair itself it rips a piece off your cell wall. Now that part is broken too — a chain reaction starts.
03
The damage sets
The broken fats turn into a glue that fuses your proteins and DNA together where they should move freely.
04
Your cells stiffen
Fused cells can’t let food in or push waste out. At the cellular level, this hardening IS aging.
05
You can see it
The leftover residue is a brown pigment. On your skin it’s an age spot. Everywhere else, it just keeps building.
03
The one number to hit

How much protection you actually need

ORAC turns “eat more antioxidants” into a target you can count. Here is Wallach’s:

Wallach’s daily target
20,000–25,000ORAC points / day

The intake he gives as optimal for reaching 100. A maintenance figure — never given alone, always paired with the rest of his program.

Fighting disease
100,000+
For active neurological disease — MS, ALS, Parkinson’s — he goes past 100,000 points a day, four times the maintenance dose.
