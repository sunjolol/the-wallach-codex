## How to use this reference

**This is a calibration anchor, not a template to copy.** When making styling
decisions for this project (and any future projects that adopt design-wisdom),
this image marks the target *vibe* for:

- **Color injection** — bright orange as a signal accent, not a wash
- **Brutalist + organic** — hard geometry meeting flowing forms
- **Tech-but-warm** — futuristic without feeling cold or sterile
- **Chaotic order** — many layers, but each one has a precise role
- **Surreal eloquence** — a sense that something advanced and beautiful is happening, not a clinical readout

**What to extract from the image:**

1. The bright, near-pure orange used as a signal accent against grays/whites
2. The brutalist architectural composition (hard geometric breaks)
3. The way fine technical detail (tiny labels, line registration marks, schematic overlays) coexists with broad, almost painterly forms
4. The sense of looking at *something* — a structure, an artifact, an event — rather than a layout
5. The branded type lock-up in the corner (small, refined, no-nonsense)

**What NOT to extract:**

1. The black/dark gray-scale dominance (we want warm cream paper as the substrate)
2. The literal cityscape / structure imagery (we don't render scenes)
3. The crowded composition (we want breathing space and editorial pause)
4. The 2004-era trance-album-cover energy (we want timeless editorial)

The synthesis goal: **take the orange signal + tech precision + surreal-eloquent composition language from this image, and graft it onto the cream-paper editorial spread (v2) without losing the warm "stay a while" feel.**
