### WAL-CLM-LETS-000351 · protocol

- **claim:** For Lyme disease (Lyme arthritis) — a spirochete bacterial infection transmitted by ticks, first described in 1975 in Lyme, Connecticut — Wallach notes that 3 to 32 days after an infected tick bite a skin lesion called erythema chronicum migrans appears on the thigh, buttock or axilla and expands up to 50 cm, with recurring attacks of arthritis, fatigue, chills, fever, stiff neck, sore muscles, nausea and vomiting, and heart involvement (an enlarged heart and atrioventricular [AV] block) in 8% of patients.
