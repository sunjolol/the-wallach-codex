### WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000330 · definition
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Choline is an essential nutrient with a vitamin-like coenzyme function that Wallach groups among the water-soluble vitamins. It has been recognized as essential for more than a hundred years. Choline serves as a structural building block of all cell membranes, plasma lipoproteins and lung surfactant, and in the nervous system it forms sphingomyelin and the neurotransmitter acetylcholine. It also carries fat out of the liver, so a shortage leads to a fatty liver. The body can make some choline from the amino acids methionine or serine with vitamin B12 and folate, but not enough to meet daily needs, which is why it must be supplied through diet or supplements.
- **verbatim** (offset 127981): “Choline has been known for more than 100 years as
being essential and it has a vitamin-like coenzyme function.”
