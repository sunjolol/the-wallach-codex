- **claim:** Surprisingly, Wallach classifies aluminum as an essential nutrient - specifically the organically-bound colloidal aluminum found in all soil-grown plants, which he considers nontoxic. Its biological job: activating the enzyme succinic dehydrogenase. In animal studies, an aluminum-deficient diet produced congenital limb defects (non-genetic birth defects) in the young and liver cirrhosis (scarring) in adults, while supplementing it raised newborn survival - leading chemistry professor Gerhard Schrauzer to call it 'probably an essential mineral for human nutrition.'
