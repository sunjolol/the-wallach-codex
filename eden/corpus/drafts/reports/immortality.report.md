- **claim:** Wallach dismisses both halves of the genetics excuse. Good genes will not carry you to 100 if you ignore the physiological requirements - and bad family history will not stop you if you meet them. ### WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000255 · protocol
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** The second longevity piece is a calorie-restricted diet WITHOUT malnutrition - a distinction Wallach says most practitioners of calorie restriction miss entirely. It is universally known to extend the life span of laboratory, pet and farm animals, and it is a common thread among centenarians both historically and in every Blue Zone today. The levels he cites are 1,250 to 1,800 calories a day, with those doing heavier physical labour at the upper end. The 'without malnutrition' half means every known essential nutrient at optimal levels, well above the RDI, in fewer calories - which is why the mineral piece and the calorie piece are inseparable.
- **verbatim** (offset 713200): “In most cases the caloric levels fall between 1,250 and
1,800 calories per day, with those performing more physical
labor consuming the higher calorie levels.”
