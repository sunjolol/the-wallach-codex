- **claim:** Wallach argues humans are biologically designed to eat high-calcium diets. In the late Paleolithic (35,000–10,000 years ago), wild plant foods and game supplied about 1,600 mg of calcium a day at rest and 2,000–3,000 mg at the activity levels of hunting and labor. The agricultural revolution ~10,000 years ago narrowed the variety of foods and permanently lowered mineral intake — by the 20th century American adults took in only one-fifth to one-third as much, a median of 300–508 mg/day for women and 680 mg/day for men (National Health & Nutrition Examination Survey II).
