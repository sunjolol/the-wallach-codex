- **essentials:** iodine, selenium, phosphorus, copper · **conditions:** goiter, cardiomyopathy, keshan_disease, pica, enzootic_ataxia · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach documents that specific mineral deficiencies produced specific deficiency diseases in distinct geographic regions — each disease appeared wherever the local soil lacked that mineral. His examples: iodine deficiency caused goiter (a swollen thyroid) in communities far from the sea; selenium deficiency caused Keshan disease, a cardiomyopathy (disease of the heart muscle), in Keshan Province, China; phosphate deficiency caused pica (a compulsive craving to eat non-food substances) in South Africa; and copper deficiency caused enzootic ataxia (a copper-deficiency disorder of movement and coordination) in Australia. In his words: "Iodine deficiency (goiter) occurred in communities living at a distance from the sea; Keshan disease (cardiomyopathy) caused by a selenium deficiency occurred in Keshan Province, China; phosphate deficiency (pica) was common in South Africa; copper deficiency (enzootic ataxia) was common in Australia, etc."
