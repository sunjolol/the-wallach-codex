- **essentials:** boron · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach points to boron's bone-protective and hormonal effects. He says it is needed both to maintain bone density and to keep normal blood levels of estrogen and testosterone. He cites a supplementation finding: within eight days of taking boron, women held on to far more of their minerals - excreting 40% less calcium, 33% less magnesium, and less phosphorus in their urine - while their level of 17-beta estradiol (the main, most active form of estrogen) doubled to what is seen in women on estrogen-replacement therapy, and testosterone doubled in both men and women. In his words: "Boron is required for the maintenance of bone density and normal blood levels of estrogen and testosterone. Within eight days of supplementing boron, women lost 40% less calcium, 33% less magnesium, and less phosphorus through their urine. Women receiving boron supplements had blood levels of estradiol 17B doubled to 'levels found in women on estrogen replacement therapy.' Levels of testosterone also double in both men and women following boron supplementation."
