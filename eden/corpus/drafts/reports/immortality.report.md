### WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000237 · mechanism

### WAL-CLM-IMMORT-000243 · mechanism
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Free radicals are molecules carrying an extra unpaired electron, and Wallach quotes Dr. Roy Walford calling them the great white sharks in the biochemical sea. The damage runs in steps: they attack polyunsaturated fats and cholesterol in cell membranes, producing lipid peroxides, which decompose into aldehydes that cross-link proteins, lipids and DNA. The result is membranes that stiffen, so nutrients and oxygen cannot get in and wastes cannot get out. They reach us through skin, breath, food and drink, and are also produced internally during normal metabolism.
- **verbatim** (offset 641589): “quench or neutralize free radicals that attack our cells, cell walls, mitochondria and
physiological processes and increase our risk of cardiovascular disease, cancer,
Alzheimer's disease and other dementias, cataracts, diabetes and aggressively speed up
the process and damage of aging itself.”
