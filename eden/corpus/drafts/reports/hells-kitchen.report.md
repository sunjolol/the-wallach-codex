### WAL-CLM-HELLS-000006 · mechanism
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** pellagra · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Pellagra turned out to be a dietary deficiency disease — a lack of vitamin B3 (niacin) — and eggs were part of the fix. Wallach recounts how a U.S. Public Health Service researcher noticed that in orphanages and mental asylums the residents came down with pellagra while the staff, who ate a wider variety of food, stayed well. When eggs and milk were simply added to the sufferers' diets, the pellagra disappeared. That result pointed to a missing dietary factor rather than the infection that most people of the era assumed, and it is Wallach's illustration that nutrient-dense foods like eggs can reverse a 'disease' that was really malnutrition. In his words: "When eggs and milk were added to the diet of the affected individuals, pellagra disappeared."
- **verbatim** (offset 274387): “When eggs and milk were 
added to the diet of the affected individuals, pellagra disappeared.”
