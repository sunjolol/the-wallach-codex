### WAL-CLM-RARE-000097 · contraindication
- **essentials:** gold · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach warns gold compounds are contraindicated in patients with liver or kidney disease, blood diseases, or SLE (lupus). Toxic reactions to gold therapy include pruritus (itching), dermatitis, stomatitis, GI discomfort, increased urine albumin, blood in the urine, aplastic anemia, reduced white blood cells, hepatitis and pneumonitis.
- **verbatim** (offset 723694): “Gold compounds are not to be used in
patients with liver or kidney disease, blood
diseases or SLE.”
