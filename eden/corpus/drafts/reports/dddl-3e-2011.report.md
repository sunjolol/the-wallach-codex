### WAL-CLM-DDDL-000139 · definition
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach counts zinc among the foundational essential minerals — a nutrient the body cannot make and has to be supplied daily. The adult body holds only about 1.4 to 2.3 grams of it, stored mainly in the liver, pancreas, kidney, bone, and muscle, yet that small amount does outsized work: no fewer than 70 metalloenzymes — including carbonic anhydrase, alkaline phosphatase, and carboxypeptidase — require zinc as a cofactor before they can work at all. Zinc is also built into the RNA molecule (the 'zinc fingers') and is needed for cell division, DNA synthesis, growth, immunity, wound healing, taste and smell, and healthy skin, hair, and nails. Because it touches so many systems, Wallach ties a long list of deficiency signs and birth defects to a shortage of it, and he includes zinc as a daily essential in his Base Line supplement program.
- **verbatim** (offset 602168): “There are no less than 70 metalloenzymes that require Zn as a
functional cofactor.”

signs of Mg deficiency.”

### WAL-CLM-DDDL-000155 · mechanism
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach turns the usual advice on its head: he says kidney stones aren't caused by too much calcium but by calcium and magnesium deficiency. When you're short on these minerals, calcium is depleted from your bones, and that lost bone calcium is the very source of the calcium that forms the stone. belief by the “orthodox” doctor, are caused by calcium and magnesium
deficiencies which cause a depletion of calcium from your bones, which is
the source of the calcium found in kidney stones.”
