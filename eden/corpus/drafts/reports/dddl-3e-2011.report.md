### WAL-CLM-DDDL-000118 · mechanism
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** The single step at the heart of Wallach's whole mineral framework — and the reason he cares about the source. Rock ground into fine dust and suspended in water is real minerals, but in a form that living cells can't take in. Plants can. Their roots pull those rock minerals up from the soil and rebuild them inside their own cells into a different chemical form — the form every living cell of every plant, animal, and human is actually built out of. So a mineral in a rock and the same mineral in a piece of broccoli, he argues, are not the same nutrient. A plant is the factory that does the one step your body can't do for itself, which is why he says the source matters.
- **verbatim** (offset 338080): “Plants, including wild forms and crops such as grains, vegetables, fruits,
and nuts, take up these inorganic metallic colloids and convert them into
intracellular (within the cell) organically bound plant colloids. These
organic plant colloids are the form of minerals found in and used by all
living cells of plants, animals, and humans.”
