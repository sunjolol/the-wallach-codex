He adds that normal individuals carry roughly 400 times more lithium in their hair than violent criminals do, that the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) estimates a daily lithium intake of 650 to 3,100 micrograms (much of it metallic and poorly absorbed), and that lithium supplementation shifts serum vitamin B12 levels.
