### WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000082 · toxicity_sign
- **essentials:** — · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Wallach holds a dual stance on fluorine/fluoride. On one hand he accepts it as an essential trace mineral: prior to 1972 fluoride was thought essential only for warding off dental caries (cavities) via tooth enamel, and in 1972 the researcher Schwarz proved fluoride to be a genuine essential mineral. On the other hand he treats water fluoridation as highly controversial and links it to cancer. Following 1977 epidemiological studies by Yiamouyiannis and Burk, full-scale congressional hearings examined the charge that 10,000 excess cancer deaths were caused by fluoridating certain public water systems. The resulting U.S. National Toxicology Program animal study, reported in 1990, found increased precancerous lesions and squamous-cell carcinoma of the oral mucous membranes, a rare osteosarcoma (bone cancer) at double the rate in males as females, and a significant increase in thyroid follicular-cell tumors and liver cancer. Clinical fluoride toxicity appears as dental fluorosis at 2 to 7 parts-per-million and osteosclerosis at 8 to 20 parts-per-million, with chronic systemic toxicity at 20 to 80 milligrams per day over years; the average American already takes in about 4.4 milligrams per day from food, toothpaste, supplements and water.

### WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000083 · deficiency_sign
- **essentials:** iron · **conditions:** anemia, pica, obesity, tachycardia · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Iron is an essential mineral; a healthy adult carries 3 to 5 grams, most of it in hemoglobin and myoglobin, where iron carries oxygen (iron is to hemoglobin what magnesium is to chlorophyll). Wallach stresses that pica -- obsessive cravings driving children and adults to eat ice (pagophagia), dirt (geophagia), lead-paint chips and high-calorie snack foods -- is a specific sign of iron deficiency, and that the snack-food form can cause weight gain and obesity. Iron deficiency arises from unsupplemented pregnancy, menstruation, chronic infection, hypochlorhydria (low stomach acid from salt-restricted diets or proton-pump inhibitors), chronic diarrhea, chronic bleeding, and impaired absorption from high-fat, high-phytate or gluten diets. Its symptoms include listlessness, fatigue, heart palpitations on exertion, rapid pulse (tachycardia), reduced cognition and memory, sore tongue, difficulty swallowing (dysphagia) and hypochromic microcytic anemia. Stomach hydrochloric acid and vitamin C aid absorption; heme iron from meat is about 10 percent absorbable versus 1 percent from plants (phytates bind it). He notes excess iron storage is usually caused by deficiencies of selenium, copper or zinc and is falsely blamed for liver cirrhosis, pancreatic fibrosis, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and diabetes.

### WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000086 · deficiency_sign
- **essentials:** germanium · **conditions:** cancer, arthritis, osteoarthritis · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Germanium is an essential trace mineral that Wallach describes as a highly efficient intracellular electrical-impulse initiator and a metallic cofactor for oxygen utilization, closely related to silica and carbon. He recounts that Dr. Kazuhiko Asai found germanium in fossilized plant life and in healing herbs (garlic, aloe, comfrey, chlorella, ginseng, watercress, Shiitake mushroom and others), which accumulate it at 100 to 2,000 parts-per-million, and that the 'holy waters' at Lourdes contain large amounts of germanium and lithium. Germanium enhances the immune system by stimulating natural killer cells, interferon, macrophages and T-suppressor cells, and Russian researchers attributed anti-cancer activity to it. Asai synthesized the organic form GE-132 (carboxyethyl germanium sesquioxide) in 1967, which is absorbed at about 30 percent efficiency. Wallach states that germanium deficiency is typified by severely reduced immune status, arthritis, osteoarthritis, low energy and cancer; the maintenance dose is 20 to 30 milligrams per day, rising to 50 to 100 milligrams per day for serious illness requiring increased body oxygen.

### WAL-CLM-EPIGEN-000087 · mechanism
- **essentials:** hydrogen · **conditions:** — · **symptoms:** —
- **claim:** Hydrogen is an essential structural element: it is the major constituent of water (about 70 percent of the human body is water) and of all organic molecules, and Wallach frames the body's acid-base balance as, in fact, the regulation of hydrogen-ion (H+) levels in cellular and extracellular fluids. Healthy blood pH ranges from 7.36 to 7.44; below 7.30 is acidosis and above 7.44 is alkalosis, while values below 6.8 or above 7.8 are rapidly fatal. The body controls hydrogen-ion concentration by dilution, buffering (reacting in fractions of a second), respiration (minutes) and the kidney (up to hours or days). Large amounts of hydrogen ions are generated or retained in disease states such as emphysema, diabetes, anxiety, chloride loss, cystic fibrosis and Addison's disease. He notes a practical corollary: bladder infections (cystitis) can often be controlled by acidifying the urine with unsweetened cranberry juice.
- **verbatim** (offset 1242602): “Hydrogen functions as a major constituent of water (70% of the human body
is water) and all organic molecules. The regulation of the acid-base balance in the
